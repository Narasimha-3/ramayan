
ततः समुद्योगमवेक्ष्य वीर्यवान्‌
हरिप्रवीरस्थ निदेशवर्तिनः।
aya wate वसुधाधिपात्मजः
प्रबुद्धनीलोत्पलतुल्यदर्शन: ॥ ३४॥

Perceiving the consummate (martial)
endeavour on the part of Sugriva, the foremost
warriour of the monkey race, who was at
his beck and call, the powerful Sri Rama
(son of DaSaratha, the suzerain lord of the
earth) looked like a full-blown blue lotus
through delight. (34)
