विसर्जयित्वा स हरीन्‌ सहस्त्रान्‌ Hacer: |
मेने कृतार्थमात्मानं wad a महाबलमू॥ २॥

Having sent away from his presence
those thousands of monkeys, who had
performed their task of bringing together all
the monkeys from the remotest corners of
the world, he deemed himself as well as Sri
Rama of extraordinary might as good as
accomplished of purpose. (2)
