
किष्किन्धाया विनिष्क्राम यदि ते सौम्य रोचते।
तस्य Ag वचन श्रुत्वा लक्ष्मणस्य सुभाषितम्‌॥ ४॥
Gia: परमप्रीतो aaa ह।
एवं भवतु गच्छाम we त्वच्छासने मया॥५॥
“Come out of Kiskindha awhile if it
pleases you, O gentle one!” Supremely
pleased to hear the aforesaid suggestion of
the celebrated Laksmana, well-worded as it
was, Sugriva, they say, replied as follows:
“Amen! Let us go out; | must obey your
order.” (4-5)
