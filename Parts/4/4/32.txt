कपिः कथयते हृष्टो यथायं मारुतात्मज:।
कृत्यवान्‌ सो5पि सम्प्राप्त: कृतकृत्योडसि राघव॥ ३१॥

“Full of joy, this son of the wind-god
speaks aright. Sugriva too has some end to

be achieved (through you). Having arrived
here at the right time, you have your object

accomplished, O scion of Raghu !

प्रसन्‍नमुखवर्णश्र व्यक्त Bes भाषते।
नानृतं वक्ष्यते at हनूमान्‌ मारुतात्मज:॥ ३२॥

“Full of delight, Hanuman speaks
unambiguously and with a cheerful glow on
his face. The gallant son of the wind-god
would not tell a lie.” (32)
