एष ara च वित्तानि प्राप्य चानुत्तमं यशः।
लोकनाथ: पुरा भूत्वा सुग्रीव॑ नाथमिच्छति॥ १८॥
“Having given away all his riches to
beggars while leaving Ayodhya for the forest
and earned unsurpassed glory, and having
been in the past the protector of the world,
Sti Rama desires to have Sugriva as his
benefactor. (18)
