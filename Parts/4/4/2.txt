
भाव्यो राज्यागमस्तस्य सुग्रीवस्य महात्मन: |
यदयं कृत्यवानू Wa: Hel चैतदुपागतम्‌॥ २॥
He said to himself, ‘Attainment of
sovereignty on the part of that high-souled
Sugriva is sure to come about as is clear
from the fact that Sri Rama has come
here with a purpose and the duty of
accomplishing it has devolved on Sugriva.’
