सख्यं Tal कर्तुमियेष ताभ्याम्‌॥ ३९॥
Having carefully heard the aforesaid reply
of Laksmana, and setting his heart on the
consummation of Sugriva’s truimph, Hanuman
(the monkey sprung from the loins of the
wind-god), who wore a most cheerful
countenance, sought then and there to
negotiate an alliance with the two princes. (39)
