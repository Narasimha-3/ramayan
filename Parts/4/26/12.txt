वृत्तज्ञो वृत्तसम्पन्नमुदारबलविक्रमम्‌।
इममप्यड्रद_ वीर॑ यौवराज्येडभिषेचय॥ १२॥

“Conversant as you are with the rules
of conduct, both wordly as well as religious,
install in the office of Prince Regent this

gallant prince, Angada, too, who is rich in

moral character and endowed with
extraordinary strength and valour. (12)
