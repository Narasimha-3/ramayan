“Pray, unite the monkeys with a master
and thereby afford exceptional delight to
them.” Requested thus by Hanuman, the
wise Sri Rama (a scion of Raghu), the
slayer of hostile warriors, who was an
adept in expression, replied as follows to
Hanuman: “Obeying, O gentle Hanuman,
the directions of my late lamented father, |
will not enter for fourteen years even a
village, much less a town. Let the valiant
Sugriva, the foremost of monkeys, be
installed without delay on the throne of
Kiskindha with due ceremony as soon as
he has entered the highly prosperous and
wonderful cave comprising the city of
Kiskindha.” Having replied thus to Hanuman,
