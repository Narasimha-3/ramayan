“By your grace, 0 scion of Kakutstha,
has this great ancestral kingdom which was
most difficult to acquire, of the high-minded
monkeys endowed with sharp teeth and

rich in consummate strength, been acquired
by Sugriva, O lord! Entering in state the
prosperous city of Kiskindha, when duly
permitted by you, he, with his host of near
and dear ones, will perform in the proper
way all his kingly duties. Having gone through
as a part of the installation ceremony the
ceremonial bath with water made fragrant
by perfumes and medicinal herbs in
accordance with the scriptural ordinance,
he will offer special worship to you with
garlands and jewels. You should, therefore,
visit this lovely mountain-cave in which the
city of Kiskindha is comprised. (4—7)
