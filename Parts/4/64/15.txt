
क इदानीं महातेजा agra सागरम्‌।
कः करिष्यति सुग्रीव॑ सत्यसंधमरिदमम्‌॥ १५॥

“What monkey of extraordinary energy
will take a leap across the sea at this
juncture? Who will enable Sugriva, the
subduer of his foes, to prove himself to be
true to his promise of wresting Sita from the
clutches of Ravana? (15)
