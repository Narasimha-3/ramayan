
Wage: We तान्‌ हरीन्‌ हरिसत्तम:।
सर्वे बलवतां श्रेष्ठा भवन्तो दृढविक्रमा:।
व्यपदेशकुले जाता: पूजिताश्चाप्यभीक्ष्णश:॥ २१॥

Once more did Angada, the foremost
of monkeys speak as follows to the aforesaid
monkeys: “You are all pre-eminent among
the mighty and endowed with unbending
prowess. Born in a family free from all
stigma, you have also been honoured time
and again for your valour by the royal court.
