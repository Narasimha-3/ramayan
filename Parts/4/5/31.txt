सीताकपीन्द्रक्षणदाचराणां

राजीवहेमज्वलनोपमानि ।
सुग्रीवरामप्रणयप्रसड़े
वामानि नेत्राणि at स्फुरन्ति॥ ३१॥

On the occasion of the friendship
concluded between Sugriva and Sri Rama
the left eyes of Sita, Vali (the king of monkeys)
and Ravana (an ogre, lit., a night-stalker)—
which were lotus-like, gold-like and fire-like,
respectively, synchronously throbbed.* (31)
