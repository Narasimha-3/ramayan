वालिनो मे महाभाग भयार्तस्याभयं HHUA
“Alarmed as | am, my spouse too having
been taken away, | have sought shelter for
my protection in this mountain, which is
difficult of access for Vali. Having been
banished and antagonized by my brother,
(Vall), O scion of Raghu, | live in the forest
perturbed and alarmed, my mind being
confused. Therefore, grant me security, O
highly blessed one, from Vall, stricken with
fear as | am. (22-23)
