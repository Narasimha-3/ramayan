
दीर्घबाहू विशालाक्षौ शरचापासिधारिणौ।
कस्य न स्याद्‌ भयं दृष्ट्वा होतौ सुरसुतोपमौ ॥ २०॥

“Who will not be actually seized with fear
to see these two warriors possessed of
unusually long arms and large eyes, nay,
carrying a bow, sword and arrow on their
person and looking like the offspring of gods?
(20)
