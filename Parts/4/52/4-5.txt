“The glorious son of DaSsaratha, Sri
Rama, the sovereign of the whole world and
a compeer of the mighty Indra (the ruler of
gods) and Varuna (the god of water), entered
the Dandaka forest alongwith his younger
brother, Laksmana, as well as with his
spouse, Sita (a princess of the Videha
territory). His consort was forcibly carried
away from Janasthana by Ravana (while
she was all alone in the cottage). (4-5)
