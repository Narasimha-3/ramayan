Wa BA महदात्मनस्तदा॥ ३८॥

Encouraged by Jambavan, the foremost
of monkeys and bears, and convinced of
his capacity for vigorous action, the monkey
chief, Hanuman, son of the wind-god, now
expanded his form that very moment, thereby
bringing excessive joy to that army of monkey
heroes. (38)
