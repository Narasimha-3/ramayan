त्रिःसप्तकृत्व: पृथिवी परिक्रान्ता प्रदक्षिणम्‌॥ ३२॥
“During the descent of the Lord as
Trivikrama (who measured the entire universe
in three strides), O dear child, the entire globe
with its mountains, forests and woodlands
was gone round clockwise by me as many
as one and twenty times. (32)
