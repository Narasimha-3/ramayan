
तथा चौषधयोउस्माभि: संचिता देवशासनात्‌।
निर्मथ्यममृतं याभिस्तदानीं नो महद्वलमू॥ ३३॥
“Nay, that time, in obedience to the
command of the gods, the herbs with the
help of which nectar was to be churned out
of the ocean of milk were gathered by
me_ single-handed, since at that time
extraordinary strength existed in me. (33)
