न शोकपरितापेन श्रेयसा युज्यते मृतः।
यदत्रानन्तरं कार्य ततू समाधातुमहथ।॥ २॥

“A departed soul is not rendered fit for
beatitude by grieving for him and giving way
to remorse. You should, therefore, attend
immediately to that which should be done
now for the welfare of the departed soul,
immediately after death. (2)
