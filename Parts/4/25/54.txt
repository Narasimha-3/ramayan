ततो5थ a वालिनमग्रूयपौरुषं
प्रकाशमिक्ष्वाकुवरेषुणा हतम्‌।
प्रदीष्ष दीप्ताग्रिसमौजसं तदा
सलक्ष्मणं राममुपेयिवान्‌ हरि: ॥ ५४॥
Having cremated at that moment
according to the scriptural ordinance the
aforesaid Vali of highest virility, who was

universally noted and had been got killed
with the arrow of Sri Rama (the foremost of
Ikswakus), Sugriva, the monkey-chief, then
forthwith sought the presence of Sri Rama,
who was present there with Laksmana and
whose splendour resembled that of a blazing
fire. (54)
