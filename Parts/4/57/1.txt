शोकाद्‌ भ्रष्टस्वरमपि ral वानरयूथपा:।
श्रदधुर्नेव तद्बवाक्यं कर्मणा aa vise: ॥ १॥
Even after hearing the words of Sampati,
whose voice had turned hoarse due to grief
caused by the news of his brother’s death,
the monkey generals did not put faith in his
words, seized as they were with suspicion

caused by his contemplated action of eating
up the monkeys. (1)
