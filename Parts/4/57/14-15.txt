
स राज्ये स्थापितस्तेन सुग्रीवो वानरेश्वर:।
राजा वानरमुख्यानां तेन प्रस्थापिता बयमू॥ १४॥

एवं रामप्रयुक्तास्तु मार्गमाणास्ततस्तत:।
aed नाधिगच्छामो wit सूर्यप्रभामिव॥ १५॥
“Installed on the throne as the lord of
monkeys by Sri Rama, the aforesaid Sugriva
is now the ruler of all the monkey chiefs.
Dispatched by him and directed by Sri Rama,
and looking about here and there in this
way, we could not find Sita (a princess of
the Videha territory) any more than one
would perceive the splendour of the sun at
night. (14-15)
