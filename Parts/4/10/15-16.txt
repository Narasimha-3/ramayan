
अनुयातश्च मां तूर्णमयं भ्राता सुदारुण:।

सतु दृष्ट्वैव मां रात्रौ सद्वितीयं महाबलः॥ १५॥

Wade भयसंत्रस्तो वीक्ष्यावां समुपागतौ।

अभिद्गुतस्तु वेगेन विवेश स महाबिलमू॥ १६॥
“This most cruel younger brother of

mine too followed me apace. The moment

the demon saw me followed by another at

night, the fellow, even though possessed of
great might, fled for life, distracted as he
was through fear. Nay, put to flight with
greater speed on perceiving us arrived very
near, he entered a big hole with impetuosity.
(15-16)
