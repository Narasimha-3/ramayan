“Observing various lands as also the
western mountain, the foremost of mountains,
and finally reaching the western mountain,
the crown of all mountains, | ran with all

speed towards the north.

प्रविशेद्‌ यदि वै वाली मूर्धास्य शतधा भवेत्‌॥ २२॥

“When, constantly and steadily pursued
by Vali, | did not find shelter even on seeking
the Himalayan range and Mount Meru and,
even so, the Northern Sea, Hanuman, who
was richly endowed with wisdom (and who
was all along with me), thereupon submitted
to me as follows: ‘It is now recollected by
me, O king, how Vall, the lord of monkeys,
was at one time actually cursed by Sage
Matanga that if he should ever venture
into the limits of his hermitage, his head
would surely get split into a hundred pieces.
