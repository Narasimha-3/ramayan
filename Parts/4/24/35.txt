
स्वर्गेषपि शोक॑ च faautat a
मया विना प्राप्स्यति वीर वाली।
Ta नगेन्द्रस्य तटावकाशे
विदेहकन्यारहितो यथा त्वमू्‌॥ ३५॥
“Without me, 0 heroic prince, Vali will

only experience grief and loss of colour
even in heaven, even as bereft of Sita
(a princess of the Videha territory) you
experience grief and cheerlessness on the
delightful slopes of Mount Rsyamuka, the
king of mountains. (35)
