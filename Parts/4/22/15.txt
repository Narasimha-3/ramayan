
Tara च ते कार्य कर्तव्यमविशड्भया।
स्यादधर्मो हाकरणे wat a हिंस्थादमानितः॥ १५॥
“Nay, the purpose of Sri Rama (a scion

of Raghu) should be unquestioningly
accomplished by you; for sin will accrue to
you in the event of your failing to do so
(bound as you are by your plighted word to
help him in recovering his lost spouse) and
he may kill you if he is disregarded. (15)
