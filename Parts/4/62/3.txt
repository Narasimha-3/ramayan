
पुराणे सुमहत्कार्य भविष्यं हि मया श्रुतम्‌।
qe मे तपसा da श्रुत्वा च विदितं मम॥ ३॥

“It has actually been heard by me
(predicted) in the old traditional history that
a very remarkable event is going to take
place in future. It has not only been learnt
by me through hearsay but also foreseen
by me by virtue of my asceticism. (3)
