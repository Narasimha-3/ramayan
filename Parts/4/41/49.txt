
अमितबलपराक्रमा भवन्तो
विपुलगुणेषु कुलेषु at प्रसूता: |
मनुजपतिसुतां यथा लभध्वं
तदधिगुणं पुरुषार्थमारभध्वम्‌॥ ४९॥
“Endowed as you~ 86७ with
immeasurable strength and prowess and
born in families adorned with ample virtues,
initiate your valuable effort in that direction

in such a way that you may find out Sita
(daughter of a ruler of men).” (49)
