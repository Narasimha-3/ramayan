
प्रकीर्णहंसाकुलमेखलानां
प्रबुद्धपद्मोत्पलमालिनीनाम्‌_।
वाप्युत्तमानामधिकाद्य _लक्ष्मी-
वराड़नानामिव भूषितानाम्‌॥ ४९॥
“The loveliness of the best of tanks
with their broken girdle of swans scattered

here and there and their wreaths of full-
blown lotuses, and, therefore, looking like
lovely women adorned with jewels, stands

enhanced today. (49)
