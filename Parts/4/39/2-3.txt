“It will not be strange if Indra, the god
of rain, pours a shower during the rains, if
the yonder sun with its countless rays rids
the sky of its darkness, if the moon, O
gentle one, makes the night clear by its
brilliance or if an individual like you affords

delight to his friends through good offices
rendered to them in return for their past
services, O scourge of your foes! (2-3)
