
कुर्वाणा बहुशब्दांश्व प्रकृष्णा बाहुशालिन:।
शिरोभिर्वानरेन्द्राय. Gitar न्यवेदयन्‌॥ ४१॥

Raising many a cry, the foremost
monkeys, distinguished for their mighty arms,
reported themselves from a distance to
Sugriva, the king of monkeys, with their
bent heads, too shy as they were to force
their way through the invulnerable crowds
to his presence. (41)
