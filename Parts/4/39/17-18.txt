
पद्मयकेसरसंकाशस्तरुणार्कनिभाननः ।

Shear, वानरश्रेष्ठः. सर्ववानरसत्तम:॥ १७॥

अनेकैरब॑हुसास्त्रै्वानराणां समन्वित:।

पिता हनुमतः श्रीमान्‌ केसरी प्रत्यदृश्यत॥ १८॥

Also accompanied by many thousands
of monkeys there appeared the wise and
glorious father of Hanuman, Kesari, a jewel
among the monkeys, nay, the foremost of
all monkeys, who resembled in hue the
filaments of a lotus and whose countenance
shone like the rising sun. (17-18)
