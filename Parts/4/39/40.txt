
आगताश्च निविष्टाश्व पृथिव्यां सर्ववानरा:।
आप्लवन्त: प्लवन्तश् गर्जन्तश्च प्लवंगमा:।
अभ्यवर्तन्त सुग्रीव॑ सूर्यमभ्रगणा इब॥ ४०॥

Nay, all the monkeys which came up
there sat down on the earth. Leaping from
one tree to another and from one bough
to another and growling, the monkeys
surrounded Sugriva as masses of clouds
would encompass the sun. (40)
