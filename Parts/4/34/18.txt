
न स संकुचितः wer येन वाली हतो गतः।
waa तिष्ठ ata मा वालिपथमन्वगा:॥ १८॥

“The well-known path along which
Vali had departed to the abode of Death
when killed by Sri Rama has not been
closed. Therefore, honour the understanding
arrived at by you with Sri Rama, O Sugriva;
pray, do not follow in the wake of Vali.

(18)
