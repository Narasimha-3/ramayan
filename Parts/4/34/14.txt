
ननु नाम कृतार्थेन त्वया रामस्थ वानर।
सीताया anit get: कर्तव्य: कृतमिच्छता॥ १४॥

“Surely an effort ought to be made at
all costs by you, O monkey, since you
have acomplished your object through the
good offices of Sri Rama; you ought to
requite the services of Sri Rama by the
discovery of Sita. (14)
