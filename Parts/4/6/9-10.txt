अनुमानात्‌ तु जानामि मैथिली सा न संशय:।
हियमाणा मया दृष्टा रक्षसा रौद्रकर्मणा॥ ९॥
क्रोशन्ती रामरामेति लक्ष्मणेति a विस्वरम्‌।
स्फुरन्ती रावणस्यथाड्ले पन्‍नगेन्द्रवधूर्यथा॥ १०॥
“By surmise on my part | conclude that
the aforesaid princess of Mithila was seen
by me while being carried off by Ravana (the
ogre) of terrible deeds, crying in a husky
voice “Rama, Rama ! and “Laksmana !” and
wriggling like a female serpent in the arms of
Ravana—there is no doubt about it. (9-10)
