ततः परमगम्या स्याद्‌ feagat त्रिदशावृता।
रहिता चन्द्रसूर्याभ्यामदृश्या तमसावृता॥ ६६॥

“Beyond that mountain the eastern
quarter is inaccessible, inhabited as it is
solely by the deity presiding over it, viz.,
Indra, is without the moon and the sun and
as such remains enveloped in darkness
(screened as it is from the sun by the
eastern mountain standing between them)
and, therefore, invisible. (66)
