
शैलेषु तेषु सर्वेषु कन्दरेषु नदीषु a
ये च नोक्ता मयोद्देशा विचेया तेषु जानकी॥ ६७॥

“Sita, daughter of Janaka, should be
sought for on the peaks of all the above-
mentioned mountains, at the banks of all
the aforesaid rivers and in all the
aforementioned caves as also in all other
regions which have not been mentioned by
me. (67)
