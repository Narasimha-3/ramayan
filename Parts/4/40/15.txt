सुहृदद्वितीयो विक्रान्तः wre: कालविशेषवित्‌।
भवानस्मद्धिते युक्त: सुहद्वाप्तोडर्थवित्तम:॥ १५॥
“You are my second near and dear
one here, Laksmana being the foremost,
are valiant and wise in all matters; you
know the particular time when a thing ought
to be done, are intent on doing good to us,
benevolent, trustworthy and the foremost of
those knowing my purpose.” (15)
