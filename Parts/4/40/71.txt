
वनषण्डमण्डितां
दिशं चरित्वा निपुणेन वानरा:।
अवाप्य सीतां रघुवंशजप्रियां
ततो निवृत्ता: सुखिनो भविष्यथ॥ ७१॥

“Having combed with care the eastern
quarter loved by the mighty Indra and graced
with a number of forests, O monkeys, and
having found Sita, the beloved of Sri Rama
(born in the line of Raghu), and having
returned from that eastern quarter you will
become happy.” (71)
