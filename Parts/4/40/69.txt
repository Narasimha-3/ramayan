एतावद्‌ art: शकयं गन्तुं वानरपुड्रवाः।
अभास्करममर्यादं न जानीमस्ततः WAU ६८ ॥

“Only thus far can the monkeys
proceed, O jewels among the monkeys !
We have no knowledge of the region
beyond the eastern mountain, which is
devoid of the sun and the other luminaries

and is without limit.
अभिगम्य तु aedt निलयं रावणस्यथ a
मासे पूर्णे निवर्तध्वमुदयं प्राप्य पर्वतम्‌॥ ६९॥
“Having reached the eastern mountain
and definitely found out Sita as well as the
abode of Ravana, return before a month is
complete. (69)
