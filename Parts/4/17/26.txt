लिड्डमप्यस्ति ते राजन्‌ दृश्यते धर्मसंहितम्‌॥ २६॥

“You are a sovereign’s son, hence
worthy of trust, and pleasing of aspect too.
Emblems too such as matted hair and the
bark of trees for clothing, conformable to
righteousness exist and are seen on your
person, O prince! (26)
