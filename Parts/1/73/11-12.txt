“Alongwith his sons, dressed in festive
attires, appropriate to the occasion of wedding,
O king, Emperor DaSaratha, a jewel among
the foremost of men, awaits the pleasure of
the donor; for all gifts take place only with the
co-operation of the donor and the donee.
Therefore, performing the noble rites pertaining
to the wedding, discharge your sacred
obligation (of redeeming the word given by
you to king DaSaratha).” (11-12)
