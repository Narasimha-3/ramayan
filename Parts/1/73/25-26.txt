समक्षमग्रे: संस्थाप्प राघवाभिमुखे तदा॥ २५॥
अब्रवीज्जनको राजा कौसल्यानन्दवर्धनम्‌।
इयं सीता मम सुता सहधर्मचरी qa rei
Duly escorting after that Sita, adorned
with all sorts of jewels, and having
comfortably seated her before the sacred
fire opposite Sri Rama, the ornament of
Raghu’s race, King Janaka then spoke as
follows to Sri Rama, the promoter of
Kausalya’s joy : “This Sita, my daughter, is
going to be your helpmate in discharging
your sacred obligations. (25-26)
