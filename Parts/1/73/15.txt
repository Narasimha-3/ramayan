
कृतकौतुकसर्वस्वा वेदिमूलमुपागता: |

मम कन्या मुनिश्रेष्ठ det बह्नेरिवार्चिष:॥ १५॥
“Having gone through all auspicious rites

connected with the wedding, my daughters,

O jewel among sages, have already arrived

at the foot of the sacrificial altar, looking like
so many blazing flames of fire. (15)
