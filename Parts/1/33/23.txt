स्पृष्टमात्रे तदा पाणौं विकुब्जा विगतज्वरा:।
युक्त परमया लक्ष्म्या बभौ कन्याशतं तदा॥ २३॥

The moment their hands were touched
by Brahmadatta in the course of the wedding,
the girls got cured of their deformity and
were rid of their agony. Invested with supreme
splendour all the hundred girls shone brightly
at that time. (23)
