Seeing the union of her son worthy of
her, Somada, the Gandharva damsel, too
joyfully greeted her son as well as her
aforesaid daughters-in-law, one after another,
in order of seniority and, embracing the said
brides again and again and applauding
KuSanabha for his hospitality and rich dowry,
she departed from there. (26)
