“What may this be, which makes my
heart quiver? My mind is sorrowful.” Hearing
this question of King DaSaratha, the great
seer, Vasistha, replied in a sweet tone
as follows: “Hear what it portends. The
indication that comes from the screaming of
birds in the air is that a grave danger is
imminent. (11-12)
