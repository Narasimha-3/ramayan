“You will witness here the joyous mood
of both your sons, Sri Rama and Laksmana,
acquired through personal prowess. It is
through my good fortune again that the
highly glorious and exceedingly powerful
sage Vasistha has come alongwith all the
jewels among Brahmanas even as Indra,
who attained the rulership of gods by virtue
of his having performed a full one hundred
horse sacrifices in his past life, would in the
company of gods. Fortunately enough all
obstacles that stood in the way of Sita’s
marriage have been thoroughly overcome
and luckily for me, my race has been exalted
through matrimonial alliance with the very
mighty scions of Raghu, distinguished for
their valour. The following morning, at the
conclusion of the sacrifice, O ruler of men,
you ought to celebrate the wedding with the
help of the foremost of sages, O jewel
among men!” Hearing the aforesaid
submission of Janaka in the midst of the
Rsis, DaSaratha, the paramount ruler of
men, the foremost of those who know how
to speak, replied as follows to king Janaka :
“The execution of a gift depends on the
donor: this has been heard by me in the
past. (10—14)
