नाकुण्डली नामुकुटी नास्त्रग्वी नाल्‍्पभोगवान्‌।
नामृष्टो न नलिप्ताड़ो नासुगन्धश्च विद्यते॥१०॥

There was no one in Ayodhya who

was devoid of gold ear-rings, diadem and
flower-wreaths. Nobody lacks in objects of
enjoyment. There was none who did not put
on a smart appearance after taking bath.
No one was there who did not smear his
body with sandal-paste and did not use
scents. (10)
