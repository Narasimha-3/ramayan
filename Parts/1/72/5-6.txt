We sue, 0 jewel among men, as a
wife to prince Bharata as also to the shrewd
Satrughna, the two others sons of Emperor
DaSaratha, the two daughters, of this pious-
minded prince, who are peerless on earth
in point of comeliness. O king, whereas |
ask your two daughters, Sita and Urmila,
for these two high-souled princes, Sri Rama
and Laksmana, O Janaka ! (5-6)
