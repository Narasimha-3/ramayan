ततः प्राक्रमदिष्टिं at पुत्रीयां पुत्रकारणात्‌।
जुहावाग्नौ च तेजस्वी मन्त्रदृष्टेन कर्मणा॥ ३॥

For securing the birth of four sons to
DaSaratha, the glorious Rsyasrnga forthwith
commenced the said sacrifice capable of

procuring a male progeny, and poured
oblations into the sacred fire according to
the procedure shown in the sacred texts.
