ततो देवा: सगन्धर्वा: सिद्धाश्व परमर्षयः।
भागप्रतिग्रहार्थ at समवेता  यथाविधि॥ ४॥

The various gods, including Brahma,
accompanied by Gandharvas (celestial
musicians), Siddhas (a class of semi-divine
beings naturally endowed with mystic powers),
and the greatest of seers assembled there
to accept in person their share of offerings
according to the established procedure. (4)
