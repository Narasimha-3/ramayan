
इष्टिं ase करिष्यामि पुत्रीयां पुत्रकारणात्‌।
अथर्वशिरसि प्रोक्तिरमन्त्रै: सिद्धां विधानत:॥२॥

“For the sake of securing sons to you
| shall duly perform a sacrifice capable of
procuring a male progeny and well-known
for its unfailing effect with the help of Mantras
(sacred texts) taught in the Atharva-Veda.”
