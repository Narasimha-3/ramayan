पृथिव्याश्वापि निर्भदों ge wa सतानतः।
wre च पुत्राणां विनाशो दीर्घदर्शिनाम्‌॥ ४॥

“The excavation of the earth invariably

takes place in every Kalpa. And the
destruction of the sons of Sagara too is
foreseen by men possessing a long range
of vision. Hence there is no occasion for
grief on this account either.” (4)
