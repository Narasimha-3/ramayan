ततः प्रागुत्तरां गत्वा सागरा: प्रथितां दिशम्‌।
रोषादभ्यखनन्‌ सर्वे पृथिवीं सगरात्मजा:॥ २४॥
Proceeding in the celebrated north-
easterly direction, presided over by Lord
Siva and as such held sacred for all holy
undertakings, all the sons of Sagara
continued digging the earth in an agitated
mood. (24)
