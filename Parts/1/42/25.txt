
तमेवमुक्त्वा राजानं Wet चाभाष्यलोककृत्‌।
जगाम त्रिदिवं देवै: सर्वे: सह मरुद्रणैः॥ २५॥

Having thus spoken to the aforesaid
king and instructed Ganga as well to oblige
Bhagiratha when the time comes, Brahma,
the maker of the universe, rose to
Brahmaloka with all the gods including the
(forty-nine) wind-gods. (25)
