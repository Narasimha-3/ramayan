
वसिष्ठस्यथाअश्रमपदं_शून्यमासीन्महात्मन:।
मुहूर्तमिव निःशब्दमासीदीरिणसंनिभम्‌॥ २४॥
वबदतो a वसिष्ठस्थ मा भेरिति मुहूर्मुहुः।
नाशयाम्यद्य गाधेयं नीहारमिव भास्कर:॥ २०॥

“The site of the hermitage of the mighty
Vasistha turned desolate. In an hour or so
it became noiseless and looked barren as it
were in spite of Vasistha repeatedly calling
out : ‘Don’t be afraid, | shall presently make
short work of ViSwamitra, the son of Gadhi,

as the sun dispels fog.’ (24-25)
