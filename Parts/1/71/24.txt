मघा weal महाबाहो तृतीयदिवसे weit
फल्गुन्यामुत्तरे राज॑स्तस्मिन्‌ वैवाहिकं कुरु।
रामलक्ष्मणयोरर्थे at कार्य सुखोदयम्‌॥ २४॥

Indeed, the constellation Magha is in

the ascendant today, O mighty-armed king !
On the third day, when the celebrated Uttara
Phalguni mansion is in the ascendant, my
lord, perform the wedding ceremony. Gifts,
which are calculated to bring happiness in
its wake, should be made for the welfare of
Sri Rama and Laksmana. (24)
