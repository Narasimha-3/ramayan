
कदाचित्‌ तु महातेजा योजयित्वा वरूथिनीम्‌।
अक्षौहिणीपरिवृतः परिचक्राम मेदिनीम्‌॥ २१॥

“Collecting an army and accompanied
by an Aksauhini (a division consisting of
21,870 elephants, an equal number of
chariots, 65,610 horse and 1,09,350 foot),
the exceptionally glorious king once went
round the globe. (21)
