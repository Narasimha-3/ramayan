“Marching successively across cities
and states, rivers and great mountains and
hermitages, the king, they say, came to the
site of Vasistha’s hermitage, rich in flowers,
creepers and trees of various kinds, infested
with hordes of wild animals of every
description, and frequented by Siddhas and

Caranas. (22-23)
