
दशरथनृपसूनुसत्तमाभ्यां तृणशयने5नुचिते तदोषिताभ्याम्‌ ।
कुशिकसुतबचो5नुलालिताभ्यां सुखमिव सा विबभौ विभावरी च॥ २४ ॥

To Sri Rama and Laksmana, the two
jewels among the sons of King DaSaratha,
who, even though they lay there on a bed of
straw, unworthy of princes, were bestowed
with love all along by ViSwamitra (son of
Gadhi), that night appeared delightful indeed.
