पश्य लक्ष्मण दुर्वृत्तान्‌ राक्षसान्‌ पिशिताशनान्‌।
मानवास्त्रसमाधूताननिलेन यथा घनानू॥ १५॥

करिष्यामि न संदेहो नोत्सहे हन्तुमीदृशान्‌।
इत्युक्वा वचन WAM संधाय वेगवान्‌॥ १६॥
मानव परमोदारमस्त्र परमभास्वरम्‌।
चिक्षेप परमक्रुद्धो मारीचोरसि राघव:॥ १७॥

“Look here, 0 Laksmana, | shall scatter
the wicked Raksasas, that feed on raw flesh,
by means of the Manavastra, the missile
presided over by Swayambhuva Manu, as
clouds are dispersed by the wind: there is no
doubt about it. | do not feel inclined to kill
them as they are destined to survive for some
more years.” Having made this observation,
Sri Rama, a scion of Raghu, who was full of
agility, put to his bow the most excellent and
supremely effulgent missile presided over by
Manu, and hurled it most angrily at the breast
of Marica. (15—17)
