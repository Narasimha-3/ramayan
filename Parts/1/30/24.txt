
स हत्वा राक्षसान्‌ सर्वान्‌ यज्ञघ्नान्‌ रघुनन्दन:।
ऋषिभि: पूजितस्तत्र यथेन्द्रो विजये पुरा॥२४॥

Having got rid in this way of all
the Raksasas that interfered with the
performance of sacrifices, Sri Rama was
honoured in that holy retreat by the Rsis
even as Indra was felicitated in the past on
his victory scored over demons. (24)
