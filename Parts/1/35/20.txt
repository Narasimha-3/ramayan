“The other daughter of Himavan, who
looked upon askesis as Her only wealth, O
scion of Raghu, practised austerities in the
shape of meditation on Lord Siva, while
remaining a virgin and observing a noble and
formidable vow of forgoing even dry leaves
to keep Her body and soul together. (20)
