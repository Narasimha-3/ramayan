तदप्यस्त्र॑ महाघोरं ब्राह्मं ब्राहेण तेजसा।
वसिष्ठो ग्रसते सर्व ब्रह्मदण्डेन राघव॥ १६॥

Vasistha, however, neutralized even that
exceptionally terrible Brahmastra in its entirety,
including all its limbs, by his Brahmanical staff
combined with the Brahmanical (spiritual)
glory, O scion of Raghu ! (16)
