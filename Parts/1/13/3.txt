
यज्ञों मे क्रियतां ब्रह्मनू यथोक्त मुनिपुंगव।
यथा न foot: क्रियन्ते यज्ञाड़ेषु विधीयताम्‌॥ ३॥

“Let the sacrifice intended to be
performed by me, O holy one, be commenced
as enjoined by the scriptures, O jewel among
sages, and steps may be taken to ensure
that no obstacles may be thrown by the
ogres etc., in the way of items forming part
of the sacrifice. (3)
