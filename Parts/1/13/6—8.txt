ततोअब्रवीद्‌ द्विजान्‌ वृद्धान्‌ यज्ञकर्मसुनिष्ठितान्‌।
स्थापत्ये निष्ठितांश्चैव वृद्धान्‌ परमधार्मिकान्‌॥ ६॥
कर्मान्तिकाडिशिल्पकारान्‌ वर्धकीन्‌ खनकानपि।
गणकाउजिशल्पिनश्चैव तथेव  नटनर्तकानू॥ ७॥
तथा शुच्रीज्शास्त्रविद: पुरुषान्‌ सुबहुश्रुतान्‌।
यज्ञकर्म समीहन्तां भवन्तो राजशासनातू॥ ८॥
The sage Vasistha then spoke to elderly
Brahmanas well-versed in ritual acts as
well as to elderly and most pious Brahmanas,
skilled in architecture relating to sacrifices,
as also to servants engaged for the whole
sacrificial session, artists, carpenters, those
entrusted with the work of digging the
sacrificial pits, astronomers and artisans,
nay, even to actors and dancers and likewise
to pure-minded men well-versed in sacred
lore and to those whose knowledge extended
over a very wide range of subjects:
“Undertake you your respective duties
connected with the sacrifice under orders of
His Majesty. (6—8)
