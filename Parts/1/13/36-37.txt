उपयाता नरव्याप्र राजानस्तव शासनातू।
मयापि सत्कृता: सर्वे यथाई राजसत्तम॥ ३६॥
यज्ञियं a ad सर्व पुरुष: सुसमाहितैः।
निर्यातु च var, यष्टुं यज्ञायतनमन्तिकातू्‌॥ ३७॥

“Kings have arrived at your command,
O tiger among men! Nay, all have been
received with hospitality according to their
deserts by me, O jewel among monarchs !

Moreover, everything connected with the
sacrifice has been got ready by your men

with great attention. Therefore, proceed you
to the sacrificial hall close by to commence
the sacrifice. (36-37)
