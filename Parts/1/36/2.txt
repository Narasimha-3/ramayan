
धर्मयुक्तमिदं ब्रह्मनू कथितं wi त्वया।

दुहितु:ः शैलराजस्य ज्येष्ठाया वक्तुमहसि।
विस्तरं विस्तरज्ञोडसि दिव्यमानुषसम्भवम्‌॥ २॥
“You have narrated, holy Sir, this most
excellent story capable of conferring merit on
those who hear it. Be pleased now to give a
detailed account of Ganga (the elder daughter
of Himavan), relating to her celestial and earthly
career, since you know these details. (2)
