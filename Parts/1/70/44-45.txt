
आदिवंशविशुद्धानां wat परमधर्मिणाम्‌।
इक्ष्वाकुकुलजातानां ATO सत्यवादिनाम्‌॥ ४४॥
THs aad aa TTI
सदूशाभ्यां नरश्रेष्ठ सदृशे दातुमहसि॥ ४५॥

“On behalf of King Dasaratha, as his
family priest, | sue for your two daughters,
O protector of men, for the sake of Sri
Rama and Laksmana, belonging as they do
to the line of exceedingly pious, valiant and
truthful monarchs born in the race of Ikswaku
and absolutely pure of descent from the
very beginning. Be pleased, O jewel among
men, to give away your worthy daughters to
the two equally worthy princes.” (44-45)
