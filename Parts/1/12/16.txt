
सरय्वाश्चोत्ते तीरे यज्ञभूमिविधीयताम्‌।
शान्तयशएचाभिवर्धन्तां यथाकल्पं यथाविधि॥ १६॥

“Nay, let 9 sacrificial ground be
prepared on the northern bank of the Sarayu
and let propitiatory rites (intended to avert
evils) be elaborately gone through in proper
order of sequence and in accordance with
the scriptural ordinance. (16)
