
गुरूणां वचनाच्छीघ्नं सम्भारा: सम्भ्रियन्तु मे।
समर्थाधिष्ठितश्ना श्र: सोपाध्यायो विमुच्यताम्‌॥ १५॥

“Let the requisites for the sacrifice
be got together apace according to the
instructions of my elders. And let the sacrificial
horse be let loose under the superintendence
of four hundred Ksatriya princes capable of
guarding and controlling it, alongwith the
chief-priest. (15)
