
ACT सुप्रजा राम Gat संध्या प्रवर्तते।
उत्तिष्ठ नरशार्दूल कर्तव्यं comin २॥

“Kausalya is blessed with a worthy son
in you, O Rama! The morning twilight has
setin. Therefore, get up, O tiger among men !
Contemplation on the Deity and the daily
routine of duties making for the purification of
the body have to be gone through.” (2)
