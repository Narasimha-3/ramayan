
जपतस्तु मुनेस्तस्य विश्वामित्रस्थ धीमत:।
उपतस्थुर्महार्हाणि सर्वाण्यस्त्राणि राघवम्‌॥ २४॥

Even while that intelligent sage
ViSwamitra. was silently repeating the
Mantras, embodying the said missiles, all
the missiles, worthy of great adoration as
they were, appeared before Sri Rama in
their shining ethereal forms. (24)
