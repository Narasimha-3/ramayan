
स्थितस्तु प्राइमुखो भूत्वा शुचिर्मुनिवरस्तदा।
ददौ रामाय सुप्रीतो मन्त्रग्राममनुत्तमम्‌॥ २२॥

Getting purified through ablutions etc.,
and sitting with his face turned towards the
east, ViSwamitra, the foremost of hermits,
who was highly delighted, imparted to Sri
Rama a string of Mantras (sacred texts
capable of invoking the missiles) unexcelled
by others in their field. (22)
