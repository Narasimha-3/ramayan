Nay, full of joy, all spoke as follows
with folded hands (as a mark of respect) to
Sri Rama: “Here do we stand as your
servants, O supermely generous Sri Rama,
a scion of Raghu! We are prepared to do
all that you would have us do. May good
betide you !” Addressed thus by those mighty
missiles in living form, Sri Rama, a scion of
Kakutstha, thereupon accepted them with a
cheerful mind as his own and, stroking
them with his hand, commanded them as
follows: “Appear in my mind whenever |
think of you.” (25—27)
