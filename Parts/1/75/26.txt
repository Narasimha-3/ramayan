
द्त्त्वा महेन्द्रनिलयस्तपोबलसमन्वित: |
TAT तु धनुषो भेदं ततोऊहं द्रुतमागतः॥ २६॥

“Nay, having made the gift, | took up
my abode on Mount Mahendra. Richly
endowed with spiritual energy earned through
austerities in the meantime, however, |
expeditiously arrived here from that distant
place on hearing of the breaking of the bow.
