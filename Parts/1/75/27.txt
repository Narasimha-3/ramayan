क्षत्रधर्म पुरस्कृत्य Weta धनुरुत्तमम्‌॥ २७॥

Placing above all else the noble duty of
a Ksatriya (which consists in accepting the
challenge of a warrior to a duel in a righteous
cause), O Rama, take in your hand the
celebrated and superb bow belonging to
Lord Visnu, inherited by me through my
father from my grandfather. (27)
