
पृथिवीं चाखिलां प्राप्प कश्यपाय महात्मने।
यज्ञस्थान्तेदददं॑ राम दक्षिणां पुण्यकर्मणे॥ २५॥
“Having acquired dominion over the
entire globe in this way, | gifted it, O Rama, to
the high-souled sage Kasyapa of meritorious

deeds by way of sacrificial fee at the end
of a sacrifice which | performed in atonement

for the great bath of blood. (25)
