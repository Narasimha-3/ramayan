इति हृदयमनोविदारणं मुनिवचनं तदतीव शुश्रुवान्‌।

नरपतिरभवन्महान्‌ महात्मा व्यधितमना: प्रचचाल चासनातू्‌॥ २२॥
The great king, magnanimous though

he was, felt afflicted in mind even as he

heard the aforesaid request of the sage,
which was extremely agonizing to the heart
and the mind, and he thus fell down
unconscious from his seat. (22)
