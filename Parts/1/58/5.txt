
बालिशस्त्वं नरश्रेष्ठ गम्यतां स्वपुरं पुनः।
याजने भगवान्‌ शक्तस्त्रैलोक्यस्थापि पार्थिव॥ ५॥

“You are yet puerile of understanding,
O jewel among men! Return, therefore, to
your capital. The supremely glorious Vasistha
is even capable of conducting the sacrifices
of all the three worlds, O ruler of the earth !
