“Nay, the elders no less than exalted
souls have been gratified through my good
qualities and right conduct. My preceptors,

however, did not derive satisfaction through
me even though | strove hard in the cause
of righteousness and sought to undertake a
sacrifice in order to be able to attain to
heaven in my physical body, O jewel among
sages! |, therefore, reckon fate alone as
supreme and personal effort as of no avail
when matched against fate. (20—22)
