ततः प्रभाते विमले कृतकर्मा नराधिपः।
विश्वामित्रं महात्मानमाजुहाव सराघवम्‌॥ १॥

Having concluded his religious rites for
the morning, Janaka, a ruler of men, sent
for the high-souled Viswamitra alongwith Sri
Rama and Laksmana, the two scions of
Raghu, the next day when the day had
clearly dawned. (1)
