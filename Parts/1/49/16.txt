
सा fe गौतमवाक्येन दुर्निरीक्ष्य aya ह।
त्रयाणामपि लोकानां यावद्‌ रामस्य दर्शनम्‌।
शापस्यान्तमुपागम्य at दर्शनमागता॥ १६॥

Under the curse of Gautama, really
speaking, she had grown imperceptible to
all the three worlds, pending her sight of Sri
Rama; so it is said. Having reached the end
of the curse she had now come within the
range of their sight. (16)
