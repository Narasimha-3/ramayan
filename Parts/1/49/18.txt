
पाद्यमर्घ्य॑ तथा55तिथ्यं चकार सुसमाहिता।
प्रतिजग्राह काकुत्स्थो विधिदृष्टेन कर्मणा॥ १८॥

Fully composed in mind she offered to
them, with due ceremony as enjoined by the
scriptural ordinance, water to wash their feet
and hands with as well as other hospitality
and Sri Rama gladly accepted them. (18)
