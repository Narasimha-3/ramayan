Living on fruits and roots alone and
with his mind and senses fully controlled,
he practised austerities of a high order.
Immediately after his commencing the course
of his austerities were born to him four sons
devoted to truthfulness and virtue—
Havispanda, Madhuspanda, Drdhanetra and
Maharatha by name. On the completion of a
thousand years Brahma (the progenitor of
the universe) actually appeared and
addressed the following sweet words to
ViSwamitra, who was rich in asceticism:
“The worlds attained by royal sages have

been won by you through askesis, O son of
Kusika (Gadhi) ! (3—5)
