कैकेय्यै चावशिष्टार्थ ददौ पुत्रार्थकारणात्‌।
प्रददौ चावशिष्टार्ध पायसस्यामृतोपमम्‌॥ २८ ॥
अनुचिन्त्य॒सुमित्राय॑ पुनरेवमहामतिः।
एवं तासां ददौ राजा भार्याणां पायसं पृथक्‌ ॥ २९॥
Again he gave half of the rest to Kaikeyi
and, reflecting awhile, the highly intelligent
DaSaratha gave the other half of the nectar-
like Payasa once more to Sumitra. In this
way the monarch apportioned the Payasa
separately to all his aforementioned wives.
(28-29)
