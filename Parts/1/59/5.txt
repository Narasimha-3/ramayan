हस्तप्राप्तमह॑ मन्ये स्वर्ग तव॒ नराधिप।
यस्त्व॑ कौशिकमागम्य शरण्यं शरणागतः॥ ५॥

“| consider heaven as already attained
by you, O ruler of men, in that having
sought the presence of ViSwamitra, son of
KuSika, i.e., myself, you have come for
protection to one who is capable of affording
shelter to those who need it.” (5)
