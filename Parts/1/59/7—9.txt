Summoning all his dependents, so the
tradition goes, he gave the following
instructions to them : “Under my orders bring

here all the Rsis, including the sons of
Vasistha, as well as their relations well-
versed in a good many subjects, alongwith
their pupils and priests capable of officiating
at a sacrifice. If anyone who is summoned
on the strength of my word or anyone else
makes any disparaging observation, all
those remarks should be reproduced to
me in their entirety.” Hearing the aforesaid
order of ViSwamitra, his followers proceeded
in various directions under his command.
