लवण: क्रोधसंयुक्तो युद्धाय समुपस्थितः।
आकर्णात्‌ स विकृष्याथ ag धनुर्धन्विनां वर: ॥ ३४॥
a मुमोच महाबाणं लवणस्य महोरसि।
उरस्तस्थ विदार्याशु प्रविवेश रसातलम्‌॥ ३५॥
गत्वा रसातलं दिव्य: शरो विबुधपूजितः।
पुनरेबागमत्‌ तूर्णमिक्ष्याकुकुलनन्दनम्‌॥ ३६॥

Lavana filled with anger appeared to give
him (Satrughna) the fight. He, Satrughna, the
best among archers, then having stretched
that bow up to his ear, shot the arrow at the
mighty chest of Lavana. That divine arrow
descended to the nether-world breaking open
Lavana’s chest, and having gone there, being
worshipped by the gods, came back quickly
again to Satrughna, the descendant of the
family of Iksvaku. (34—36)
