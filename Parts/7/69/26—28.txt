‘All the great gods are bewildered at the
power of that arrow ! O my children, this is
the eternal powerful arrow of the former God,
the creator of the world, Visnu, due to which
the gods are in fear; this mighty arrow was
created for the destruction of those two sons
of Diti, Madhu and Kaitabha; Visnu alone knows
well about this powerful arrow. (26—28)
