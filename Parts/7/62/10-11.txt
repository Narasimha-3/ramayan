Hearing the words of Bharata,
(Satrughna) younger brother of Laksmana,
stood up leaving his golden seat. Having
bowed to the King, Sri Rama, Satrughna
said: The middle one, Bharata, among us,
possessed of long arms, O descendant of
Raghu, has already performed his share.
