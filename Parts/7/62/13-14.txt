Gar च बहूनीह अनुभूतानि पार्थिव।
wart दुःखशप्यासु नन्दिग्रामे महायशा:॥१३॥
'फलमूलाशनो भूत्वा जटी चीरधरस्तथा।
अनुभूयेदृ्शं दुःखमेष. राघवनन्दन:॥ १४॥
‘Here many sufferings were endured by
him, O king: sleeping on uncomfortable bed
in the Nandigrama, this noble one consuming
only fruits and roots, with matted hair, and dress
of bark, has greatly experienced sorrow, O
descendant of the Raghus. (13-14)
