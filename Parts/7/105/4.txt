Laksmana the killer of the enemy heroes,
on hearing words of the ascetic, spoke after

wonderstruck

having saluted the noble one, Durvasa:
किं कार्य afe भगवन्‌ को ह्ार्थ: fee करोम्यहम्‌।
व्यग्रों हि राघवो ब्रह्मन्‌ मुहूर्त परिपाल्यताम्‌॥ ४॥
‘Sir, what has to be done for you for
what purpose you have come? What should
| do? The descendant of Raghu is now
indeed busy, O Brahmana, wait for a
moment.’ (4)
