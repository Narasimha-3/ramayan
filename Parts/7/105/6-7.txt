भरतं चैव सौमित्रे युष्माकं या a संततिः।
न हि शक्ष्याम्यहं भूयो wat धारयितुं हदि॥७॥
‘Inform about me at this very moment
Sri Rama, O son of Sumitra, If you do not do
so, | shall curse this city, you, this territory,
Bharata and also Rama and even this city’s
progeny, | am indeed unable to contain my
anger inside me anymore. (6-7)
