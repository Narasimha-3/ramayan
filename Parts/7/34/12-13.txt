ग्रहीतुं वालिनं तूर्ण निःशब्दपदमब्रजतू॥ १३॥

Alighting from the Puspaka on seeing
Vali, who resembled a mountain of gold and
whose countenance shone like the newly
risen sun, absorbed in saying the Sandhya
prayers, Ravana who had the hue of
collyrium, proceeded hastily with silent steps
in order to capture him. (12-13)
