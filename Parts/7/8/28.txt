WH मया तव नराधिप राक्षसाना-
मुत्पत्तिद्य कथिता सकला यथावत्‌।
भूयो Prater रघुसत्तम रावणस्य
जन्मप्रभावमतुल॑ ससुतस्य॒सर्वम्‌॥ २८॥
“O king, | have thus related in detail to
you today all about the origin of the Raksasas.
Learn from me further, O jewel among the
Raghus, the origin of Ravana and his son,

as also all about their immeasurable powers.
