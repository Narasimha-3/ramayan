fea: सपौरैः पुरुहूतसंनिभः।
ततोडर्जुनः tat प्रविवेश at पुरी

बलि निगृहोव सहस्त्रलोचन:॥ ७३॥
Being covered with heaps of flowers
and unbroken grains of rice by Brahmanas

(the twice-born) and other citizens on
capturing Ravana, Arjuna, who closely
resembled Indra (who is invoked by many),
thereupon entered his well-known city even

as Indra (the thousand-eyed god) entered
his city after capturing Bali. (73)
