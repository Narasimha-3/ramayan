श्रुत्वा तु व्याहतं Akt सूतस्य परमाद्धुतम्‌।
प्रहर्षमतुलं॑ लेभे साधु साध्विति चाब्रवीतू॥ २९॥

Laksmana felt great relief and was

pleased to hear the charioteer speak such
wonderful things. He cried out. “Excellent !

You have said well’.

ततः संवदतोरेव॑ सूतलक्ष्मणयो: afer

अस्तमर्के गते वासं केशिन्यां तावथोषतु:॥ ३०॥
Then, the setting sun descended on

the mountain, while the charioteer and

Laksmana were thus conversing; they

prepared for night halt on the banks of the
Kesini river. (30)
