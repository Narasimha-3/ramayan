They (Brahmanas) approved of that
sacrifice indeed for the appeasement of God
Rudra (Siva). A Rsi among kings, well-
known as Marutta, destroyer of enemies,
who was the pupil of Sarnvarta, made
necessary arrangements for that sacrifice.
A great sacrifice was performed close to
the hermitage of Budha. (14-15)
