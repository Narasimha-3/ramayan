ta: पुरूरवा राजा प्रतिष्ठानमवाप्तवान्‌।
sa ह्ाश्वमेधस्य प्रभाव: पुरुषर्षभौ।
स्त्रीभूतः पौरुषं लेभे यच्चान्यदपि दुर्लभमू्‌॥ २४॥

The king, Pururava, son of Ila, obtained
Pratisthana, such is the power of ASvamedha,
O bull among men, one who was formerly

a woman, achieved manhood—all that was
difficult to obtain.’ (24)
