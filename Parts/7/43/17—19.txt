कीदृशं हृदये तस्य सीतासम्भोगजं सुखम्‌।
AAT तु पुरा रावणेन बलाद्धृताम्‌॥ १७॥
agra पुरा नीतामशोकवनिकां गताम्‌।
Tae वशमापन्नां कथं WaT न कुत्स्यति॥ १८॥
अस्माकमपि दारेषु सहनीयं भविष्यति।
यथा हि कुरुते राजा प्रजास्तमनुवर्तते॥ १९॥

“What must be the pleasure in his
heart born out of conjugation with Sri Sita?
Why does not Sri Rama censure her, who
formerly had been forcibly carried away by
Ravana, placing her in his lap and even
taken to Lanka, first, who went into the
Asoka grove and remained under the control
of the Raksasas. Such conduct of our wives
shall have to be suffered by us also, since
whatever a king does, the subjects follow.’
(17—19)
