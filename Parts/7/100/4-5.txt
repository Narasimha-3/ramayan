श्रुत्वा तु राघवों धीमान्‌ महर्षि गार्ग्यमागतम्‌।
मातुलस्याश्वपतिन:  प्रहितं तन्महाधनम्‌॥ ४॥

WIR च AHR: HVAT सहानुजः।
गार्ग्य सम्पूजयामास यथा शक्रो बृहस्पतिम्‌॥ ५॥

Having heard that the great sage
Gargya had come, bringing with him the rich
gifts sent by the maternal uncle, Asvapati,
Sri Rama, alongwith his brothers, welcomed
Gargya at a distance of two miles in advance,
and honoured Gargya as Indra honours
Brhaspati. (4-5)
