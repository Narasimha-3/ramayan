After some time, the King of Kekaya,
Yudhajit, sent his preceptor, Brahmana
Gargya, the son of Angira, possessed of
inimitable lustre to Sri Rama, the noble
descendant of Raghu. The King presented
a gift of ten thousand horses as a token of
affection and also blankets and precious
gems, and garments of various kinds and

brilliant ornaments. (1—8)
