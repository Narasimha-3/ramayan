Paying salutation to Sri Rama with his
head and going round Him, and falling at the
feet of Laksmana and Bharata, and the chief
priest Vasistha, Satrughna of pious mind, the
torturer of the foes, the mighty one going round
Sri Rama again, departed. (16-17)
