एवमुक्ता तु सा कन्या राम कालेन केनचित्‌।
जनयामास बीभत्सं रक्षोरूपं सुदारुणम्‌॥ २८॥
दशग्रीव॑ महादंष्टूं नीलाझ्ननचयोपमम्‌।
ताम्रोष्ठ॑ विंशतिभुजं महास्यं दीप्तमूर्धजम्‌॥ २९॥
“Spoken to in these words, 0 Rama,
that girl in course of time brought forth a
hideous child having the form of a Raksasa,
very cruel, with ten heads, huge teeth,

resembling a heap of antimony, having
coppery lips, with twenty hands, vast mouths,
and fiery hair. (28-29)
