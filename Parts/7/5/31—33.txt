“At that time, 0 Sri Rama, the delight of
the Raghus, there lived a Gandharva woman
by name Narmada. She had three daughters
equal in lustre to Hr, Sr and Kirti, the
goddesses presiding over modesty, prosperity
and fame, respectively. This non-Raksasa
lady voluntarily gave all the three Gandharva
maidens, whose faces were beaming with
joy like the full moon, in the order of their
seniority in age, in marriage to the three
Raksasas. (31—33)
