‘For which object have you practised
penances, that | want to hear, O ascetic?
Are you a Brahmana? O good man, may
welfare be with you, are you a Ksatriya
difficult to overcome, or belonging to the
third caste, a VaisSya, or a Stdra? Please
speak out the truth.’ (18)
