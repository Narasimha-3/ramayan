व्यसन सुमहत्‌ क्रुद्ध: स ते दद्यान्महातपा:।
यदि वान्यन्मया कार्य धर्मदृष्टेन सत्पथा॥१०॥
वरयस्व ars पितरं मे महाद्युतिम्‌।
अन्यथा तु et PS भवेद्‌ घोराभिसंहितम्‌॥ ११॥

‘Enraged, he (Bhargava), the performer
of great penances, shall cause you much
grief. If, however, for something other than
mere passion, you desire me, then ask my
hand following the righteous path from
my father, O best among men; otherwise
you will meet with frightful consequences.

(10-11)
