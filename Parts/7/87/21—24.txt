Having become a woman, he did not
ask for any other boon from the Lord of the
gods, Siva. Then in great sorrow, the King
paying obeisance to the daughter of the lord
of mountains, Parvati, with all his heart,
said: ‘O goddess of boons, giver of boons,
you are the loveliest woman of the worlds.

O goddess whose vision is fruitful, with your
benevolent eye favour me. Knowing what was
in the mind of the Rsi among kings, Ila, She
spoke these auspicious words approved by
Lord Siva in his presence: ‘The giver of one
half of the boon is the Lord and that of the
other half | am the giver. (21—24)
