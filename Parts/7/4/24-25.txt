“The Raksasi repaired to the slopes of
Mount Mandara and gave birth to a son,
brilliant like lightning, even as Goddess Ganga
delivered the offspring (of Lord Siva) released
by Agni. Desiring to revel with Vidyutkesa,
she delivered the child and revelled with her
husband, forgetting all about her own child.
Deserted by her, the baby then rumbled like
a cloud. (24-25)
