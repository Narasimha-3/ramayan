
पुरमाकाशगं प्रादात्‌ पार्वत्या: प्रियकाम्यया।
उमयापि वरो दत्तो राक्षसीनां नृपात्मज॥३०॥
सद्योपलब्धिर्गर्भस्थ प्रसूतिः सद्य wa al
wa wa वयःप्रापतिं मातुरेव वयःसमम्‌ू॥ ३१॥
“To please Parvati, Siva gave him a
flying city; Uma also gave to the Raksasa
woman the boon of instantaneous conception,
instantaneous delivery and instantaneous
growing up of the child to the age of its
mother. (30-31)
