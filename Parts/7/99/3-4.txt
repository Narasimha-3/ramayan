अपश्यमानो बैदेहीं मेने शून्यमिदं जगत्‌।
शोकेन परमायस्तो न शान्ति मनसागमतू॥ ४॥

“On Sita entering the netherworld through
the efficacy of truth, Sri Rama was much
agitated at the conclusion of the sacrifice
and considered this earth as a void. Not
being able to see Sita and being terribly
afflicted by sorrow, he had no peace of
mind. (3-4)
