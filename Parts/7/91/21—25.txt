‘May Bharata proceed carrying coins

in many hundred millions of gold and silver,
carefully. Many rows of mobile markets, all
actors and dancers, cooks, many damsels
possessed of eternal charm, may they
accompany Bharata; the citizens, both young
and aged ones, Brahmanas in front leading
the march, the labourers, carpenters,
treasurers and knowers of Veda, all my
mothers (Kaikeyi with Sumitra and Kausalya),
the spouses of the princes (Bharata etc.)
‘the gold statue of my wife, Sita, and the
knowers of the consecration ceremony, may
Bharata possessed of great fame proceed.’
(21—25)
