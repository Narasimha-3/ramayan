(0 Laksmana, invite all those
Brahmanas, who have migrated to other
countries, are devoted to righteousness, on
the occasion of ASvamedha. O one of long
arms, invite the sages rich in penance, and
all the Brahmanas staying abroad together
with their spouses. (13-14)
