qe: क्रोधाभिभूतेन विनाशार्थ दुरात्मनो:॥२१॥
मधुकैटभयोवीर विघाते  सर्वरक्षसाम्‌।
स््रष्टुकामेन लोकांस्त्रीस्ती चानेन eat युधि॥ २२॥
तौ हत्वा जनभोगार्थे कैटभं तु मधुं तथा।
अनेन शरमुख्येन ततो लोकांश्वकार सः॥२३॥
‘Therefore, this one, the best among
arrows, is invisible to all beings, this was
created by angry Visnu, desirous of the
destruction of the two wicked ones, Madhu
and Kaitabha, and creation of the three
worlds after the two and the other Raksasas
were killed with this arrow. (21—23)
