यच्च तस्य महतच्छूलं त््यम्बकेण महात्मना।
दत्त शत्रुविनाशाय मधोरायुधमुत्तमम्‌॥ २५॥
ad संनिश्षिप्प भवने पूज्यमानं पुनः पुनः।
दिश: Gat: समासाद्य प्राप्नोत्याहारमुत्तमम्‌॥ २६॥
‘The great trident, the best among
weapons, that was given to Madhu by the
mighty three-eyed Lord Siva for the purpose

of destroying the foes; placing that honourable
weapon at home, Lavana proceeding to all
quarters repeatedly acquires the best of his
food. (25-26)
