आदिप्रभूति वै राजन्‌ पश्ञसर्गशतानि a
काण्डानि षटकृतानीह सोत्तराणि महात्मना॥ २७॥
कृतानि गुरुणास्माकमृषिणा चरितं तव।
प्रतिष्ठा जीवितं यावत्‌ तावत्‌ सर्वस्य वर्तते॥ २८॥
‘From the beginning, O King, five hundred cantos with six Kandas (parts) together
with the Uttara (best Kanda) have been
composed by the noble one, our preceptor,
sage Valmiki, keeping in mind your life’s deeds
which will continue till all beings live. (27-28)
