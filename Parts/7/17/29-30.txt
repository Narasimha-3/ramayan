
असिर्भूत्वा करस्तस्या: केशांश्छिन्नांस्तदाकरोत्‌ |

सा ज्वलन्तीव रोषेण दहन्तीव निशाचरम्‌॥ २९॥

उवाचाग्निं समाधाय मरणाय HAA |

धर्षितायास्त्वयानार्य न मे जीवितमिष्यते॥ ३०॥
“Getting transformed her hand _ into

sword, she then cut off her hair. Hastening

to put an end to her life, she kindled a fire.
Burning, as it were, with anger, she spoke

to the Raksasa as if she would burn him:
‘Insulted by you, O ignoble man, | no longer
desire to live. (29-30)
