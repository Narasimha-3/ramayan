शापे त्वयि मयोत्सृष्टे तपसश्न व्ययो भवेत्‌।
यदि त्वस्ति मया किंचित्‌ कृतं od Et तथा॥ ३३॥
तस्मात्‌ त्वयोनिजा साध्वी भवेयं धर्मिण: सुता।
एवमुक्त्वा प्रविष्टा सा ज्वलितं जातवेदसमू॥ ३४॥
“‘And if | pronounce a curse on you,
my reserve of Tapas will be lost. If | have
done anything meritorious or have given
gifts or tended the sacred fire, | will surely
appear as Ayonija (not born of the womb),
the pious daughter of a virtuous man.’ Having
said thus, she entered the kindled fire.
(33-34)
