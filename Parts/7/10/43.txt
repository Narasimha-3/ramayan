वाणि त्वं राक्षसेन्द्रस्य भव वाग्देवतेप्सिता।
तथेत्युक्त्वा प्रविष्टा सा प्रजापतिरथाब्रवीतू॥ ४३॥
‘O goddess of speech, as the gods desire,
you become the speech in the mouth of the
chief of ogres, Kumbhakarna.’ Saying ‘Be it
so’, she entered the mouth of Kumbhakarna.
