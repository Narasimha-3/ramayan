गेयात्‌ पुष्पसमृद्धय्ा च शैत्याद्‌ वायोर्गिरे्गुणात्‌।
प्रवृत्तायां रजन्यां च चन्द्रस्योदयनेन च॥१२॥
रावण: स महावीर्य: कामस्य वशमागत:।
विनि:श्वस्य विनि:श्वस्य शशिनं समवैक्षत॥ १३॥

By reason of the music, the wealth of
blossom, the coolness of the wind, the charm
of the hill and the rising of the moon, now
that the night had commenced, the aforesaid
Ravana, who was exceptionally powerful,
fell a prey to concupiscence. Sighing again
and again, he gazed at the moon. (12-13)
