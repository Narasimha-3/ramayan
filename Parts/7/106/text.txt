Laksmana departs and goes to heaven
alongwith his body

arrival of Durvasa and also the promise

‘O one of long arms, this end of yours
was foreseen by me, which causes
horripilation and also separation from
Laksmana, O Sri Rama, possessed of glory.

‘Leave him, Laksmana. Time (Death)
is indeed too powerful, do not give up your
promise, for, it being given up, righteousness
will perish. (9)

‘With righteousness gone, the three
worlds, both moving and stationary, indeed
the hosts of divine sages, shall perish, there

‘Therefore, O lion among men, separated
from Laksmana today, you, on your part,
restore normalcy to this earth with the
protection of the three worlds.’ (11)

Hearing these words full of righteous
import, spoken by those assembled,
Sri Rama said to Laksmana in the midst of
that assembly: (12)

‘| leave you, 0 son of Sumitra
(Laksmana), may not the cause of
righteousness suffer; desertion or slaying—
both are the same to the pious ones.’ (13)

On Sri Rama speaking thus, Laksmana,
with his eyes filled with tears, went out
in haste and did not enter his apartment.

to heaven, honoured indeed the descendant
of Raghu, Laksmana. (18)
