सुमन्त्रस्तु तथेत्युक्त्वा युक्त परमवाजिभि:।
रथं सुरुचिरप्रख्यं स्वास्तीर्ण सुखशय्यया॥ ४॥
आनीयोवाच् सौमित्रिं मित्राणां मानवर्धनम्‌।
waist समनुप्राप्तो यत्कार्य क्रियतां प्रभो॥५॥

“It shall be as you say !”, replying thus
Sumantra, after having brought the chariot
yoked with the best horses, beautifully
shaped and well-equipped with comfortable
seats, spoke thus again to laksmana (son
of Sumitra), the giver of greater honour to

his friends: ‘The chariot is ready, O Lord,
kindly do the needful.’ (4-5)
