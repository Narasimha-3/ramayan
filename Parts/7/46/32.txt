
तस्यास्तद्‌ वचन श्रुत्वा WAST नयने शुभे।
नाविकानाहयामास लक्ष्मण: परवीरहा।
Ba स सज्जा नौश्वेति दाशाः प्रा्ललयोउब्रुवन्‌॥ ३२॥

Laksmana, the destroyer of the heroes
among foes, hearing these words of her, he
wiped his eyes dry summoned the boatmen.
The ferry-men with folded hands replied
thus: “This boat is already made to depart”

(32).
