
अथापराह्समये . भ्रातृभि: सह राघव:।
Bera मधुरां वाणीमन्तरिक्षान्महाप्रभु:॥ २॥
सौम्य राम निरीक्षस्व सौम्येन वदनेन art
कुबेरभवनातू wa विद्धि मां yeh प्रभो॥३॥
After sometime in the afternoon the

mighty Lord Sri Rama (a scion of Raghu),
heard, alongwith his brothers, a sweet voice
coming from the sky as follows: “Dear
Rama, look on me with affection beaming
from your face; know me, O Lord, to be the
Puspaka, come from the mansion of Kubera.
(2-3)
