लोकापवादो sera, येन त्यक्ता हि मैथिली।
eat लोकभयाद्‌ ब्रह्मन्नपापेत्यभिजानता।
परित्यक्ता मया सीता तद्‌ War श्षन्तुमहति॥ ४॥
‘The censure by people is great, due to
which Maithili (Sita) was forsaken. This Sita,
O Brahmana, was forsaken by me due to
fear of censure, although knowing that she
was sinless, so please forgive me. (4)
