Qt तदोत्सड्रगतं सदागते:।
चतुर्मुखो वीक्ष्य कृपामथाकरोत्‌

सदेवगन्धर्वऋषिय क्षराक्षसै: ॥ ६५७५॥

“Perceiving at that time the son of the
wind-god (who is perpetually in motion),
radiant as the sun, fire and gold, in his lap,
Brahma (the four-faced god) accompanied
by Gandharvas, Rsis (the seers of Vedic
Mantras), Yaksas and ogres, including gods,

thereupon immediately took pity on the
child.” (65)
