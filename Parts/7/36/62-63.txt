
राघवश्च तमेवार्थ चिन्तयामास fafera: |
ततोउस्तं भास्करे याते विसृज्य नृपवानरान्‌॥ ६२॥
संध्यामुपास्थ विधिवत्‌ तदा नरवरोत्तम:।
प्रवृत्तायां रजन्यां तु सोउन्तःपुरचरो5भवत्‌॥ ६३॥
Sri Rama too pondered in amazement
over that very subject of performing
sacrifices. Having seen off the assembled

kings and monkeys on the sun having set,
and performed the Sandhya devotions with
due ceremony, Sri Rama, the foremost of
jewels among men, for His part retired into

the gynaeceum when the night had set in.
(62-63)
