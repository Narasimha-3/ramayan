
ऋषिशापाहतबलस्तदैव कपिसत्तम:।

सिंह: कुझ्जररुद्बो वा आस्थितः सहितो रणे॥ ४३॥
“Deprived of the knowledge of his own

might by the curse of the sages, Hanuman,

the foremost of monkeys, remained standing
by the side of Sugriva during that very
period like a lion kept back by an elephant,
in the course of the latter's combat with
Vali. (43)
