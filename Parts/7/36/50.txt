स्त्वत्कारणाद्‌ राम सुरैर्हि WET: ५०॥
“Nay, alongwith the aforesaid leaders
of monkeys, Gaja, Gavaksa, Gavaya,
Sudamstra, Mainda, Prabha, Jyotimukha and
Nala—these bears too were actually

procreated by the gods for your sake only,
O Rama! (50)
