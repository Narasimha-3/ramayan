Then as the night appeared, Satrughna
asked the Brahmana, Cyavana, son of Bhrgu
the exact might of Lavana, and the power of
the trident also. He enquired, ‘O Brahmana,
who were killed by this special trident having
entered into a duel with Lavana.’ (1-2)
