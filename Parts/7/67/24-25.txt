By you performing this deed the welfare
of the worlds will be achieved. Thus all has
been narrated to you, O bull among men,
about the might of the wicked Lavana and
the unfathomable dreadful power of the trident,
O king Mandhata; Lavana perished due to
his own misdeeds. (24-25)
