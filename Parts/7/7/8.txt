शरैः पूर्णायतोत्सृष्टैर्वज़कल्पैर्मनोजवै: |
चिच्छेद विष्णुर्निशिति: शतशो5थ सहस्रश:॥ ८॥

By means of sharp arrows hard like
adamant and having the speed of the mind,
shot from the bow, whose string was pulled
to the extreme length, Visnu mangled to
pieces the Raksasas in their hundreds and
thousands. (8)
