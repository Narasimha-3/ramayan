तैरए्वै््राम्यते wed: सुमाली राक्षसेश्वर:।
इन्द्रियाइवै: परिभ्रान्तैर्शतिहीनो यथा नरः॥ ३०॥

Like the unsteady man who is tossed
this side and that by his fickle sense-organs,
which may be likened to horses, Sumali, the
lord of the Raksasas, was drawn this side
and that by those horses running helter-
skelter. (30)
