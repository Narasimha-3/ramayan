‘We shall not live without a king, This is
our supreme decision.’ Then Brahma, the
highest god, after calling the guardians of
regions alongwith Indra, said: ‘All of you
give me a portion of your authority.’ Then all
the guardians gave away a portion of their
power. (40-41)
