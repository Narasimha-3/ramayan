त्वं हि शक्तस्तारयितुं सेन्द्रागपि दिवौकसः॥ ३२॥
तस्मात्‌ प्रदास्थे विधिवत्‌ तत्‌ प्रतीच्छ नराधिप।
अथोवाच महात्मानमिक्ष्वाकूणां महारथः॥ ३३॥
रामो मतिमतां अ्रेष्ठ: क्षत्रधर्ममनुस्मरन्‌।
प्रतिग्रहोड्यं. भगवन्‌ ब्राह्मणस्थाविगर्हित: ॥ ३४॥

“You are worthy of wearing this
ornament. You indeed are capable of giving
precious gifts, and also protecting the gods
together with Indra. Therefore, duly | give
this to you, please accept it.”

The wisest among the wise, the great
charioteer of Iksvaku dynasty, Sri Rama
while aware of his Ksatriya duties, asked
the Mahatma: “Sir, accepting donation is not
prohibited only for Brahmanas. (32—34)
