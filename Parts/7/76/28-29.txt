उष्यतां de wet wert मम weal
प्रभाते पुष्पकेण a गन्तासि पुरमेव हि॥२८॥
त्वं हि नारायण: श्रीमांस्त्वयि सर्व प्रतिष्ठितम्‌।
त्वं प्रभुः सर्वदेवानां पुरुषस्त्व॑ सनातनः॥ २९॥
“Stay one night with me, O descendant
of Raghu, You are Narayana (the abode of
all souls), and all is established in You. You
are lord of all the gods, the eternal Purusa,
You may, indeed, go back to your city at
dawn. (28-29)
