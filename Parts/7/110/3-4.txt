
ay afer qed तु ब्रह्मा लोकपितामहः।
सर्व: परिवृतो देवैक्रषिभिश्चव महात्मभिः॥ ३॥
आययौ यत्र काकुत्स्थ: स्वर्गाय समुपस्थितः।
विमानशतकोटीभिर्दिव्याभिरभिसंवृतः ॥ ४॥

At that moment, Brahma, the grandfather
of the world, accompanied by all the gods,
high sages and noble ones, came to the
place where the scion of Kakutstha was
ready to depart to heaven. He, the scion
of Kakutstha, was surrounded by divine
chariots in millions. (3-4)
