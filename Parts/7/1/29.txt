
दिष्ट्या Weal महाबाहो कालस्थेवाभिधावत:।
मुक्त: सुररिपोर्वीर प्राप्तश्न॒ विजयस्त्वया॥ २९॥

“That you, O mighty-armed hero, obtained
release from the Nagapasa (noose of
snakes) of Indrajit, the enemy of gods, and
rushed at him like Yama, and got the better of
him, is really due to our good luck. (29)
