
तस्मादियं नरवरात्मज शुद्धभावा
दिव्येन दृष्टिविषयेण मया प्रविष्टा।
लोकापवादकलुषीकृतचेतसा या

त्यक्ता त्वया प्रियतमा विदितापि शुद्धा॥ २४॥

‘Therefore, this one 0! son of king
DaSaratha, forsakan by you for fear of public
censure, | have known her through divine
vision to be of pure conduct and devoted to

you even though you already knew that she
was chaste.’ (24)
