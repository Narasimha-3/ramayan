“O lovely lady, which desire of yours
should be fulfilled.” Then, with a smile, Vaidehi

replied to Sri Rama in the following words:
“O Raghava ! | wish to visit the holy penance-

groves and to stay O Lord! at the feet
of sages, endowed with an overwhelming
effulgence living on the banks of the Ganga,
and living only on fruits and edible roots.
This is my greatest wish that | should stay
even for one night in the penance-grove of
those who live only on fruits and edible
roots, O scion of Kakutstha!” “Be it so”,
thus it was promised by Sri Rama, who was
unwearied in action. He further said, “O
Vaidehi, rest assured, you will undoubtedly
go tomorrow.” (82—35)
