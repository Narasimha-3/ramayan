Hearing these words of Sri Rama and
His prolonged proclamation, the Rsis, who
were performing right penances and dwelling
on the banks of the Yamuna, arose. In great
joy the noble ones said: ‘O best among
men, this befits you alone in this world, and
none other. (15-16)
