
ततः ward विमले कृतपूर्वाह्निकक्रिय:।
अभिचक्राम काकुत्स्थो दर्शनं पौरकार्यवित्‌॥ २॥
After dawn in the morning, which was
a clear one, having performed the morning
duties, Sri Rama the scion of Kakutstha
approached the place where he could see
his subjects, desirous to know the activities
of the citizens. (2)
