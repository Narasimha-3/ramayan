सम्प्रवेशय वे क्षिप्रं कार्यार्थी योउत्र तिष्ठति॥ २८॥
‘O Sri Rama! listen to what | have to

say. O delighter of Kausalya! O long-armed
one! O lord! According to what you had

commanded, | called on those who have
come with some business. A dog, which
has come with a request is standing at your
palace-gate.’ After hearing Laksmana’s
speech, Sri Rama said: “Let it enter quickly,
the one standing there with some request.’
