On hearing this, Sri Rama sent the
gatekeeper, who brought the learned
Brahmana Sarvarthasiddha by name. Thus
the eminent Brahmana, endowed with
splendour seeing Sri Rama there asked
him, ‘O sinless Sri Rama! Tell me what do
you want me to do for you?’

Thus told by the Brahmana, Sri Rama
addressed the following words, ‘O Brahmana!
You hurt the dog. What ill did it do to you, for
which you struck it with a stick? Anger is a
deadly enemy, anger is a friend-faced enemy.
Anger is a highly sharp sword. Anger takes
away everything, destroys all virtues.
Whichever sacrifices a person performs and
whatever in charity he gives, all that he
destroys by anger. Therefore, one should
give up anger and control like a charioteer,
one’s senses, running forth towards their
objects like very wicked horses, after turning
them away from their objects (17—23)
