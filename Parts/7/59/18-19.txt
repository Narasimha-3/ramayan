एतच्छुत्वा च वचन सारमेयस्य लक्ष्मण:।
राघवाय तदाख्यातुं प्रविवेशालयं शुभम्‌॥ १८॥
निवेद्य रामस्य पुनर्निर्जगाम नृपालयात्‌।
वक्तव्यं यदि ते किंचित्‌ तत्त्व ब्रूहि ora Tu १९॥

On hearing the dog’s speech, Laksmana
entered the auspicious royal palace in order
to communicate the same to Sri Rama and
after informing Him, Laksmana came out of
the royal palace and said to the dog, “If you
have to say something, then tell the king
about it.” (18-19)
