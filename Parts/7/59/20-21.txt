Having heard Laksmana’s speech the

dog said, ‘Agni (the Fire-god), Indra, Surya
(the sun-god) and Vayu (the wind-god) are
present in the temples, the royal abode and
the houses of Brahmanas. O Laksmana!
we, of the lowest origin, are unfit to go there.
