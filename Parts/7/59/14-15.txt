धर्माच्छरण्यतां याति राजा सर्वभयापहः॥ १५॥

On this Sri Rama told the dog: “Tell me
fearlessly what | should do for you. Do not
delay,” On hearing Rama’s speech, the dog
addressed the follwoing words: ‘The king
should acquire territory by ‘Dharma’ and

should protect it by ‘Dharma.’ The king
comes to be recognized as a refuge and
dispels all fear. (14-15)
