
एष राजनू परो धर्म: फलवानू Wea राघव।
नहि धर्माद्‌ भवेत्‌ किंचिद्‌ दुष्प्रापमिति a ata: ue

“This act of ‘Dharana’ or sustaining is
‘Dharma’—this is the conclusion. O king Sti
Rama, this is the highest ‘Dharma’ which
yields reward after death. My view is that
nothing is unattainable by ‘Dharma’. (9)
