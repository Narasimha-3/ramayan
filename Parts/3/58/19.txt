विगर्हमाणोनुजमार्तरूप॑
क्षुधाश्रमेणेव पिपासया I
विनिःश्वसन्‌ शुष्कमुखो विषण्ण:
प्रतिश्रयं प्राप्प समीक्ष्य शून्यम्‌॥ १९॥
Severely reproaching his younger
brother, Laksmana, who wore a distressed
appearance, nay, heaving a deep breath,
Sri Rama, whose mouth was parched from
hunger, fatigue and thirst, grew despondent
to perceive the site of his hermitage desolate
on reaching it. (19)
