
स्वमाश्रमं॑ स॒प्रविगाह्य वीरो
विहारदेशाननुसृत्य कांश्ित्‌।

एतत्तदित्येव निवासभूमौ

yest व्यथितो बभूव॥२०॥

Directly entering his own hermitage,
nay, exploring some of Sita’s sporting
grounds and saying to himself that it was
the same place where she used to disport,
the aforesaid hero felt disquieted in his own
abode, his hair standing on end through
agony. (20)
