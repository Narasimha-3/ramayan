Severely reproaching his younger
brother, Laksmana, who wore a distressed
appearance, nay, heaving a deep breath,
Sri Rama, whose mouth was parched from
hunger, fatigue and thirst, grew despondent
to perceive the site of his hermitage desolate
on reaching it. (19)

Directly entering his own hermitage,
nay, exploring some of Sita’s sporting
grounds and saying to himself that it was
the same place where she used to disport,
the aforesaid hero felt disquieted in his own
abode, his hair standing on end through
agony. (20)
