
इदं ad बन्धुहितार्थिना मया

यथोच्यमानं यदि नाभिपत्स्यसे।
सबान्धवस्त्यक्ष्यसि जीवितं रणे

हतोउद्य रामेण शरैरजिहागैः॥ २५॥

“If you do not accept this advice being
tendered in right earnest by me, seeking the
welfare of my relative in your person, you
will, when pierced by Rama with the straight-
going arrows today, will give up the ghost
with your kith and kin on the field of battle.”

(25)
