Seeing that group of hermitages of
sages, adorned with highly blessed
Brahmanas, knowers of Brahma, the glorious
and highly lustrous Rama removed the string
of his great bow and entered into the
hermitages. Then, beholding Rama, those
great sages possessed of divine knowledge
approached him and the renowned Vaidehi
with pleasure. They saw him given to the
practice of virtue and charming like the
rising moon and also Laksmana and Vaidehi
of renown and, being firm observers of
vows, welcomed them performing auspicious
rites. The  forest-dwellers saw with
amazement the handsome build of the body,
grandeur, tenderness and ascetic dress of
Rama. (9—13)
