नगरस्थो वनस्थो वा त्वं नो राजा जनेश्वर:॥ २०॥

“It is hence that the king, who is saluted
by all, enjoys the foremost, delightful
pleasures. We being the residents of your
kingdom deserve protection by you. Whether
staying in the city or in the forest, you, the
lord of the people, are our king. (20)
