Canto I
Reception of Sri Rama, Laksmana and Sita in the hermitages of sages

Yaga तु महारण्यं दण्डकारण्यमात्मवान्‌।
wat ददर्श दुर्धर्षस्तापसाश्रममण्डलम्‌॥ १॥
Having entered the dense forest of
Dandaka, the self-controlled and unassailable
Sri Rama saw a group of hermitages of
sages. (1)
