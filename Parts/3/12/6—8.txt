Entering the sanctuary and joining his
palms, that favourite disciple of Agastya
reported at once the arrival of Rama, exactly
as requested by Laksmana, to the foremost
sage, unassailable through askesis: “The
yonder two sons of Dasaratha, Rama and
Laksmana, have entered the site of our
hermitage with Sita, the former’s wife. Those
vanquishers of foes have come to see
you in order to be of some service to you.
