सोत्साहता चास्तु विमार्गणेउस्या:।
उत्साहवन्तो हि नरा न लोके

सीदन्ति कर्मस्वतिदुष्करेषु॥ १९॥

“Casting off grief, take heart now. And
let vigorousness be brought to bear upon
the quest for Sita: for men who are full of
vigour never lose heart even when engaged
in pursuits which are most difficult to carry
through.” (19)
