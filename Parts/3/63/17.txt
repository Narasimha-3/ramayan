(Addressing the wind-god) “There is
nothing, absolutely nothing, in all the worlds,
which is not always known to you. Pray,
give me news about Sita—(that guardian
angel of my race)—whether she is dead or
has been borne away or is on her way to
some unknown destination, having been
carried away by some desperado.” (17)
