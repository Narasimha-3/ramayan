
निकृत्तपक्षं रुधिरावसिक्तं
a गृश्तराजं परिगृह्य राघव:।
aa मैथिली प्राणसमा गतेति
विमुच्य art निपपात भूमौ॥ २९॥

Embracing the aforesaid king of
vultures—whose wings had been lopped off
and who was lying bathed in blood—and
uttering the words “Where has the princess
of Mithila, who was dear to me 85 life,
gone?” Sri Rama (a scion of Raghu) sank
down on the earth. (29)
