विराजयन्ती तं देशं विद्युत्सौदामनी यथा॥ ३४॥
Casting herself into the fire when
spoken to as aforesaid and granted leave by
Sri Rama, Sabari, who wore matted locks on
her head and was clad in the bark of trees
and black deerskin before she leapt into the
fire, rose to the highest heaven alone, looking,
as she did, like a blazing fire : so the tradition
goes. Adorned with celestial jewels and
celestial garlands, daubed with heavenly
sandal-paste and clad in heavenly raiment,

she wore a pleasing aspect in that ethereal
form, illumining that region like a streak of
lightning flashing from a cloud. (32—34)
