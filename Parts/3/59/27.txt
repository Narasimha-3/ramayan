शराहतेनेव तदार्तया गिरा
स्वरं ममालम्ब्य सुदूरसुश्रवम्‌।
saad ag वचन सुदारुणं
त्वमागतो येन विहाय मैथिलीम्‌॥ २७॥

“Feigning my voice, that most poignant
cry, which could be easily heard at a long
distance, was uttered in plaintive tones by
the ogre the moment he was hit with the
shaft, led by which you came away leaving
Sita (the princess of Mithila) alone. (27)
