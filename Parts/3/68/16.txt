
Wat fastaa: साक्षाद्‌ भ्राता वैश्रवणस्यथ च।
इत्युक्त्वा दुर्लभान्‌ प्राणान्‌ मुमोच्च पतगेश्वर: ॥ १६॥
“He (Ravana) is a son of Sage Visrava
himself and a half-brother of Kubera, the
first-born son of ViSrava.” Saying so, Jatayu,
the king of birds, yielded up his life, which
was difficult to retain. (16)
