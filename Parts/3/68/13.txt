
बिन्दो नाम Hedi sat न च काकुत्स्थ सो5बुधत्‌।
त्वत्प्रियां जानकीं हत्वा रावणो राक्षसेश्वर:।
झषवद्‌ बडिशं qa क्षिप्रमेव विनश्यति॥ १३॥
“It was an hour ‘Vinda’ by name.
Ravana, however did not know it, O scion
of Kakutstha! Having borne away your
beloved spouse, Sita (daughter of Janaka),
Ravana, the king of ogres, will perish very
soon as a fish on swallowing a hook. (13)
