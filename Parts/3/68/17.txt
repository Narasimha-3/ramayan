त्यक्त्वा शरीरं YET प्राणा जम्मुर्विहायसम्‌॥ १७॥

In the presence of Sri Rama, who went
on saying: “Speak, speak (further) !” with
joined palms as a mark of respect to his
father’s friend, the life-breath of the vulture
rose to the skies leaving his body. (17)
