
कृताभिषेक: A रराज रामः

सीताद्वितीय:ः सह लक्ष्मणेन।
कृताभिषेकस्त्वगराजपुत्र्या

es: सनन्दिर्भगवानिवेश: ॥ ४३॥

Having finished his bath, Sr Rama
shone with Sita and Laksmana even as
Lord Siva (the Destroyer of the universe)
with Nandi (His beloved associate) and
Parvati (the Daughter of the lord of mountains)
on having concluded His bath in the Ganga.

(43)
