तामार्तरूपां विमना रुदन्तीं
सौमित्रिरालोक्य विशालनेत्राम्‌।

आश्वासयामास न चैव भर्तु-
et भ्रातरं किंचिदुवाच सीता॥ ३९॥

Depressed in spirit to perceive her
weeping with a distressed air, Laksmana,
son of Sumitra, consoled the large-eyed
lady. Sita, however, did not utter even a
word to her aforesaid brother-in-law. (39)
