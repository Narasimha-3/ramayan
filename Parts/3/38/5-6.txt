““A terrible calamity has arisen before
me through Marica, O ruler of men !’ Solicited
in the above words, on that occasion, King
DaSaratha, whose mind was set on piety,
replied as follows to the eminent and highly
blessed Sage ViSwamitra: ‘This scion of
Raghu is less than twelve* years of age
and has not yet practised archery. (5-6)
