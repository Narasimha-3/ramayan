Then, Rama of extraordinary lustre
spoke of his own Ikswaku dynasty to that
demon of flaming mouth, who was inquiring,
“Know us two to be Ksatriyas of character,
who have come to the forest. For our part,
we want to know you—who are you
wandering in the Dandaka forest?” (2-3)
