
तस्याभिप्रायमाज्ञाय Wa लक्ष्मणमत्रवीतू।
वहत्वयमलं तावतू पथानेन तु राक्षस:;॥ २२॥

यथा चेच्छति सौमित्रे तथा aed ware: |
अयमेव हि नः wer येन याति निशाचर:॥ २३॥
Reading his mind, Rama said to
Laksmana—‘Let this ogre gladly carry us
by this way, O son of Sumitra ! Let the ogre
carry us as he will. Indeed this is the path
of ours by which the night-wanderer is going.”
(22-23)
