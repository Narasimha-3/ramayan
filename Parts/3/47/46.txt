यदन्तरं काझ्ननसीसलोहयो-
यदन्तरं चन्दनवारिपड्डयो: |
हस्तिबिडालयोर्वने
तदन्तरं दाशरथेस्तवैव च॥ ४६॥
“Nay, the same disparity exists between
Sri Rama (son of Dagaratha) and you as
does between gold anda base metal like lead,
between sandal-paste and mud and between
an elephant and a cat in a jungle. (46)
