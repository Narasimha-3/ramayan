“Its rocky bed causes sand to be formed
on it and it remains ever adorned with lotuses
and lilies, O Rama ! Swimming on the water
of Pampa, lovely swans, ducks, Krauficas
and ospreys call in sweet tones there, O

scion of Raghu! Having not known
destruction before, they are not frightened
to see human beings. (12-13)
