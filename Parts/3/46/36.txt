इयं Get ब्राह्मण काममास्यता-
मिदं च पाद्यं प्रतिगृह्मयतामिति।
इदं च fee वनजातमुत्तमं
त्वदर्थमव्यग्रमिहोषभुज्यतामू ॥ ३६॥
“Here is a seat of green grass, 0
Brahmana ! Let yourself be seated at will on
it. Also let this water to wash your feet with
be accepted. And here is excellent produce
of the forest dressed for you. This may be
partaken of by you on this spot at ease.”
(36)
