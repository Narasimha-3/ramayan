समर्पयामास AMA रावण:॥ ३७॥

Being invited to dine in the aforesaid
words and closely observing Queen Sita,
the princess of Mithila, who had uttered
the full formula with which a guest is invited
to dine, Ravana fully and resolutely made
up his mind to carry her away by force
and thereby to bring about his own death.
