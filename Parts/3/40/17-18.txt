
श्रुणु तत्कर्म साहाय्ये यत्कार्य वचनान्मम।
सौवर्णस्त्वं मृगो भूत्वा चित्रो रजतबिन्दुभि:॥ १७॥
आश्रमे तस्य रामस्य सीताया: WAS चर।
प्रलोभयित्वा aed यथेष्टं गन्तुमहसि॥ १८ ॥

“Now hear of that role which has to be
played by you by way of co-operation at my
instance : turning into a golden deer freckled
with silvery spots, roam you in the hermitage
of the aforesaid Rama, before the eyes of
Sita. Having cast your full charm on her,
you may go wherever you please. (17-18)
