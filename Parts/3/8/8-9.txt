
अविषद्यातपो यावत्‌ gat नातिविराजते।
अमार्गेणागतां लक्ष्मी प्राप्येवान्वयवर्जित:॥ ८॥
तावदिच्छामहे गन्तुमित्युक्ता चरणौ मुनेः।
ववन्दे सहसौमित्रि: सीतया सह राघव:॥ ९॥

“Before the sun starts shining brightly
with unbearable heat, just as a man from a
low family becomes unbearable after getting
wealth earned through unfair means, we
desire to go.” Saying this, Raghava bowed
down at the feet of the sage, together with
Laksmana and Sita. (8-9)
