कच्चित्‌ सकुशलं राजँल्‍लोकानां राक्षसाधिप।
are नाधिजाने त्वं यतस्तूर्णमुपागतः ॥ ३८ ॥

“| hope all is pretty well with the
people in your kingdom, O monarch ! Since,
however, you have approached me in haste
| am full of apprehension and do not anticipate
that everything is quite well, O suzerain
Lord of ogres.” (38)
