
असाध्य:ः कुपितो रामो विक्रमेण महायशा:।
आपगायास्तु पूर्णाया ait परिहरेच्छरै:॥ २३॥
सतारग्रहनक्षत्रं नभश्चाप्यवसादयेत्‌।
असौ रामस्तु सीदन्तीं श्रीमानभ्युद्धरेन्महीम्‌॥ २४॥
“When enraged, the highly illustrious
Rama is incapable of being tamed. He can
with his arrows even stem the current of a
flooded river, and pull down even the vault
of heaven with its stars, planets and
constellations. That glorious Rama can even
lift up the sinking earth. (23-24)
