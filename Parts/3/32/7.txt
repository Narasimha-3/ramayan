देवासुरविमर्देषु बज्राशनिकृतब्रणम्‌।
ऐरावतविषाणाग्रैरुत्कृष्टकिणवक्षसम्‌ Won

He bore on his person scars of
wounds caused by the thunderbolt and
lightning in the course of conflicts between

the gods and the demons and his chest
was marked with callosities resulting
from abrasions caused by the tusks of
Airavata. (7)
