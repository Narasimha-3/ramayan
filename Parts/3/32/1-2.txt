
ततः शूर्पणखा gear सहस्त्राणि चतुर्दश।
हतान्येकेन रामेण रक्षसां भीमकर्मणाम्‌॥ १॥
दूषणं a खरं wa at fifi रणे।
दृष्ट्वा पुनर्मानादानू ननाद जलदोपमा॥ २॥
Then, seeing fourteen thousand ogres
of terrible deeds killed by Rama single-
handed and also perceiving Dusana, Khara
and Trisira too killed in battle, Surpanakha
uttered loud roars like a cloud. (1-2)
