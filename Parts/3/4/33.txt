प्रहष्टरूपाविव_ रामलक्ष्मणौ
विराधमुर्व्या प्रदेर निपात्य तम्‌।
ननन्दतुर्वीतभयौ महावने

शिलाभिरन्तर्दधतुश्च राक्षसम्‌॥ ३३॥
Appearing as though pleased on having
thrown that Viradha into a pit in the earth,
Rama and Laksmana rejoiced in the great
forest, their fear being removed, and covered
the ogre with rocks. (33)
