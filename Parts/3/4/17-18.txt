प्रसाद्ममानश्ष मया सोउब्रवीन्मां महायशा:।
यदा दाशरथी रामस्त्वां वधिष्यति संयुगे॥ १७॥
तदा प्रकृतिमापन्नो star स्वर्ग गमिष्यति।
अनुपस्थीयमानो मां स Heal व्याजहार TU Vel
“When requested for grace by me, he,
possessed of great fame, said to me, ‘When
Rama, son of DaSsaratha, will kill you in
battle, then regaining your pristine state you
will go to paradise.’ He, not being attended
upon by me, angrily addressed me thus.
(17-18)
