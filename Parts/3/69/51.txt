इति ब्रुवाणो दृढसत्यविक्रमो

महायशा दाशरथिः प्रतापवान्‌।
अवेक्ष्य सौमित्रिमुदग्रविक्रमः

स्थिरां तदा Fat मतिमात्मनाकरोत्‌॥ ५१॥

Looking at Laksmana (son of Sumitra),

who looked miserable, while speaking, as
aforesaid, the highly illustrious and glorious
Sri Rama (son of Daégaratha) who was
possessed of unflinching and unfailing
prowess and exalted valour, presently
collected his mind by himself. (51)
