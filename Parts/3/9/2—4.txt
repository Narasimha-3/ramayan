“In a very subtle way, a great man is
led to ‘Adharma’. If one keeps away from
addictions born of desire, it can be avoided
in this world. Indeed, the addiction born of
desire in this world are only three in number:
False speech is extremely bad; the other
two are even worse than that—copulation
with the wife of another and cruelty without
enmity. A false statement you have never
made nor will you ever make, O Raghava !
