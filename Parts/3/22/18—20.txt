
मुद्रैः पद्टिशै: शूलैः सुतीक्षणश्च परश्वधेः।
खडगैश्नक्रै श्व॒ हस्तस्थेरभ्राजमानै: सतोमरैः॥ १८॥
शक्तिभि: परिधै्धोरिरतिमात्रैश्चल कार्मुकै:।
गदासिमुसलैवर्ज्रैर्गृहीतैभी मदर्शनेः ॥१९॥
राक्षसानां सुघोराणां सहस्त्राण चतुर्दश।
निर्यातानि जनस्थानात्‌ खरचित्तानुवर्तिनामू्‌॥ २०॥

With clubs, PattiSas, darts, exceptionally
sharp axes, glittering swords, discuses held
in their hands alongwith iron clubs and also
with javelins, fearful clubs tipped with iron
and huge bows, maces, scimitars, Musalas
and terrible-looking thunderbolts seized by
them, all the fourteen thousand of the most
dreadful ogres, who followed the mind of Khara,
sallied forth from Janasthana. (18—20)
