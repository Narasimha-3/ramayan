प्रवृद्धमन्युस्तु खरः खरस्वरो
रिपोर्वधार्थ त्वरितो यथान्तक:।

अचूचुदत्‌ सारथिमुन्नदन्‌ पुन-
महाबलो मेघ इवाश्मवर्षवानू॥ २४॥

With his indignation risen high and
thundering like a cloud raining hail-stones,
the very mighty Khara, who was seized
with a flurry to kill the enemy and looked
like Death, urged on his charioteer once
more in a sharp tone. (24)
