
चतुर्दशश सहस्त्राणि मम चित्तानुवर्तिनाम्‌।
रक्षतमां भीमवेगानां. समरेष्वनिवर्तिनाम्‌॥ ८॥
नीलजीमूतवर्णानां लोकहिंसाविहारिणाम्‌ ।
सर्वोद्योगमुदीर्णानां eat सौम्य कारय॥ ९॥

“O gentle one, get all the fourteen
thousand ogres who follow my mind, who
are possessed of terrible impetuosity, who
never restreat from the fields of battle, who
possess the hue of dark blue clouds, who
divert themselves by indulging in carnage
and are full of martial zeal, to exert in every
way for war. (8-9)
