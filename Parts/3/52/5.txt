
त्राहि मामद्य काकुत्स्थ लक्ष्मणेति वराड्रना।
Gare समाक्रन्दच्छुण्वतां तु यथान्तिके॥ ५॥

“Deliver me today, 0 Rama, a scion of
Kakutstha, and Laksmana!” So did Sita (a
lovely lady), who was greatly alarmed, cry
bitterly at such a pitch that those listening at
close quarters at least might hear her. (5)
