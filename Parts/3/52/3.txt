
न नूनं राम जानासि महदव्यसनमात्मनः।
धावन्ति नूनं काकुत्स्थ मदर्थ मृगपश्षिण:॥ ३॥
“Surely, O Rama, you do not perceive
the great calamity in the shape of my
abduction which has befallen you, although
deer and birds are moving in a direction
foreboding evil in order to indicate my
abduction, O scion of Kakutstha ! (3)
