
उत्पातवाताभिरता नानाद्विजगणायुता: l
मा भेरिति विधूताग्रा व्याजहरिव पादपा:॥ ३४॥
Violently shaken by a portentous blast

(released by the flight of Ravana) and
inhabited by birds of every description, the
trees with their topmost branches set into
motion said (to Sita) as it were: “Don’t be
afraid |” (34)
