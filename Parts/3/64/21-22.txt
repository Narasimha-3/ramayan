साधु गच्छावहे देव दिशमेतां च Aad २२॥
Like one distressed, the wise Laksmana

submitted to his eldest brother, Sri Rama,

as follows: “Inasmuch as, having sprung

on their feet all at once when accosted by

you in the words: ‘Where is Sita?’ These
deer pointed to the vault of heaven as well
as to the southern quarter, we had better
move in this south-westerly direction, which
is presided over by ogres headed by Nirrti,
my lord! (21-22)
