
भक्षितायां हि deat हतायामपि लक्ष्मण।
के हि लोके प्रियं कर्तु शक्ता: सौम्य ममेश्वरा: ॥ ५३॥

“When even Sita (a princess of the
Videha kingdom) has been devoured or
borne away for that matter, what powerful
beings in this world are really capable of
rendering good offices to me, O gentle
Laksmana? (53)
