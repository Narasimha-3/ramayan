
हता मृता वा deat भक्षिता वा तपस्विनी।
a tered सीतां हियमाणां महावने॥ ५२॥

“Poor Sita (a princess of the Videha
territory) has been killed or devoured
by ogres or is dead through agony of
separation from me. Even virtue was not
able to protect Sita while she was being
carried away by ogres in the great forest !

(52)
