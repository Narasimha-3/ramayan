कर्तारमपि लोकानां yt करुणवेदिनम्‌।
अज्ञानादवमन्येरनू_ सर्वभूतानि लक्ष्मण॥ ५४॥

“All living beings, 0 Laksmana, would
through ignorance of His power hold in
contempt even Lord Siva, the maker,
protector and destroyer of the worlds, though
valiant in the extreme, if He remains looking
on with compassion without meddling with
the affairs of the world. (54)
