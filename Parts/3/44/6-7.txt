Now it appeared seized with fear of
being hurt by Sri Rama’s arrow, utterly
confused and leaping in the air; while at
other times it disappeared into some depths
of the forest like the orb of the autumnal
moon screened by fragmented clouds. Just
after a while it became visible at hand and
again flashed into view at a distance the
very next moment. (6-7)
