
क्वचिन्मत्त इवाभाति कान्तान्वेषणतत्पर:।

a वनानि नदीः शैलानू गिरिप्रस्नवणानि a
काननानि चर वेगेन भ्रमत्यपरिसंस्थित:॥ ३७॥

Intent on finding out his darling, he now
appeared as though drunk and (now) he
would range with rapidity forests, rivers,
mountains, cataracts falling from mountains
and woodlands too, knowing no rest. (37)
