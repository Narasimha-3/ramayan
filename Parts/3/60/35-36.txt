“Ah, mighty-armed Laksmana, do you
perceive my darling anywhere? Ah Sita, my
darling, where have you gone, O blessed
one?” Wailing in these words again and
again and running about from grove to grove,
Sri Rama now sprang up with impetuosity
and now violently revolved like a whirlwind.
