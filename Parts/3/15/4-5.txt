रमते यत्र वैदेही त्वमह॑ Ba लक्ष्मण।
तादृशो दृश्यतां देशः संनिकृष्टजलाशयः॥ ४॥
वनरामण्यक॑ यत्र जलरामण्यक॑ तथा।
संनिकृष्टं च यस्मिस्तु समित्पुष्पकुशोदकम्‌॥ ५॥
“Look about for a site with a pool nearby,
in which the princess of Videha, yourself and
| will feel delighted, where there is beauty of
forest and beauty of water, and also in the
vicinity of which there are firewood, flowers,
the holy Kuga grass and water.” (4-5)
