मृतायामपि बैदेह्ाां नष्टायामपि राघव।
शोचितुं Aa वीर BMA: प्राकृतस्तथा॥ १४॥
“Even in the event of Sita (a princess of
the Videha territory) being dead or having
disappeared as a result of being carried away
by someone, O heroic Rama (a scion of
Raghu), you ought not to grieve in the same
way as any other common man. (14)
