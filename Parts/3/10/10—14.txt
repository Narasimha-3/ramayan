“Coming together, all of them made the
following submission : ‘We have been greatly
tormented in the Dandaka forest by many
ogres assuming different forms according
to their will. Rama, you protect us from
them. When the time comes for pouring
oblations into the sacred fire and also on
holy occasions, the flesh-eating, unassailable
ogres attack us, O sinless one ! You are
the greatest asylum for us poor ascetics,
who are seeking an asylum, assailed as we
are by the ogres. Although we are able to
kill the night-wanderers by virtue of our
austerities, we do not desire to waste the
power accumulated by us for a long time. O
Raghava, austerities are beset with many
obstacles and are always difficult to be
performed. (10—14)
