“Nor are we aware of his home or even
of his might. It is but becoming of you to
show compassion to us, who are helplessly
knocking about here and there in this way,
stricken as we are with grief at the loss
of Sita, and behaving with benevolence
towards all. Fetching dry logs of wood broken
by elephants, we shall in course of time
cremate you, 0 valiant ogre, after throwing
you in an extensive pit dug by us. While being
cremated by us as aforesaid, furnish to us
detailed information about Sita as to by whom
she has been borne away and where she
has been kept. (23—25)
