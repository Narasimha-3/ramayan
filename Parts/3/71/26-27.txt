
कुरु कल्याणमत्यर्थ यदि जानासि तत्त्वत:।
एवमुक्तस्तु रामेण वाक्य दनुरनुत्तमम्‌॥ २६॥
Wart कुशलो वक्ता वक्तारमपि राघवम्‌।
दिव्यमस्ति न मे ज्ञानं नाभिजानामि मैथिलीम्‌॥ २७॥

“Render this great service to us if you
know the thing correctly.” Requested in these
words by Sri Rama, the demon for his part,
who was an eloquent speaker, addressed the
following most excellent reply to Sr! Rama
(a scion of Raghu), who too was an orator :
“| have no divine knowledge, nor am | aware
of Sita, the princess of Mithila. (26-27)
