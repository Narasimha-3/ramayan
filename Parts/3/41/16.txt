
ded काकतालीयं घोरमासादितं मया।
अन्न त्वं शोचनीयोउसि ससैन्यो विनशिष्यसि॥ १६॥

“Although this terrible and unforeseen
calamity in the form of death at your hands
has been met with by me, nevertheless on
this score you alone deserve to be pitied

and not I, inasmuch as you are going to
meet your doom with your army. (16)
