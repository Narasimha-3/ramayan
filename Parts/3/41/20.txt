
निवार्यमाणस्तु मया हितैषिणा

न मृष्यसे वाक्यमिदं निशाचर।
परेतकल्पा हि गतायुषो नरा

fed न Tera सुहद्धिरीरितम्‌॥ २०॥

“You do not heed this counsel of mine,
though being checked by me, your well-
wisher, O ranger of the night! For men
whose span of life has come to an end and
who are on the verge of death do not
accept the salutary advice tendered by their
selfless friends. (20)
