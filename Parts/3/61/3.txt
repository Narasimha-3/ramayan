क्व नु लक्ष्मण बैदेही क॑ वा देशमितो गता।
केनाहता वा सौमित्रे भक्षिता केन वा प्रिया॥ ३॥

“Where on earth, 0 Laksmana, could

Sita, a princess of the Videha kingdom, be?
Nay, to what territory has she gone from
this place? Or, by whom, O darling of Sumitra,
has she been borne away or by whom has
she been devoured? (3)
