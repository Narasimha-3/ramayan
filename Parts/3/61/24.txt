
प्राप्स्यसे त्वं महाप्राज्ञ मैथिलीं जनकात्मजाम्‌।
यथा विष्णुर्महाबाहुर्बलिं बद्ध्वा महीमिमाम्‌॥ २४॥
“You will recover Sita (a princess of
Mithila), Janaka’s daughter, O_ highly
sagacious prince, even as the mighty-armed
Lord Visnu, in the form of the Divine Dwarf,
got back this globe for his elder brother,
Indra, after tying down Bali as a captive.”
(24)
