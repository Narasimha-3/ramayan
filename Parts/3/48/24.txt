जीवेच्चिर॑ ager पश्चा-

च्छचीं प्रधृष्याप्रतिरूपरूपाम्‌।
न मादृशीं राक्षस धर्षयित्वा
पीतामृतस्यापि तवास्ति मोक्ष: ॥ २४॥

“Having laid one’s hands on Saci, the
consort of Indra, the wielder of the
thunderbolt, of peerless beauty, one might
survive long, but on treating with indignity a
woman like me there is no escape from

death for you even though you have quaffed
the drink of immortality, O ogre ! (24)
