
स॒ मेरूश्रृड्जाग्रगतामनिन्दितां

प्रविश्य पातालतले5पि वाशञ्निताम्‌।
प्लवड्रमानामृषभस्तव॒ प्रियां
Pea रक्षांसि पुनः प्रदास्यति॥ २७॥
“Having entered his abode and

exterminated the ogres, that jewel among
monkeys will restore to you your beloved
spouse, who is beyond reproach, even if
she has reached the summit of Mount
Meru or has found place in the depths of
Patala (the nethermost subterranean region).”

(27)
