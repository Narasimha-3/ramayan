इत्येवमुक्तता मदनाभिपीडितः
स लक्ष्मणं AMAA: |
विवेश पम्पां नलिनीमनोरमां
तमुत्तमं शोकमुदीरयाणः ॥ २९॥
Having spoken in these words to
Laksmana and giving vent to that foremost
grief of his, Sri Rama, who was deeply
tormented with love and whose mind was
exclusively fixed on her, entered Pampa,
which ravished the soul by its lotuses. (29)
