
सप्तानां च समुद्राणां तेषां तीर्थेषु लक्ष्मण।
उपस्पृष्ट a विधिवत्‌ पितरश्चापि तर्पिता:॥ ४॥

“A bath has been taken with due
ceremony in the sacred water of all the
seven oceans girding the earth, drawn by
them in a miniature form, O Laksmana, and
the manes too have been propitiated with
offerings of water. (4)
