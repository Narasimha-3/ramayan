Penetrating deep into the residence of
Ravana (the suzerain lord of ogres), even
as the sun would disappear into a mass of
clouds, he saw from afar his elder brother,
Ravana, seated on his throne, even as
Indra would behold Brahma (the self-born
creator) sitting on his seat. (4)
