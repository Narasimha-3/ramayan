
देहीत्यन्यो ददात्यन्यो ददामीत्यपर: पुनः।
fe क्लेशयसि तिष्ठेति तत्रान्योन्यं बभाषिरे॥ ६६॥

“Give battle to me,” said one, on which
the other offered to fight with him; while
still another intervened, saying: “I offer
resistance to him, tarry; why do you subject
yourself to unnecessary suffering?” So did
the warriors of the two contending armies
spoke to one another in the streets of Lanka.
(66)
