
अथ हरिवरनाथ:ः प्राप्तसंग्रामकीर्ति-
निशिचरपतिमाजौ योजयित्वा श्रमेण।

गगनमतिविशालं लड्डयित्वार्कसूनु-
ईरिगणबलमध्ये रामपाएवँ जगाम॥ २९॥

Reducing Ravana (the lord of ogres)
to exhaustion in combat and traversing
the very extensive sky, Sugriva, a son of

the sun-god, the lord of monkey-chiefs, who
had won renown in combats, now arrived
by the side of Sri Rama in the midst of the
army of monkey battalions. (29)
