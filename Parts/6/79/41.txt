रजनिचरं fed Guest तमू।
प्रददृशुरथ देवता: प्रहृष्टा

गिरिमिव aged यथा विकीर्णम्‌॥४१॥

Highly rejoiced, the gods present at
the scene watched the aforesaid night-ranger,
the son of Khara, killed by the force of the
shafts of Sri Rama (son of King Dagaratha),
and resembling a mountain struck by
lightning and shattered. (41)
