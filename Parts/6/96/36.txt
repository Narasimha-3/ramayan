विनाशितं प्रेक्ष्य विरूपनेत्र

महाबलं तं हरिपार्थिवेन।

aad कपिराक्षसाना-

मुद्वृत्तगड़ाप्रतिमं बभूव॥ ३६॥
Perceiving the notorious Virupaksa,
who was endowed with extrordinary might,
made short work of by Sugriva, the king
of monkeys, the united army of the
monkeys and the ogres looked like the
holy Ganga in spate. (36)
