
तमब्रवीत्‌ ततो राजा रावणो राक्षसं स्थितम्‌।
विद्युज्जिहं vate समीपपरिवर्तिनम्‌॥ ४०॥
अग्रतः HS सीताया: शीघ्र दाशरथे: शिरः।
अवस्थां पश्चिमां भर्तु: कृपणा साधु पश्यतु॥ ४१॥

King Ravana thereupon spoke as follows
to the said ogre, Vidyujjihva, who had a long
tongue and stood nearby: “Place at once in
front of Sita the head of Rama (son of
DaSaratha). Let the poor woman clearly
behold the ultimate condition of her husband.”

(40-41)
