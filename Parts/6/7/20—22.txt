रुद्रादित्यमहाग्राहं मरुदवसुमहोरगम्‌।
रथाश्वगजतोयौघं पदातिपुलिनं महतू॥ २१॥

ama हि समासाद्य देवानां बलसागरम्‌।
गृहीतो दैवतपतिर्लड्रां चापि प्रवेशित:॥ २२॥
“After assailing the sea in the form of

the celestial army with javelins and spears
for its fish and entrails thrown asunder for
duck-weeds, nay, full of turtles in the form
of elephants, teeming with frogs in the form
of horses, infested with alligators in the
form of the eleven Rudras (gods of
destruction) and twelve Adityas (sons of
Aditi), and with huge serpents in the form of
the forty-nine Maruts (wind-gods) and the
eight Vasus, and having chariots, horses
and elephants for its volume of water and
foot-soldiers for its sandy beach, Indra,
the ruler of gods, was actually captured
by him and thrust into Lanka as a detenu.
(20—22)
