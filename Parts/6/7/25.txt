
राजन्नापदयुक्तेयमागता . प्राकृताज्जनातू।
ae नैव त्वया कार्या त्वं वधिष्यसि राघवम्‌॥ २५॥

“This adversity, which has come from
ordinary beings (like human beings and
monkeys), O king, does not deserve your
concern. It should not at all be given any
corner in your heart; for, you shall kill Rama
without doubt.” (25)
