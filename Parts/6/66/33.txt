ऋषभशरभमैन्दधूप्रनीला:

कुमुदसुषेणगवाक्षरम्भतारा: \
द्विविदपनसवायुपुत्रमुख्या-
स्त्वरिततराभिमुखं रणं प्रयाता:॥ ३३॥

Rsabha, Sarabha, Mainda, Dhumra and
Nila, Kumuda, Susena, Gavaksa, Rambha
and Tara headed by Dwivida, Panasa
and Hanuman (son of the wind-god),
marched ahead to the battle-field with
quicker steps. (33)
