“The moment his name is mentioned,
my wrath is aroused towards that vile ogre,
Ravana, by whom neither was virtue rightly
understood, nor morality, nor was pedigree
taken into consideration, but by whom the
aforesaid reproachful act was perpetrated
as prompted by his mean ogre-like mentality.
| shall perceive with my own eyes the
extermination of all the ogres due to the
crime of that mean fellow. (5-6)
