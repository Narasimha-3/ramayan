
wa: a रामो हरिवाहिनीपति-
विभीषणेन प्रतिनन्द सत्कृतः।
सलक्ष्मणो यूथपयूथसंयुतः

सुवेलपृष्ठे न्‍्यवसद्‌ यथासुखम्‌॥ २०॥

Honoured by Vibhisana after greeting
him joyfully, nay, accompanied by Laksmana
and joined by a company of troop-
commanders, the celebrated Sri Rama

thenceforward sojourned happily on the top
of the Suvela mountain. (20)
