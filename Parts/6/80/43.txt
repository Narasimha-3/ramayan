Set महात्मा त्वरितं निरीक्षते॥ ४३॥

Having made the foregoing speech,

which was full of great significance, the
high-souled Sri Rama, the foremost hero of
the Raghus, who was surrounded by the
foremost of the monkeys, now began to
reflect on the speedy means of putting an
end to the fierce ogre of cruel deeds. (43)
