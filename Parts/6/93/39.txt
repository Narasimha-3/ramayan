Fea oat राक्षसराजवाहिनीं

रामस्तदा शक्रसमो महात्मा।
अस्त्रेष. शस्त्रेषु. जितक्लमश्च

dea देवगणैः Wee ॥ ३९॥

Having destroyed that army of Ravana,
the king of ogres, the high-souled Sri Rama,
a compeer of Indra (the ruler of gods), who
had conquered fatigue incidental to the use
of missiles and weapons, began to be extolled
by the celestial hosts, who were feeling
exceedingly jubilant. (39)
