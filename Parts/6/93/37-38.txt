On that occasion Sri Rama too, whose
mind was set on virtue, remarked to Sugriva,
who stood close by, as also to Vibhisana
and to the monkey, Hanuman, Jambavan,
the king of bears, Mainda, the foremost of
monkeys, and Dwivida too: “Such wonderful
power of using the mystic missiles exists
either in me, who am no other than Lord
Visnu, or in Lord Siva, (the three-eyed

Divinity).” (37-38)
