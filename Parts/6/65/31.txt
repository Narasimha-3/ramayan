
सर्वाभरणसर्वाड्: शूलपाणि: स राक्षस:।
त्रिविक्रमकृतोत्साहे नारायण इवाबभौ॥ ३१॥

Decked all over his limbs with all kinds
of jewels and pike in hand, that ogre trod
like Lord Narayana, (in His all-embracing
form) determined to take the three long
strides which were intended to cover the
entire universe. (31)
