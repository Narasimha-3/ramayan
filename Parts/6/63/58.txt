चिराय सीता वशगा भविष्यति॥ ५८॥
“Revel, 0 king, and drink wine today.
Nay, banishing agony, discharge your duties.
When Rama is dispatched by me to the
abode of Yama, the god of retribution, today,
Sita will submit to your will for long.” (58)
