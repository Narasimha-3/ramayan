आदित्यं पातयिष्यामि सनक्षत्र॑ महीतले।
शतक्रतुं वधिष्यामि पास्थामि वरुणालयम्‌॥ ५४॥
“| shall hurl down on the earth the sun
alongwith the constellations. Nay, | shall kill
Indra (who is presupposed to have performed
a hundred horse-sacrifices in his previous
birth or births as a condition precedent for
his attaining Indrahood) and drink up the
ocean (the abode of Varuna, the god of
water). (54)
