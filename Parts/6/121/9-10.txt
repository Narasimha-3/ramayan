“May prosperity attend you! There is a
heavenly and excellent aerial car, Puspaka
by name, brilliant as the sun and belonging
to my brother, Kubera, the ruler of Yaksas,
which was borne away by the very mighty
Ravana after conquering the former in combat
and which moves according to the will of its
rider. Nay, having been retained for your
sake, it still continues with me, O warrior of
unequalled prowess! (9-10)
