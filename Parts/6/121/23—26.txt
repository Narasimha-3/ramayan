एवमुक्तस्तु रामेण राक्षसेन्द्रो विभीषण:।
विमान सूर्यसंकाशमाजुहाव त्वरान्वित:॥ २३॥
aa: raring वैदूर्यमणिवेदिकम्‌।
erm: परिक्षिप्तं सर्वती रजतप्रभम्‌॥ २४॥
पाण्डुराभि: पताकाभिध्वजैश्व समलंकृतम्‌।
शोभितं काझ्नेईम्यैहेमपदाविभूषिते: ॥ २५॥
प्रकीर्ण किड्डिणीजालैर्मुक्तामणिगवाक्षकम्‌।
averse: परिक्षिप्तं सर्वती मधुरस्वनम्‌॥ २६॥

Requested in these words by Sri Rama,
Vibhisana, the ruler of ogres, for his part
thereupon hastily invoked the presence of
the aerial car, Puspaka, brilliant as the sun,
whose parts were all made of gold and
were picturesque, and seats made of cat’s-
eye gems; which was full of attics and
shone like silver on all sides; which was
artistically decorated with whitish yellow
pennons and flags with armorial bearings
and graced with golden mansions adorned
with lotuses of gold; which was hung with
networks of tiny bells and contained eye-
holes set with pearls and was surrounded
on all sides with rows of bells giving forth a
melodious sound. (23—26)
