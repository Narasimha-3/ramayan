
अतिकायो5तितेजस्वी राक्षसेन्द्रसुतस्तदा।
आरुरोह रथश्रेष्ठं श्रेष्ठ: सर्वधनुष्मतामू्‌॥ २५॥
सुचक्राक्ष॑ सुसंयुक्ते स्वनुकर्ष सुकूबरम्‌।
तूणीबाणासनैर्दीप्तं. प्रासासिपरिघाकुलम्‌॥ २६॥
Then Atikaya another son of Ravana
(the ruler of ogres), the foremost of all
archers, who was full of exceeding martial
ardour, mounted his superb chariot with
excellent wheels and axles, magnificent
horses, strong Anukarsas (axle-trees) and
stout Kubaras (poles to which the yoke is
fixed), nay, radiant with quivers and bows
and fully furnished with spears, swords and
clubs studded or tipped with iron. (25-26)
