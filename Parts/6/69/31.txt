देवान्तकः समादाय परिघं हेमभूषणम्‌।
परिगृह्य fat deat वुर्विष्णोर्विडम्बयन्‌॥ ३१॥
Firmly grasping his club tipped with
iron and decked with gold, Devantaka stood
resembling the figure of Lord Visnu holding
(as he did) the Mandara mountain in His
arms while proceeding to churn the ocean
of milk for drawing out nectar. (31)
