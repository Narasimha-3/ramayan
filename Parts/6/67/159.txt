समुद्यतं पन्‍नगभोगकल्पम्‌।
ऐन्द्रास्त्रयुक्ते. जघान रामो

बाणेन जाम्बूनदचित्रितेन॥ १५९॥

With an arrow decked with gold and
charged with a mystic missile presided ever
by Indra (the ruler of gods), Sri Rama
severed his uplifted arm, which held the
palmyra tree and looked like the coils of a
serpent. (159)
