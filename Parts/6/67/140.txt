
जिह्नया परिलिहान्तं सृक्किणी शोणितोक्षिते।
Wed वानरानीक॑ कालान्तकयमोपमम्‌॥ १४०॥

With his tongue he was licking the
corners of his mouth, which were wet with
blood, and was crushing the army of
monkeys even like Yama (the god of
retribution) figuring as Death at the time of
universal dissolution. (140)
