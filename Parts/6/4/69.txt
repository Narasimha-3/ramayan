
प्रहृष्टमुदिता: सर्वे सुग्रीवेणाभिपालिता: |
वानरास्त्वरिता यान्ति सर्वे युद्धाभिनन्दिन:।
प्रमोक्षयिषव: सीतां मुहूर्त क्वापषि नावसन्‌॥ ६९॥
The monkeys, who all felt highly rejoiced
and cheerful, protected as they were on all
sides by Sugriva, and who all welcomed
war, eager as they were to get Sita liberated,
walked with quick steps and did not linger
anywhere even for an hour. (69)
