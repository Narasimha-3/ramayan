अधिकं शैलराजस्तु धातुभिस्तु विभूषितः।
धातुभ्य: प्रसृतो रेणुर्वायुवेगेन घट्टित:॥ ७६॥
सुमहद्वानरानीक छादयामास॒ सर्वतः।
गिरिप्रस्थेषु रम्येषु सर्वतः सम्प्रपुष्पिता:॥ ७७॥
केतक्यः सिन्दुवाराश्च वासन्त्यश्व मनोरमा:।
माधव्यो गन्धपूर्णाश्च कुन्दगुल्माश्व पुष्पिता: ॥ ७८ ॥

Richly adorned with minerals, the lord
of mountains for its part shone all the brighter.
The dust raised from the minerals and brought
together by the wind covered the huge army
of monkeys on all sides. Clothed with
blossom on all sides on the lovely plateaux,
the Ketaki and Sinduvara trees and Vasanti
creepers ravished the soul. The Madhavi
creepers were charged with odour, while
the Kunda (jasmine) shrubs were in blossom.

(76—78)
