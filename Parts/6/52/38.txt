मुदमगमत्‌ कपिभि: सुपूज्यमान:॥ ३८॥

Having exterminated his enemies
and causing streams of blood to flow, that
high-souled son of the wind-god, who
had been exhausted with the slaughter
of enemies, experienced joy while being
warmly acclaimed by the fellow monkeys.
