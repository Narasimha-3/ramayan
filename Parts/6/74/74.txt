सर्वे विशल्या fae: क्षणेन

हरिप्रवीराश्ष wags ये yl
गन्धेन तासां प्रवरौषधीनां

सुप्ता निशान्तेष्विव सम्प्रबुद्धा:॥ ७४॥

Nay, even those eminent heroes among
the monkeys, who had been killed, were
healed of their wounds and rid of their pain
in an instant by inhaling the fragrance of
these most excellent herbs even as those
who are asleep would feel when fully awake
at the close of night. (74)
