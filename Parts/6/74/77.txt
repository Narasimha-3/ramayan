Thereupon the monkey, Hanuman,
sprung from the loins of the wind-god (lit.,
the wafter of odour) for his part, who was
endowed with tremendous speed, bore the
aforesaid mountain, full of medicinal simples,
with all speed right to the Himalaya mountain,
and joined Sri Rama once more. (77)
