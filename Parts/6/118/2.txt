
विधूयाथ चितां at तु वैदेहीं हव्यवाहन:।
उत्तस्थौ मूर्तिमानाशु गृहीत्वा जनकात्मजाम्‌॥ २॥

Scattering that funeral pile, and taking
the aforesaid daughter of Janaka, a princess
of the Videha territory, in his arms, the god
of fire (lit., the bearer of sacrificial offerings)
for his part forthwith rose at once from the

fire in a corporeal form. (2)
