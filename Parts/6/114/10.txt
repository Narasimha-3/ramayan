
दिव्याड्रागा वैदेहि दिव्याभरणभूषिता।
यानमारोह भद्रं ते भर्ता wat द्रष्ट्र्मिच्छति॥ १०॥

“Having taken your bath, nay anointed
with heavenly cosmetics and adorned with
celestial jewels, pray, mount the palanquin.
May prosperity attend you. Your husband
desires to see you, O princess of the Videha
territory!” (10)
