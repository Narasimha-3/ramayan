
पूर्वकातू प्रत्ययाच्चाहमुक्तो विश्वस्तवा तया।
द्रष्ट््मिच्छामि. भर्तारमिति पर्याकुलेक्षणा॥ ४॥

“By her, who was full of trust in me
because of the confidence which had been
inspired by me on a former occasion, | was
spoken to in these words, ‘I desire to see
my husband’, and her eyes grew wild with
emotion.” (4)
