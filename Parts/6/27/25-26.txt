यो गड्जामनुपर्येति त्रासबन्‌ गजयूथपान्‌।
हस्तिनां वानराणां च॒ पूर्ववैरमनुस्मरन्‌॥ २५॥
एष यूथपतिरनेता गर्जनू गिरिगुहाशयः।
गजान्‌ रोधयते वन्यानारुजंश्व महीरुहान्‌॥ २६॥

“Thundering and uprooting trees, this
commander and leader of monkeys—who,
remembering, as he does, the old antagonism
between elephants and monkeys, roams
along the banks of the Ganga, sowing terror
among the leaders of herds of elephants,
and dwells in mountain caves—obstructs
thereby the passage of wild elephants.

(25-26)
