प्रगृहीता: प्रकाशन्ते gett मरीचय:।
पृथिव्यां चानुकृष्यन्ते हहो नामैष aT st
“This monkey of terrible deeds is known
by the name of Hara. The glossy hairs
adhering to his long tail, which are coppery,
yellow, brown and white and of great length,
shine brightly like the sun’s rays, standing
erect as they do, and, being thrown about,
are dragged along the ground in his train.
(2-3)
