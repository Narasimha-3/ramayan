सर्वे महाराज महाप्रभावा:

सर्वे महाशैलनिकाशकाया:।
सर्वे समर्था: पृथिवीं क्षणेन

कर्तु प्रविध्वस्तविकीर्णशैलाम्‌॥ ४८ ॥

“All the aforesaid monkeys are endowed
with extraordinary might. All have bodies
resembling big mountains and all are capable
of levelling in a trice the earth with its

mountains razed to the ground and scattered,
O great king!” (48)
