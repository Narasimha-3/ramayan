at परिययौ लड्ढडां सर्वान्‌ गुल्मानवेक्षितुम्‌॥ २॥
Having reflected awhile, and then
deliberated with his counsellors, Ravana,
the suzerain lord of ogres, for his part
then actually proceeded in the forenoon to
the city of Lanka in order to inspect all

the fortified posts. (2)
