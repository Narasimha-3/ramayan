Hanuman too, as well as all the aforesaid
monkeys (lit., the denizens of the woods),
the celebrated and valiant Laksmana, who
was endowed with extraordinary energy,
thereupon speedily returned from the field
of battle to the spot where Sugriva and Sri
Rama (a scion of Raghu) were, leaning on
Vibhisana and Hanuman. (2-3)
