
तदैव रामः प्लवगाधिपस्तथा
विभीषणश्चर्क्षपतिश्च वीर्यवान्‌।
अवेक्ष्य सौमित्रिमरोगमुत्थितं
मुदा ween: सुचिरं जहर्षिरि॥ २८॥
On that very occasion Sri Rama as well
as Sugriva, the suzerain lord of monkeys, as
also Vibhisana and the valiant Jambavan,
the lord of bears, with their troops rejoiced for
a pretty long time on beholding Laksmana,

sound, active and full of joy. (28)
