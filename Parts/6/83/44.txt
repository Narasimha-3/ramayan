“This submission has been made by
me to win your pleasure. Enraged to learn
about the destruction of Janaka’s daughter,
| shall completely destroy with my arrows
Lanka with its chariots, elephants and horses,
alongwith Ravana, the ruler of ogres, O
sinless one!” (44)
