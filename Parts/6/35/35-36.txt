विष्णुं मन्यामहे राम॑ मानुषं रूपमास्थितम्‌।
नहि मानुषमात्रोड्साौ weal दृढविक्रमः॥ ३५॥
येन बद्धः समुद्रे च सेतु: स परमाद्धुतः।
कुरुष्व नरराजेन संधिं रामेण रावण।
ज्ञात्वावधार्य कर्माणि क्रियतामायतिक्षमम्‌॥ ३६॥

“We look upon Sri Rama as Lord
Visnu having assumed a human semblance.
The aforesaid Sri Rama of unyielding
prowess, by whom that most wonderful bridge
has been constructed over the deep, is no
mere human being. Therefore, conclude
peace, O Ravana, with Sri Rama, a ruler
of men. Having come to know of his
exploits, as well as of the evil portents in
Lanka, let that which is good for the
future be done after mature consideration.”

(35-36)
