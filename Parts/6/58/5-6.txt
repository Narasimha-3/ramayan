ततः Veet Pratt भीम॑ भीमपराक्रमम्‌।
गर्जन्त॑ सुमहाकायं राक्षसैरभिसंवृतम्‌॥ ५॥
ददर्श महती सेना वानराणां बलीयसाम्‌।
अभिसंजातघोषाणां प्रहस्तमभिगर्जताम्‌॥ ६॥
The huge army of the exeedingly mighty
monkeys, thereupon saw the terrible and
gigantic Prahasta of redoubtable prowess
sallying forth roaring, surrounded on all sides
by ogres. An uproar arose among them and
they began to roar at Prahasta. (5-6)
