
स तानू गृहीत्वा दुर्धर्षो राघवाय न्‍्यवेदयत्‌।

Aged Agel सर्व लक्ष्मणाय a वीर्यवान्‌॥ २१॥
Accepting them all, the valiant

Vibhisana, who was difficult to overpower,
placed all those benign offerings, which were
intended to bring felicity, at the disposal of
Sri Rama (a scion of Raghu) and Laksmana.
