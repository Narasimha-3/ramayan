Nay, hearing to his rage the loud
uproar of the gods (lit, the dwellers in
heaven) as well as of the monkeys (the
denizens of the forest), Ravana, the lord of
ogres, the avowed enemy of Indra, stood
on the battlefield once more, prepared for a
conflict. (26)
