“What adversary, however, can really
conquer me in war?” Having made this
unkind observation, the glorious Ravana,
the suzerain lord of ogres, who made people
cry by his tyranny, thereupon presently
climbed up his snow-white palace, which
had the height of many Palmyra trees, with
intent to survey the army of monkeys.
