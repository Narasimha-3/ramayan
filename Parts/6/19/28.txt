अब्रवीच्च हनूमांश्व सुग्रीवश्ष॒ विभीषणम्‌।
कथं सागरमक्षोभ्यं ATA वरुणालयम्‌।
सैन्ये: परिवृता: सर्वे वानराणां महौजसाम्‌॥ २८॥

Nay, Hanuman and Sugriva said to
Vibhisana, “How shall we all be able to
cross the imperturbable sea, the abode of
Varuna, the god of water, surrounded as we
are by hordes of monkeys endowed with
extraordinary energy? (28)
