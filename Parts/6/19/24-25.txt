Embracing Vibhisana, while he was
speaking as aforesaid, Sri Rama for his
part joyfully said to Laksmana, “Please fetch
water from the sea and consecrate at once
the highly sagacious Vibhisana with it as
the king of orges, now that | am happy
with him, O bestower of honour on others!”
