
विभीषणस्य शूरस्य यथार्थ क्रियतां बच:।
अलं कालात्ययं कृत्वा सागरो5यं नियुज्यताम्‌।
यथा सैन्येन गच्छाम पुरीं रावणपालिताम्‌॥ ४०॥

“Let the advice of the valiant Vibhisana,
which is in consonance with facts, be carried
out. It is no use losing time; let Ocean over
there be requested to help us so that we
may reach the city protected by Ravana
with our army.” (40)
