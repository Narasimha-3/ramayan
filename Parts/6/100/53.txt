
दृष्टिं दृष्टिविषस्येव सर्पस्थ मम रावण:।
यथा वा वैनतेयस्य दृष्टिं प्राप्तो भुजंगम:॥ ५३॥
“Having fallen within my view, Ravana

cannot survive any more than one who has
entered the range of vision of a snake

injecting deadly venom with his very glance,
or, than a serpent who has fallen under the
gaze of Garuda, the king of birds and enemy
of serpents. (53)
