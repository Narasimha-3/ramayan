
यदर्थ amt सैन्यं समानीतमिदं मया।
सुग्रीवश्च कृतो राज्ये निहत्वा वालिनं रणे।
यदर्थ सागरः wit: सेतुर्बद्धश्न सागरे॥ ५१॥
सोउयमद्य रणे पापश्चक्षु्बिषयमागतः।
wafer oat जीवितुमहति॥ ५२॥

“This sinful ogre on whose account
this army of monkeys has been dragged by
me to this distant land, Sugriva has been
installed on the throne of Kiskindha after
disposing of Vali in combat, and on whose
account the sea has been crossed and a
bridge thrown over it, has fallen within the
range of my vision on the battlefield today.
Having come within my sight, he does not
deserve to survive any longer. (51-52)
