
प्रच्छादयत्येष. हि. Tara:

सर्वा fest: सायकवृष्टिजालै:।
एतच्च सर्व पतिताग्रयशूरं

न भ्राजते वानरराजसैन्यम्‌॥ ७१॥

“Indeed this prince of ogres is thickly
covering all the four quarters with the
showers of his shafts. And this entire army
of Sugriva, the ruler of monkeys, whose
leading heroes have fallen, looks charming
no more. (71)
