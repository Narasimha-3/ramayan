“How can the highly intelligent Indrajit—
on whom a boon of invincibility has been
bestowed by Brahma (the self-born creator),
nay, who is steadfast of purpose and has
concealed his terrible form and who stands
with his weapons uplifted, even though his
body is invisible—be struck down in combat
today? (69)
