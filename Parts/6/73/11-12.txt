गजस्कन्धगता: केचित्‌ केचित्‌ परमवाजिभि: |
व्याप्रवृश्चिकमार्जारखरोष्ट्रे श्र भुजड़मै:॥ ११॥

वराहैः श्वापदेः सिंहैर्जम्बुके: पर्वतोपमै:।
काकहंसमयूरैश्व राक्षता भीमविक्रमा:॥ १२॥
Some ogres of terrible prowess rode
on the back of elephants, while others rode
on the back of excellent horses, tigers,
scorpions, cats, donkeys and camels as
well as on (the back of) serpents, boars,
beasts of prey such as lions, jackals as
large as hills, as also on the back of crows,

swans and peacocks. (11-12)
