
आवां तु gear पतितौ विसंज्ञौ
निवृत्तयुद्धौ हतहर्षरोषौ।
प्रवेक्ष्यत्यमरारिवास-
wet समासाद्य रणाग्रद्यलक्ष्मीम्‌॥ ७9२॥
“Finding us fallen unconscious and
bereft of joy and anger, having desisted
from fighting, nay, winning laurels in combat,
he will surely return to Lanka, the abode of
the enemies of gods.” (72)
