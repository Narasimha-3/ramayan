Tat सहाव्यग्रमनाः ATI ७०॥

“I know the self-born Lord Brahma—
who is the source (the creator) of this
universe—is inconceivable in his essence
and the missile used is presided over by
him. Therefore, remaining undisturbed in
mind, O wise one, endure you with me
today the hail of arrows on this spot. (70)
