Nay, smelling him on the head, Ravana
made inquiries in the matter with a delighted
mind. And to his inquisitive father, Ravana,
Indrajit submitted, in accordance with facts,
how the two princes (Sri Rama and
Laksmana) had been deprived of all
movement and splendour by being entwined
with arrows in the form of sankes. (48-49)
