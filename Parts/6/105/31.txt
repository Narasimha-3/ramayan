
अथ रविरवदन्निरीक्ष्य राम॑
Wert: परम प्रहृष्यमाण:।
निशिचरपतिसंक्षयं विदित्वा
सुरगणमध्यगतो THAT ३१॥
Delighted in mind to gaze on Sri Rama,
nay, feeling supremely exhilarated on
perceiving the destruction of Ravana

(the ruler of the night-stalkers) at hand,
the sun-god, standing in person in the midst
of a host of gods, exclaimed: “Make haste!”
