पुष्कराक्ष महाबाहो महावक्ष:  परंतप।
दिष्ट्या कृतमिदं कर्म त्वया धर्मभूतां वर॥२॥

“O lotus-eyed, mighty-armed and broad-

एतच्छुत्वा शुभं वाक्य राघवेणानुभाषितम्‌।

Hearing the foregoing excellent reply
made by Sri Rama (a scion of Raghu),
Lord Siva (the Supreme Lord) thereupon
chested scourge of your enemies, by good
luck has this feat been performed by you, O
jewel among those upholding the cause of
virtue. (2)
