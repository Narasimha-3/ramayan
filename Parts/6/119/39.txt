Taking his seat in the aerial car and
bidding adieu to his two sons and Sita, too,
Dasaratha, the foremost of the rulers of
men, who had a majestic bearing, was
clothed with splendour and felt thrilled all
over with delight, ascended to the realm of
Indra, the foremost of gods. (39)
