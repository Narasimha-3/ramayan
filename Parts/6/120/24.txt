ततस्तु सा लक्ष्मणरामपालिता

महाचर्मूईष्टजना यशस्विनी।
frat ज्वलन्ती विरराज सर्वतो

निशा प्रणीतेव हि शीतरश्मिना॥ २४॥

Thereupon that illustrious and mighty
army of happy troops for its part, which
was protected by Laksmana and Sri Rama
and was blazing with splendour on all sides,
shone brightly indeed like a night illumined
by the moon. (24)
