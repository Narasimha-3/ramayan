अभिषेचय चात्मानं पौरान्‌ गत्वा प्रहर्षय॥ २१॥

Reassure this devoted and illustrious
princess of Mithila and see your younger
brother, Bharata, who is practising austerities
through grief caused by separation from
you, the lofty-minded Satrughna as well as
all your mothers, O scourge of your enemies!
Reaching Ayodhya, bring excessive delight
to the citizens and get yourself consecrated
on the throne.” (20-21)
