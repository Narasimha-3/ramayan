
अथ समरपरिश्रमं Ped
समरमुखेष्वजितस्य लक्ष्मणस्य।
प्रियहितमुपपादयन्‌ महात्मा
समरमुपेत्य. विभीषणो5वबतस्थे॥ ७७॥

Reaching the battlefield in the meantime,
the high-souled Vibhisana took up his position
there in order to relieve the fatigue,
occasioned by the combat, of Laksmana,

who was invincible in the forefront of battle,
thereby affording him pleasure and doing a
good turn to him. (77)
