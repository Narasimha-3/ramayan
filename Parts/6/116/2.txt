
सा तदाश्रुतपूर्व हि wi uefa मैथिली।

gen भर्तुर्वची घोरं लज्जयावनताभवत्‌ू॥ २॥
That princess of Mithila stood bent low

with shame on hearing on that occasion in

the presence of a large gathering the
foregoing vehement speech of her husband,

the like of which had not been actually
heard by her before. (2)
