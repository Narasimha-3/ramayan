
न प्रमाणीकृतः पाणिर्बाल्ये मम निपीडितः।
मम भक्तिश्च शीलं च सर्व ते पृष्ठतः कृतम्‌॥ १६॥

“The fact that my hand was pressed
by you in my childhood as a token of your
acceptance when it was placed in yours by
my father during the nuptials was not duly
recognized by you while repudiating me.
Nay, my devotion to you as well as my
chastity have all been ignored by you.” (16)
