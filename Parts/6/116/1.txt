एवमुक्ता तु वैदेही परुषं रोमहर्षणम्‌।
राघवेण सरोषेण श्रुत्वा प्रव्यधिताभवत्‌॥ १॥

Hearing the harsh utterance, which
caused her hair to stand on end, when
spoken to as aforesaid by Sr Rama
(a scion of Raghu), who was full of anger,
Sita, a princess of Videha territory, for her
part, felt greatly afflicted. (1)
