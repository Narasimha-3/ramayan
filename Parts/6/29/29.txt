चारा बहिर्नित्यचरा निशाचरा:।
गिरे: सुवेलस्थ समीपवासिनं

wae रामबलं॑ महाबला:॥ २९॥

Arrived in the presence of Ravana, the
ten-headed monster, the aforesaid ogres,
who were always ranging outside as spies
and who were endowed with extraordinary
might, reported the army of Sri Rama to
be camping in the vicinity of the Suvela
mountain. (29)
