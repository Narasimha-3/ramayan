
ततः a संदीपितकोपवद्नि-
निशाचराणामधिपो महाबल:।

wa  पुत्रव्यसनं विचिन्तयन्‌
मुहरर्मुहुश्षेचक तदा विनिःश्वसनू॥ १९॥

The fire of wrath having been inflamed
in him, that suzerain lord of ogres, who was
endowed with extraordinary might, then
remained brooding on the self same fall of
his son, Atikaya, and also sighing again and
again at that time. (19)
