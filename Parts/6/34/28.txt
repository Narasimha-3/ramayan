लड्ढागता राक्षसराजभृत्या:।
हतौजसो दैन्यपरीतचेष्टा:

श्रेयो न पश्यन्ति नृपस्थ दोषातू॥ २८॥

Hearing the tumult of the monkey forces,
the servants of Ravana (the ruler of ogres)
present in Lanka felt debilitated and their
movements were overcome by depression.
Owing to the folly of their ruler they did not
see any prospect of prosperity. (28)
