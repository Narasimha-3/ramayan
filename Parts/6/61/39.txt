
रामवाक्यमुपश्रुत्तयय eta  जितकाशिनः।
पादपैरदयन्‌ वीरा are: परवाहिनीम्‌॥ ३९॥

Hearing the command of Sri Rama, the
heroic monkeys, who were bent upon taking
the life of their enemies, and who had
assumed a triumphant air, began to strike
the hostile army guarding the precincts of

the city outside the gates. (39)
