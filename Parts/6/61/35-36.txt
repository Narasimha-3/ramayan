गच्छ सैन्यानि सर्वाणि व्यूह्य तिष्ठस्व पावके।
द्वाराण्यादाय लड्जायाश्चर्याश्रास्याथ संक्रमान्‌॥ ३५॥
शैलश्रुड्राणि वृक्षांश्व॒ शिलाश्चाप्युपसंहरन्‌।
भवन्तः सायुधाः सर्वे वानराः शैलपाणयः ॥ ३६॥

“Go, disposing all the troops in battle
array, nay, occupying the gates, highways
and bridges of Lanka and getting together

mountain-tops, trees and rocks too, stand
ready for operation, O son of the fire-god!
And, armed with weapons and rocks in hand,
you too remain alert, O monkeys!” (35-36)
