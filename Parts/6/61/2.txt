ad दृष्ट्वा राक्षसश्रेष्ठं॑ पर्वताकारदर्शनम्‌।
क्रममाणमिवाकाशं पुरा नारायणं यथा॥२॥
Perceiving that prince of ogres, who
looked like a mountain, taking strides across
space, as it were, like Lord Narayana
(manifested in the form of Trivikrama, who
measured the whole universe in a couple of
strides), he became alert. (2)
