कुम्भकर्ण et श्रुत्वा राघवेण महाबलम्‌।
अतिकायं a दुर्मर्ष लक्ष्मणेन edt तदा।
fot चेन्द्रजितं Ut रावणो नावबुध्यते॥ २१॥

“Ravana has not realized the greatness
of Rama even on hearing of Kumbhakarna,
who was endowed with extraordinary might,
having been killed by Rama, a scion of
Raghu, as well as Atikaya, who was difficult
to disregard, and his beloved son, Indrajit,
having been made short work of by Laksmana
the other day. (21)
