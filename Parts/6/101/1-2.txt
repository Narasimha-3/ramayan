Canto CI

Sti Rima laments over the precarious condition of Laksmana.
The latter regains consciousness under the treatment
of the monkey Susena with the help of a herb
brought at his instance all the way from
the Himalaya mountain by Hanuman

शक्त्या निपातितं gear रावणेन बलीयसा।
लक्ष्मणं समरे शूरं शोणितौघपरिप्लुतम्‌॥ १॥
स दत्त्वा तुमुलं युद्ध रावणस्य दुरात्मनः।
