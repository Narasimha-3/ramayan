
स तूत्पातांस्ततो दृष्ट्वा राक्षसानां भयावहान्‌।

प्रादुर्भूतान्‌ सुघोरांश्च धूप्राक्षो व्यथितो5भवत्‌।

WAR राक्षसा: सर्वे धूमप्राक्षस्य TAT ३५॥
Dhumraksa for his part felt perturbed

at that time to behold most terrible portents,

boding danger to the ogres, which had

come to light before him. Nay, all the ogres
marching in front of Dhumraksa fainted. (35)
