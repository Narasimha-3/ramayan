स॒पश्चिमेन द्वारेण निर्ययौ राक्षसैर्वुतः।
इन्द्रजित्‌ सुमहावीर्य: पौलस्त्यो देवकण्टकः॥ ३॥
Surrounded by other ogres, the
notorious Indrajit, a scion of Sage Pulastya
(one of the nine mind-born sons of Brahma
entrusted with the duty of creating the world)
and a thorn in the side of gods, who was
endowed with exceptional prowess, issued
through the western gate. (3)
