“Again, this valiant prince—who stands
close by, nay, who is dark-brown of
complexion and has lotus-like eyes, is a
great car-warrior among the scions of
Ikswaku, whose heroism is well-known in
the world, in whom virtue never wavers,
who never transgresses righteousness, who
knows the use of the (mystic) missile presided
over by Brahma (the creator) as also the
Vedas, nay, who is the foremost of the
knowers of the Vedas, who can split the
vault of heaven with his arrows and even
rend the earth, whose wrath is like the
wrath of Death and whose prowess is similar
to that of Indra (the ruler of gods), and
whose consort, Sita, was borne away by
you from Janasthana. The selfsame Rama
is advancing to wage war on you, O king!
(18—21)
