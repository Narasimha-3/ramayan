“These monkeys, whom you see ranged
like huge elephants in rut, like banian trees
standing on the banks of the Ganga or like
sal trees growing on the Himalayas, O king,
are difficult to resist, mighty and able to
change their form at will. They look like
Daityas and Danavas and are endowed
with the prowess of gods in war. (2-3)
