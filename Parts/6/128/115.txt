
TAM सुरा: सर्वे प्रीयन्ते सम्प्रश्रण्वताम्‌।
विनायकाश्च शाम्यन्ति गृहे तिष्ठन्ति यस्य वै॥ ११५॥

Through a hearing of this epic all the
gods of the Hindu pantheon get pleased
with those who carefully listen to it. Nay, all
obstacles coming in the way of an individual,
in whose house a copy of the epic exists,
definitely come to an end. (115)
