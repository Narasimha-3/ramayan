
विजयेत महीं राजा प्रवासी स्वस्तिमान्‌ भवेत्‌।
स्त्रियो रजस्वला: श्र॒त्वा पुत्रान्‌ सूयुरनुत्तमान्‌॥ ११६॥
A king listening to this epic will be able
to conquer the earth, an individual staying
away from home will fare well and women
listening to it prior to their pregnancy will
give birth to sons who are unsurpassed by
another in point of excellences. (116)
