तेन तद्ठविहितं शस्त्र वारुणं परमाद्भुतम्‌।
ततः Baal महातेजा इन्द्रजितू समितिंजय:।
आग्रेयं संदधे det स लोकं संक्षिपन्निव॥ ५६॥

That most wonderful weapon presided
over by Varuna was rendered ineffectual by

the missile presided over by Rudra (the god
of destruction). Feeling provoked, the said
Indrajit, a victor in combat, who was endowed
with extraordinary energy, thereupon put to
his bow a flaming missile presided over by
the god of fire, as if he was going to destroy
the world with it. (56)
