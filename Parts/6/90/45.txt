ते तस्य are भित्त्वा तु रुक्मपुल्ला निमित्तगा:।
बभूवुलोहितादिग्धा रक्ता इब महोरगा:॥ ४५॥

Having penetrated through his body,
the aforesaid arrows for their part, which
were provided with golden feathers and
flew straight to their target, got stained
with blood and looked like huge red
serpents. (45)
