
स॒त्वमिन्द्राशनिप्रख्यै: शरैरवकिरन्‌ परान्‌।
अभिद्रवाशु यावद्‌ a नैतत्‌ कर्म समाप्यते॥ ४॥

“Therefore, so long as this undertaking
of Indrajit is not actually carried through,
promptly assail you the enemies, covering
them with your arrows resembling the
thunderbolt of Indra, the ruler of gods. (4)
