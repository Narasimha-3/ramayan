
अतिबलमतिकायमशभ्रकल्पं
afer विनिपात्य स लक्ष्मण: प्रहृष्ट: |
त्वरितमथ तदा स॒ रामपार्श्व
कपिनिवहैश्व सुपूजितो जगाम॥११६॥

Greatly rejoiced and highly honoured
by hosts of monkeys on having struck down
in a duel Atikaya, who was endowed with
surpassing might and looked like a mass of
cloud, the aforesaid celebrated Laksmana
forthwith hastily sought the presence of Sri
Rama on that occasion. (116)
