प्रहर्षयुक्ता बहवस्तु arn:

प्रफुल्लपद्मप्रतिमाननास्तदा ॥
अपूजयँलल क्ष्मणमिष्टभागिनं
हते रिपौ भीमबले दुरासदे॥ ११५॥

Filled with extreme delight on their

enemy, who was endowed with terrible might
and was difficult to approach, having been
killed, numerous monkeys for their part,
whose faces resembled full-blown lotuses,
forthwith paid their homage to Laksmana,
who had secured victory for them. (115)
