
योउसौ नवाकंदितताग्रचक्षु-
Weal घण्टानिनदप्रणादम्‌।
गज खरं गर्जति a महात्मा
Tet नाम A wa वीरः॥ १७॥
The yonder well-known hero, whose

eyes are coppery like the newly risen
sun, and who, riding an elephant whose
trumpeting resembles the pealing of bells,
roars in harsh tones and is endowed with
extraordinary courage, is Mahodara by name.

(17)
