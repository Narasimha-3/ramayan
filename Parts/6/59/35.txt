गतेषु_ रक्षःसु यथानियोगम्‌।

Having dismissed his well-known
counsellors, and the ogres, who accompanied
him, having departed as directed, Ravana
began to split asunder the waters of the sea
of monkeys’ army in the same way as a
gigantic fish would rend the entire expanse

of a sea. (35)
