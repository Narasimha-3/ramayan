मेदाद््रगात्रो रुधिरावसिक्त:।

जग्राह शक्ति स्वयमुग्रशक्तिः
स्वयंभुदत्तां युधि देवशत्रु:॥ १०७॥

Struck with arrows and bathed in blood,
his bow having been cut, and his limbs
splattered with fat, Ravana (the enemy of
gods), who was himself endowed with
formidable energy, seized on the battlefield
a javelin, gifted to him by Brahma, the self-

born creator. (107)
