आसेदतुर्महारण्ये कबन्ध॑ नाम राक्षसम्‌।
ततः कबन्धवचनाद्‌ राम: सत्यपराक्रम:॥ ३५॥
ऋष्यमूकगिरिं गत्वा सुग्रीवेण समागतः।
wat: समागमः पूर्व प्रीत्या wet व्यजायत॥ ३६॥

“The two princes met in a vast forest
an ogre, Kabandha by name, and killed
him. Repairing to the Rsyamuka mountain
according to the advice of Kabandha, Sti
Rama of unfailing prowess thereupon came
into contact with Sugriva. A meeting of
hearts through affinity had taken place
between them even before they met.(35-36)
