All the gods (who had been witnessing
the combat from their arieal cars) alongwith
Rsis (seers of Vedic Mantras), as also both
the brothers, Sri Rama and Laksmana
applauded the aforesaid exploit of Angada
(son of Vali), who was worthy of praise for
his valour. (30)
