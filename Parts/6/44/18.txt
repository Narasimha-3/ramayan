तेषामापततां शब्दः क्रुद्धानामपि गर्जताम्‌।
उद्वर्त sa सप्तानां समुद्राणामभूतू FA: UNS Ul

The noise of those roaring ogres,
rushing furiously towards Sri Rama,
resembled the roar of all the seven oceans
(dividing the seven Dwipas or principal
divisions of the terrestrial globe from one
another) at the time of the final dissolution.
(18)
