विमुच्य. रोषं रिपुनिग्रहातू ततो
राम: स॒ सौम्यत्वमुपागतोउरिहा॥ १२४॥
Having laid aside once for all the bow
alongwith the arrows, as well as the massive

armour, conferred on him by the mighty
Indra (the ruler of gods), and having totally
given up his anger now that his enemy had
been subjugated, the aforesaid Sri Rama,
the destroyer of his enemies, thereupon
assumed a gentle mien. (124)
