Having encountered and killed the ogres,
that great monkey, Hanuman (son of wind-
god), enjoyed the glory appropriate to a
hero much in the same way as Lord Visnu
did on killing in the front of the army in line
of battle the redoubtable and great demons

(Madhu, Kaitabha and so on) who were
endowed with great strength and were
capable of destroying their enemies. (38)
