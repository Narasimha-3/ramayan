विभीषणश्चैव महाबलस्तदा॥ ३९॥
At that time hosts of gods, nay, Sri
Rama himself as well as Laksmana, who
was endowed with exceeding might, even
so, the monkeys headed by Sugriva as
also Vibhisana, who was endowed with
extraordinary might, honoured the monkey,
Hanuman. (39)
