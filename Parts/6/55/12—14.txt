All the birds and beasts uttered unkind
and fearful cries. Not minding those evil

portents, the ogre, whose shoulders were
fully developed like those of a lion, and who
equalled a tiger in prowess, sallied forth to
the battlefield. Even as the aforesaid ogre
was issuing forth with other ogres, an
immense tumult arose, convulsing the sea,
as it were. The huge army of the monkeys
was frightened by that clamour. (12—14)
