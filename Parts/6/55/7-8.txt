Mounting his huge chariot, which was
decked with jewels and pure gold, Akampana,
who had the colour of a cloud and resembled
a cloud in size, and whose loud voice
resembled thunder, then sallied forth,
surrounded by dreadful ogres. He could not
be shaken in a major conflict by gods. (7-8)
