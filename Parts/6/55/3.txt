
एष शास्ता च गोप्ता च नेता च युधि सत्तम:।

भूतिकामश्च मे नित्यं नित्यं a समरप्रिय:॥ ३॥
“He is a tamer of foes, nay, the protector

of his own people and a commander of

his forces on the field of battle. He is the
foremost of warriors, nay, ever desirous of

my welfare and ever fond of war.
एष जेष्यति काकुत्स्थौ सुग्रीव॑ च महाबलम्‌।
वानरांश्वापरान्‌ घोरान्‌ हनिष्यति न संशयः॥ ४॥
“This ogre, Akampana, will conquer
Rama and Laksmana (the two scions of
Kakutstha) as well as Sugriva, and is
endowed with extraordinary might, and will
exterminate the other formidable monkeys
