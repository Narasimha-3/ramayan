Having penetrated deep into that lovely
subterranean abode of Kumbhakarna, which
was provided with enormous gates, nay,
which was one Yojana (or eight miles) in
extent on every side, and from which issued
a stream of odour of flowers—the ogres,
though endowed with extraordinary might,
were pushed back by force of the wind from
the nostrils of Kumbhakarna and, pressing
forward with difficulty, forced their way into
the cave with effort. (24-25)
