अतिलक्षणसम्पन्नं माधुर्यगुणभूषणम्‌।
बुद्ध्या MSA युक्त त्वमेवाहसि भाषितुम्‌॥ २६॥
“You alone can actually utter words
which are endowed with rare characteristics
of good speech, nay, adorned with the quality
of mellifluity and dictated by an intellect
which is characterized by the well-known
eight excellences.* (26)
