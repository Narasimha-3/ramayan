पूर्णचन्द्रमुखं राम॑ द्रक्ष्यस्यद्य सलक्ष्मणम्‌।
स्थितमित्रं हतामित्र॑ wees सुरेश्वरम्‌॥ ५१॥

“You shall see this very day Sri Rama—
whose countenance resembles the full moon,
whose friends (such as Sugriva and
Vibhisana) are alive, nay, whose enemies
have been got rid of—accompanied by
Laksmana, even as Saci, the consort of
Indra, would see Indra, the ruler of gods.” (51)
