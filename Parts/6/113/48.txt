युक्ता रामस्थ भवती धर्मपत्नी गुणान्विता।
प्रतिसंदिश मां देवि गमिष्ये यत्र wera: ४८॥
“You are the lawfully wedded spouse
of Sri Rama, full of excellences and worthy
of him in all respects. Pray, give me a
message in return. | shall proceed to the
place where Sri Rama (a scion of Raghu)
is, O godlike lady!” (48)
