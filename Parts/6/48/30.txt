“These two princes are not vulnerable
to being conquered even by gods and
demons combined, including Indra, the ruler
of gods. Nay, after observing such indications
on the face of the two brothers as also on
the face of the warriors guarding them, has
the fact of the princes being alive been
made known by me to you. (30)
