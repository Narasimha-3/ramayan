
अनृतं नोक्तपूर्व मे न a वक्ष्यामि मैथिलि।
चारित्रसुखशीलत्वात्‌ प्रविष्टासि मनो मम॥ २९॥

“No falsehood has ever been uttered
by me in the past nor shall | ever tell a lie,
O princess of Mithila! You have found your
way into my mind due to your amiable
disposition which has proved to be a source
of delight because of your spotless character.
