
Be तु सुमहच्चित्रं wit: पश्यस्व मैथिलि।
विसंज्ञौं पतितावेता Aa लक्ष्मीर्विमुल्जञति॥ ३१॥

“Also see for yourself this very great
wonder, O princess of Mithila that bodily
splendour does not completely desert these
two princes, even though they have fallen
unconscious on the ground hit by the arrows.
