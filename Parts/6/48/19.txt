
न कालस्यातिभारोउस्ति Faas सुदुर्जय:।
यत्र WA: सह क्रात्रा शेते युधि निपातितः॥ १९॥

“Since Sri Rama lies struck down on
the field of battle with his younger brother,
Laksmana, no burden is too heavy to
shoulder for Providence (in the form of the
Time-Spirit, who is capable of doing anything).
Even ‘Death’ (which brings about the end of
all) is not very difficult to conquer for Him.

(19)
