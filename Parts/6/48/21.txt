
सा तु चिन्तयते नित्यं समाप्तव्रतमागतम्‌।
कदा द्रक्ष्यामि सीतां A लक्ष्मणं च सराघवम्‌॥ २१॥

“She, for her part, always remains
absorbed in the thought ‘O, when shall | be
able to see Laksmana returned to Ayodhya
with Sri Rama, a scion of Raghu, having
completed his vow of remaining in exile in
the woods for fourteen years, as also Sita?’ ”
