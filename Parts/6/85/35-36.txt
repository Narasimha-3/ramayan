Accompanied by Vibhisana as also
by the valiant Angada as well as by
Hanuman (son of the wind-god), the mighty
prince, Laksmana, penetrated that
heterogeneous army of the enemy, which
was resplendent with spotless weapons and
thick with ensigns, and which abounded in
huge chariots, was exceedingly formidable
and was endowed with immeasurable
impetuosity—even as one would penetrate
a thick veil of darkness. (35-36)
