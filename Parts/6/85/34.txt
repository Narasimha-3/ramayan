Duly reaching Nikumbhila, that tamer
of his enemies, Laksmana (the delight of
the Raghus), stood, bow in hand, to vanquish
utterly Indrajit (who took recourse to conjuring
tricks as an expedient in war) in conformity
with Brahma’s ordinance in the form of the
boon granted by him in favour of Indrajit
(vide verse 14 above). (34)
