Baya कृशो राजा मैथिलीकाममोहितः।
असन्मानाच्च सुहदां पाप: पापेन कर्मणा॥ १॥

King Ravana, who was infatuated with
passion for Sita (a princess of Mithila) and
had begun to be looked upon as sinful
because of his evil doing in the shape of
Sita’s abduction, grew emaciated through
disrespect shown to him by his near and
dear ones and his consuming passion for
Sita. (1)
