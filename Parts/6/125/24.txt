पञ्ञमीमद्य रजनीमुषित्वा वचनान्मुने:।
भरद्वाजाभ्यनुज्ञातं द्रक्ष्यस्यत्रेव राघवम्‌॥ २४॥

“You will be able to see Sri Rama (a
scion of Raghu) at this very place today
when he has been duly permitted by Sage
Bharadwaja after spending at the instance
of the sage with him the night of the fifth
lunar day of the bright half of the month of
ASwina.” (24)
