अयोध्यां तु समालोक्य चिन्तयामास राघव:।
प्रियकाम: प्रियं रामस्ततस्त्वरितविक्रमः ॥ १॥

Gazing on Ayodhya while still in the

air, before landing at the hermitage of
Bharadwaja, Sri Rama (a scion of Raghu)
for his part, who wished to do a kindly act,
and who was quick in exhibiting prowess,
contemplated thereupon to do a kindly act
to his friends, Sugriva and others, as well
as to the people of Ayodhya. (1)
