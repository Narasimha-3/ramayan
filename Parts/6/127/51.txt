
अभिवाद्य सुमित्रां च कैकेयीं च यशस्विनीम्‌।
स Ags ततः wal: पुरोहितमुपागमत्‌॥ ५१॥

Nay, having greeted Sumitra (the mother
of Laksmana and Satrughna) as well as the
illustrious Kaikeyi (Bharata’s mother), he
then approached and greeted all the rest of
his mothers as well as Sage Vasistha (his
family priest). (51)
