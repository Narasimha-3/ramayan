
रामो मातरमासाद्य विवर्णा शोककर्शिताम्‌।
जग्राह Wore: पादौ मनो arg: प्रहर्षयन्‌॥ ५०॥

Approaching Kausalya (his own
mother), who looked pale and had got
emaciated through grief born of separation
from her husband as well as from her two
sons and daughter-in-law), Sri Rama, who
bent low with reverence, clasped the feet of

her mother, thereby bringing excessive delight
to her mind. (50)
