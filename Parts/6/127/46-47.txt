Embracing Sugriva, the foremost of
monkeys, prince Bharata, a jewel among
the virtuous, who was endowed with
extraordinary energy, said, “You are a fifth
brother to us four brothers, O Sugriva!
For a friend is made through beneficence
actuated by good-will and a friend is as
good as a brother, while maleficence is that
which distinguishes an enemy.” (46-47)
