ART प्रत्यकरोद्‌ वीर्य विक्लवेनान्तरात्मना॥ २८॥
क्षिप्ताश्नाशु शरास्तेन शस्त्राणि विविधानि च।
मरणार्थायवर्तन्ते मृत्युकालो5भ्यवर्तत॥ २९॥

When, on account of his mind being
confused, he could no longer take up weapons
nor stretch his bow, nor, again, could he
oppose Sri Rama’s valour, while the arrows
and other weapons of all kinds swiftly
discharged by Sri Rama, had his death for
their objective, the hour of his death appeared
imminent. (28-29)
