fred  ufad समीक्ष्य॥ ३१॥

Turning in haste the chariot of Ravana,
which was rumbling like a cloud, on
perceiving the king (the lord of ogres) sunk
down, bereft of energy, the charioteer forthwith
sneaked away in dismay from the battlefield.
