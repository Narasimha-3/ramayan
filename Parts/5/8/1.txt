स तस्य मध्ये भवनस्य संस्थितो
महद्विमानं मणिरत्रचित्रितम्‌।
प्रतप्तजाम्बूनदजालकृत्रिमं
ददर्श etary पवनात्मज: कपिः ॥ १॥
Stationed in the centre of the aforesaid
palace, that intelligent Hanuman, sprung from
the loins of the wind-god, beheld a vast
aerial car, rendered picturesque by gems
and jewels and distinguished by the structure
of lattice windows of highly refined gold.
