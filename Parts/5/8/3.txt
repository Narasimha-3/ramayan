
न तत्र किंचिन्न कृतं प्रयत्नतो
न तत्र किंचिन्न महार्घरत्नवत्‌।
न ते विशेषा नियताः सुरेष्वपि
न तत्र fafa महाविशेषवत्‌॥ ३॥

There was nothing in that structure
which had not been executed with special
effort and there was no part of it which was
not inlaid with precious stones of great value.
The specialities which were incorporated
into it were not to be found even in the
abodes of gods and there was nothing in it
which was not marked with some great
speciality. (3)
