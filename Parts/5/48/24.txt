
आयान्तं स रथं दृष्ट्वा तूर्णमिन्द्रध्वज॑ कपि:।
ननाद च महानादं व्यवर्धत चर वेगवानू॥ evil
Perceiving the chariot bearing the

standard of Indra (seized by him as a token
of his having conquered Indra) coming rapidly
towards him, Hanuman, who was full of
ardour, raised a great shout and grew to
immense proportions. (24)
