यदि वैश्रवणस्य a यमस्य वरुणस्थ च।
चारुरूपमिदं कृत्वा प्रविष्टो नः पुरीमिमाम्‌॥ ९॥
“Tell us if you are a messenger of the
god of riches, Kubera, (a son of Sage
Visrava), Yama (the god of retribution) or
Varuna (the god of water) and have deeply
penetrated into this city of ours, having
assumed the disguise of a spy. (9)
