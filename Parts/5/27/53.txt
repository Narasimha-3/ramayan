पक्षी च॒ शाखानिलयं प्रविष्ट:
पुनः पुनशचोत्तमसान्त्ववादी।
वाचमुदीरयाण:
पुनः पुनशचोदयतीव SS ॥ ५३॥
“Nay, uttering excellent sweet notes
again and again after entering its nest on
the branch of a tree, a delighted bird is
repeatedly urging Sita, as it were, to rejoice,
uttering, as it did, a note announcing the
advent of an auspicious hour.” (53)
