“Speak out what kind of dream was
seen by you this night.” Hearing the request
that had escaped from the lips of the
ogresses, Trijata for her part made the
following answer bearing on the dream seen
by her during the morning wee-hours :

“Mounting a celestial palanquin made
of ivory and coursing through the airspace,
nay, drawn by a thousand horses, Sri Rama
himself, clad in a white raiment and wearing

a white garland, has duly come here with
Laksmana. (8—10)
