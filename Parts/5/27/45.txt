भर्त्िसितामपि याचध्वं tere: किं विवक्षया।
राघवाद्धि भयं घोरं राक्षसानामुपस्थितम्‌॥ ४५॥
“Beseech her forgiveness, O ogresses,
even though she has been menaced by
you. What will be gained through your anxiety
to discuss the subject any more; for a
terrible danger from Sri Rama (a scion of
Raghu) threatens the ogres. (45)
