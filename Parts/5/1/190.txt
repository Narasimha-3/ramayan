कपिराज्ञा यथाख्यातं सच्त्वमद्भुतदर्शनम्‌।
छायाग्राहि महावीर्य तदिदं नात्र era: १९०॥
“It is the same creature of weird
appearance, endowed with extraordinary
prowess and capable of securing her prey
by means of its shadow, which was depicted
in its true colours by Sugriva, the king of
monkeys; there is no doubt about it.” (190)
