
अथोर्ध्व॑ दूरमागत्य हित्वा शैलमहार्णवौ।
fad: पन्थानमासाद्य जगाम विमलेअम्बरे॥ १३४॥
Then, rising high, nay, leaving the

mountain as well as the ocean, far below
and reaching the path of the wind (i.e., his
father), Hanuman coursed

cloudless sky.

भूयश्चोर्ध्व॑ गतिं प्राप्प गिरिं तमवलोकयन्‌।
वायुसूनुर्निगलम्बो जगाम कपिकुझ्सर:॥ १३५॥

Rising still higher and looking on the
mountain below, Hanuman, a son of the
wind-god, an elephant among the monkeys,
moved on unsupported in the sky. (135)
