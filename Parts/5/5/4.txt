हंसो यथा राजतपञ्जरस्थः
सिंहो यथा मन्दरकन्दरस्थ:।

वीरो यथा गर्वितकुड्लरस्थ-

एचन्द्रोडषपि ATT तथाम्बरस्थ: ॥ ४॥

The moon shone forth in the heavens
much in the same way as a swan displays
its charm in a silvery cage, as a lion ina
cave of Mount Mandara and as a hero
mounted on a proud elephant. (4)
