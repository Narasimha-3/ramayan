
अचित्त्यामद्भुताकारां FET लंकां महाकपि:।
ania विषण्णो weve वैदेह्या दर्शनोत्सुक: ॥ ५५॥

Beholding Lanka, which was beyond
all imagination and strange to look at,
Hanuman, the great monkey, felt despondent
and delighted too at the prospect of finding
out Sita, eager as he was to see Sita, a
princess of the Videha territory. (55)
