प्रासादमालाविततां स्तम्भे: काझ्जनसंनिभे:।
शातकुम्भनिभेजलिमगन्धर्वनगरोपमाम्‌ ॥५१॥
Springing up quickly at eventide, the
powerful Hanuman proceeded to penetrate
deep into the lovely city, whose highways
had been symmetrically aligned, which was
filled with rows of mansions, and with its golden
pillars and golden lattice windows, it looked
like the city of the Gandharvas. (50-51)
