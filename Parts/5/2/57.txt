
यशस्विनीं

अन्द्रोडपि साचिव्यमिवास्य कुर्व-
स्तारागणैर्म ध्यगतो विराजन्‌।
ज्योत्स्नावितानेन वितत्य लोका-
नुत्तिष्ठतेडनेकसहस्त्रश्मि: ॥ ५७॥

Rendering aid, as it were, to him and
shining brightly with hosts of stars in their
midst, nay, overspreading the worlds with the
canopy of its light, the moon with its myriad
rays made its appearance on the horizon.

(57)
