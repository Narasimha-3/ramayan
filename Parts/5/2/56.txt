स॒पाण्डुराविद्धविमानमालिनीं
महाईजाम्बूनदजालतोरणाम्‌ ।
रावणबाहुपालितां
क्षपाचरैर्भीमबलै: सुपालिताम्‌॥ ५६॥

Presently he entered the glorious city,
which was arrayed with rows of white interlaced
seven-storeyed mansions, and conspicuous
with gold—latticed archways of great value,
nay, which was protected by Ravana and
fully guarded by ogres of terrific might. (56)
