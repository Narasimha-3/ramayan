कुसुमिततरुजालसंततानि
भ्रमरयुतानि

कनकविमलहारभूषितांगी
विहर मया सह site काननानि॥ ३६॥

समुद्रतीरजानि।

“Your person adorned with bright gold
necklaces, range in my company, O timid
one, the groves, appearing on the seashore,
nay, covered all over with clusters of trees in
blossom and crowded with black bees.” (36)
