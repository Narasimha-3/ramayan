
Pet राक्षसेन्द्रे च सपुत्रामात्यबान्धवे।
a areata रामेण शशाड्लेनेब रोहिणी॥ २०॥

“When Ravana, the lord of ogres, is
slain along with his sons, ministers and
kinsfolk, you shall be united with Sri Rama
as Rohini (a consort of the moon-god
presiding over a constellation of the same
name) is with the moon-god. (20)
