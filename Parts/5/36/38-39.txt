“Nay, | swear to you by the Mandara
and Malaya, Vindhya, Sumeru and Dardura
mountains, constituting the abode of
monkeys, as well as by roots and fruits which
serve as our food, O godlike lady, that you
will soon behold the charming countenance
of Sri Rama, resembling the risen full moon,
with beautiful eyes, lips like a ripe Bimba
fruit and lovely earrings. (38-39)
