शतयोजनविस्तीर्ण. सागरो_ मकरालयः।
विक्रमश्लाघनीयेन क्रमता गोष्पदीकृत: ॥ ८ ॥

“Nay, the sea which extends to a
distance of one hundred Yojanas (or eight
hundred miles) and is an abode of alligators,
has been treated by you like the impression
of a cow's hoof on the soil while crossing it,
laudable as you are for your prowess. (8)
