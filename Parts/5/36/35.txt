विष्टम्भयित्वा बाणौधैरक्षोभ्यं वरुणालयम्‌।
करिष्यति पुरी Het काकुत्स्थ: शान्तराक्षसाम्‌॥ ३५॥
“Filling the sea (lit., the abode of Varuna)
through and through with volleys of shafts,
even though it cannot even be shaken by
others, Sri Rama, a scion of Kakutstha, will
rid the city of Lanka of ogres. (35)
