
रामो धर्मभूतां श्रेष्ठो महेन्द्रसमविक्रम:।
एतच्छृत्वा Aa तस्य मैनाकस्य महात्मन:॥ १८॥
कार्यमावेद्य a गिरेरुद्धतं वै मनो मम।
तेन चाहमनुज्ञाता मैनाकेन महात्मना॥ १९॥
“‘Sri Rama is the foremost of those
who uphold the cause of virtue and is equal
in prowess to the mighty Indra.’ On hearing
this submission of the high-souled Mainaka

mountain, my purpose was confided by me
to the mountain and my mind got stirred up
once more to proceed further. Nay, | was
permitted by the lofty-minded Mainaka to
depart. (18-19)
