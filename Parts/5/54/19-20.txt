ततस्तस्मिन्‌ Te मुख्ये नानारत्रविभूषिते।
मेरुमन्दरसंकाशे नानामड्डलशोभिते॥ १९॥
प्रदीक्षमग्रिमुत्सज्य लाडूलाग्रे . प्रतिष्ठितम्‌।
ननाद हनुमान्‌ वीरो युगान्तजलदो यथा॥ Roll

Scattering blazing fire on his tail in
that distinguished palace, which was decked
with various jewels, which resembled
Mounts Meru and Mandara in height and
was enriched with various auspicious articles,
the valiant Hanuman thundered like a cloud
appearing at the time of universal dissolution.

(19-20)
