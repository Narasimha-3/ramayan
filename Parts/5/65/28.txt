
तौ जाताश्चासौ राजपुत्रौ विदित्वा
तच्चाभिज्ञानं राघवाय प्रदाय।
देव्या चाख्यातं सर्वमेवानुपूर्व्याद्‌
वबाचा सम्पूर्ण बायुपुत्र: शशंस॥ २८ Ul

Coming to know that the two princes,
Sri Rama and Laksmana, were restored
to confidence, and having delivered the
aforesaid token to Sri Rama, Hanuman, son
of the wind-god, reproduced in its entirety
in so many words seriatim all that was
communicated by the godlike lady. (28)
