“Since Sita does not kill Ravana by

subjecting him to a curse, it is evident that
the aforesaid Ravana is very powerful by

virtue of his asceticism. Nevertheless as
the great sin incurred by him through the
abduction of Sita has filled his cup of sin to
the brim, Sri Rama will serve only as an
instrument for his death. (30)
