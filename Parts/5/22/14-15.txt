उक्तवानसि Ae पापं aT गतस्तस्य मोक्ष्यसे॥ १५॥
“Indeed, who other than you in all the
three worlds will seek even with his mind to
have me, the consort of Sri Rama, who has
set his mind on virtue, even as one would
seek Saci, the spouse of Indra? Going
anywhere you will not escape the
consequences of the sinful proposal you
have made to the consort of Sri Rama, who
is endowed with immense energy, O vile
ogre! (14-15)
