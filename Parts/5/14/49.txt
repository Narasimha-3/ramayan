
संध्याकालमना: श्यामा श्लुवमेष्यति जानकी।
नदीं wat शुभजलां संध्यार्थ वरवर्णिनी॥ ४९॥

‘Intent on the devotions pertaining to
the morning and evening twilights, the fair-
complexioned daughter of Janaka, who ever
looks as though she were only sixteen years
old, will surely seek this stream of translucent
water for the sake of her morning ablutions
and devotions. (49)
