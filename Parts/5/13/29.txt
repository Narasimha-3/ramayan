कृतज्ञः सत्यसंधश्च Ata: प्लवगाधिप:।
TH तथागतं दृष्ट्वा ततस्त्यक्ष्यति जीवितम्‌॥ २८॥
‘Finding Sri Rama reduced to such a

plight, Sugriva, the suzerain lord of monkeys,
who is grateful and true to his pledge, will

forthwith yield up his life.

gaat व्यथिता दीना निरानन्दा तपस्विनी।
पीडिता भर्तुशोकेन रुमा त्यक्ष्यति जीवितमू॥ २९॥

‘Disconsolate and distressed, miserable
and cheerless, nay, stricken with grief over
the death of her husband, poor Ruma too
will surrender her life. (29)
