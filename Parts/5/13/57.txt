
जित्वा तु राक्षसान्‌ देवीमिक्ष्वाकुकुलनन्दिनीम्‌ |
सम्प्रदास्यामि रामाय सिद्धीमिव तपस्विने॥ ५७॥

‘Again, having conquered the ogres, |
shall hand over the godlike Sita, the delight
of Ikswaku’s race, to Sri Rama even as
success is conferred on an ascetic by a
goddess pleased with his austerities.’ (57)
