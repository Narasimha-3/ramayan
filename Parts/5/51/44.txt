ब्रह्म स्वयम्भूश्नतुराननो वा
रुद्रस्त्रिनेत्रस्त्रिपुपन्तको वा।
इन्द्रो महेन्द्र; सुरनायको वा
स्थातुं न शक्ता APT राघवस्य॥ ४४॥
“Even the self-born and four-headed

रामस्य
Brahma (the creator), the three-eyed Lord
Rudra (the god of destruction), the Destroyer
of the three cities (built by the demon Maya),
and the mighty sovereign Indra, the ruler of
gods, are unable to stand before Sri Rama,
a scion of Raghu, on the field of battle.” (44)
