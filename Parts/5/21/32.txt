तस्य ते विग्रहे ताभ्यां युगग्रहणमस्थिरम्‌।
वृत्रस्थेवेन्द्रबाहुभ्यां. बाहोरेकस्थ fara

“Your victory in an encounter with them,
situated as you are, is uncertain in the
same way as on the part of a single arm of
the demon Vrtra in its encounter with both
the arms of Indra during the great war between
gods and demons at the beginning of creation.

(32)
