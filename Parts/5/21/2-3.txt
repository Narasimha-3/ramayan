निवर्तव wat wa: स्वजने प्रीयतां मनः॥ ३॥

Placing a straw between herself and
Ravana in order to avoid direct contact with
him, poor Sita of excellent limbs and bright
smiles, who was sobbing and shuddering,
stricken with sorrow as she was, and, being
devoted to her husband, was, constantly
thinking of him alone, replied in the following
words: “Withdraw your mind from me and
let it be satisfied with your own consorts
alone. (2-3)
