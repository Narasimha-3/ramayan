“You are not deserving of seeking to
win me any more than a sinner deserves to
seek perfection in the form of final beatitude.
An unworthy act which is deprecated by a
devoted wife can never be done by me,
who was born in a noble house and have
joined a pious family by virtue of marriage.”
Having spoken thus to Ravana, who made
his foes cry in terror, and turning her back
on him, the illustrious Sita, a princess of the
Videha territory, once more addressed the
following words to him : “Being the virtuous
wife of another, | cannot be your lawful
consort. (4-6)
