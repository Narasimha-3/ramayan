“Even if you flyaway for protection to
the mountain, Kailasa, constituting the abode
of Kubera (the god of riches) or descend on
the council-chamber of King Varuna (the
deity presiding over water), you shall
undoubtedly be deprived of your life by the
arrows of Sri Rama (son of Daégaratha),
killed as you already are by the Time-Spirit
even as a huge tree felled by lightning.” (34)
