लंकाया वचरनं श्रुत्वा हनुमान्‌ मारुतात्मज:।
यत्रवान्‌ स हरिश्रेष्ठ: स्थित: शैल इवापर:॥ ३१॥
Hearing the words of Lanka, that jewel
among the monkeys, Hanuman, sprung from
the loins of the wind-god, stood like another
mountain (standing on the Trikuta mountain),
full of agility to give her a sound thrashing if
she dared to assail him. (31)
