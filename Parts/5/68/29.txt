
ततो मया वाग्भिरदीनभाषिणी
शिवाभिरिष्टाभिरभिप्रसादिता |
sare शान्ति मम मैथिलात्मजा
तवातिशोकेन तथातिपीडिता॥ २९॥

“Cheered by me with gladdening and
agreeable words, Sita, a princess of Mithila,
who, though sore afflicted with the thought
of your excessive grief on her account and
who never speaks pitiful words, derived
solace in my presence.” (29)
