
अन्‍्या वक्षसि चान्यस्यास्तस्या: काचित्‌ पुनर्भुजम्‌।
अपरा त्वड्डूमन्यस्थास्तस्याश्चाप्यपरा Pati go ॥
A certain woman lay with her head
resting an another's bosom, while another
lay using the latter’s arm as a pillow. Yet
another for her part lay converting the lap of
another into a pillow, while a third lay turning
the latter's breasts into a pillow. (60)
