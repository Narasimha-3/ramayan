अयं ह्युत्सहते ga: कर्तुमेकार्णव॑ जगत्‌।
इति चिन्तां बहुविधामकरोन्मतिमान्‌ कपि:।
दृष्ट्वा wae प्रभावममितौजस: ॥ २०॥
‘If enraged, he can really turn the world
into one ocean.’ Perceiving the glory of Ravana
(the king of ogres), who was endowed with

extraordinary might, the sagacious Hanuman
brooded on such diverse thoughts. (20)
