इतीव तदगृहमभिगम्य शोभनं
सविस्मयो नगमिव चारुकन्दरम्‌।
पुनएच तत्परमसुगन्धि सुन्दरं
हिमात्यये नगमिव चारुकन्दरम्‌॥ १५॥

Hanuman was thus filled with wonder on

चित्राशच
visiting that splendid and beautiful aerial car,
resembling a mountain, with lovely caverns
and further looking like a tree with charming
hollows and supremely fragrant during the
spring-time (lit., the close of winter). (15)
