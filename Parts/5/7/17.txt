
ततस्तदा बहुविधभावितात्मनः

कृतात्मनो जनकसुतां सुवर्त्मन: |
अपश्यतो5भवदतिदुःखितं मनः

सचक्षुष: प्रविचरतो महात्मन:॥ १७॥

The mind of the high-souled Hanuman,
who had contemplated on the Supreme Spirit
in many ways and disciplined his self, nay,
who always followed the right path and who
kept his eyes open, thereupon became sore
distressed when he did not perceive Sita on
that occasion, though ranging wide. (17)
