Reaching that honoured city, protected
by the arms of Ravana, the ten-headed
monster, and not finding anywhere, even

though moving about freely, that daughter of
King Janaka, highly respected and sore

afflicted and conquered by the force of her
consort’s virtues, Hanuman presently felt
very miserable. (16)
