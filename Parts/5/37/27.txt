
कथयन्तीव शशिना संगमिष्यसि रोहिणी।

मत्पृष्ठमधिरोह wt तराकाशं महार्णवम्‌॥ २७॥

“You will be united with Sri Rama as
Rohini with the moon-god, the very moment,
as it were, you say that you wish to be so
united. Pray, climb up my back and reach
beyond the vast sea through space the
very next moment, as it were. (27)
