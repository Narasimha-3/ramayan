
मैथिली तु हरिश्रेष्ठाच्छुत्ता वचनमद्भुतम्‌।
हर्षविस्मितसर्वाड़ी. हनूमन्तमथाब्रवीत्‌॥ ३०॥
हनूमन्‌ दूरमध्वानं कथ्थ मां नेतुमिच्छसि।
तदेव खलु ते wa कपित्व॑ हरियूथप॥ ३१॥

Thrilled all over with joy to hear the
aforesaid wonderful proposal from Hanuman,
the foremost of monkeys, Sita, a princess
of Mithila, for her part forthwith replied as
follows to Hanuman : “How do you intend to
take me, O Hanuman, to such a long distance
from this place? Indeed, | look upon the
aforesaid proposal of yours to be precisely
monkey-like, O leader of monkey hordes !

(30-31)
