
यावन्न राज्ये were: पितृपैतामहे स्थितः।
अभिषिक्तो जलक्लिन्नो न मे शान्तिर्भविष्यति॥ १०॥

“There will be no peace of mind to me
until Sri Rama, who deserves the kingship,
is installed on the ancestral throne, duly
consecrated and wet with water after a
ceremonial bath forming part of the
consecration. (10)
