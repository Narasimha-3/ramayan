एवं ते भाषमाणस्य पद्मा श्रीरुपतिष्ठताम्‌।
aed ज्येष्ठे नृपसुते पृथिवीं दातुमिच्छसि॥ १५॥

“Let the Goddess of Fortune, known
by the name of Padma (so-called because
She has Her abode in a bed of lotuses),
ever be with you, who are speaking in this
strain and seek to restore the sovereignty
of the globe to the eldest prince, Sri Rama !”

(15)
