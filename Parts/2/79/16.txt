अनुत्तमं॑ तद्बचचन॑ नृपात्मज:
प्रभाषितं संश्रवणे निशम्य च।
प्रहर्षजास्तं प्रति बाष्पबिन्दवो
निपेतुरार्यानननेत्रसम्भवा:. ॥ १६॥

The prince, Bharata, rejoiced to hear

the excellent benediction poured by the
counsellors into his ears. Nay, teardrops
born of excessive delight and gratitude
towards them flowed from the eyes of
Bharata, whose countenance too was lit up
with joy. (16)
