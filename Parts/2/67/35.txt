
यमो वैश्रवण: शक्रो वरुणश्र महाबल:।
विशिष्यन्ते नरेन्द्रेण वृत्तेन महता ततः॥ ३५॥

“Even Yama, the god of retribution,
Kubera (son of 519५8, the god of riches),
Indra (the ruler of gods) and the very mighty
Varuna (the deity presiding over water) are
outstripped by a king of excellent conduct
by virtue of such conduct inasmuch as he
combines in himself the virtues of all the
above-named deities. (35)
