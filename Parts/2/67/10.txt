
नाराजके जनपदे बीजसमुष्टि: प्रकीर्यते।

नाराजके faq: gat भार्या वा वर्तते वशे॥ gol
“In a rulerless land handfuls of seeds

are no longer scattered for fear of uncertainty

of crops. Nay, in a rulerless land a son is
not amenable to the control of his father nor
is a wife amenable to the control of her
husband, there being no executive authority
to enforce correct conduct. (10)
