इति संचिन्त्य तद्‌ राजा नाध्यगच्छत्‌ तदासुखम्‌।
प्रतिलभ्य aa: संज्ञां कैकेयीवाक्यतापित:॥ ३॥
Reflecting thus, the king could not
make out on the spur of the moment what it
was; on the other hand, he experienced
great agony causing him to swoon. Then,
regaining consciousness, he felt tormented
once more by the memory of Kaikeyi’s
words. (3)
