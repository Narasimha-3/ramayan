
त्रयोदशो5यं दिवसः पितुर्वृत्तस्य ते विभो।
सावशेषास्थिनिचये किमिह a विलम्बसे॥ २२॥

“Today is the thirteenth day since your
father was cremated, my lord! Why then do
you lag behind now in winding up the
remaining part of the ceremony of collecting
the bones, viz., cleaning and washing the
ground on which the king’s body had been
cremated and consigning the ashes to the
river? (22)
