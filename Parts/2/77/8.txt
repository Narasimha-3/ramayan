दृष्ट्वा भस्मारुणं तच्च दग्धास्थि स्थानमण्डलम्‌।
faq: शरीरनिर्वाणं Prev fare ह॥८॥

Seeing that circular spot, the place
where his father’s remains had been

consigned to fire, nay, which was now
covered with ashes and red because of its
having been burnt with a blazing fire and
was further strewn with burnt bones, Bharata,
they say, grew melancholy and began to
cry. (8)
