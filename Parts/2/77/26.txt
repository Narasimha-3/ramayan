
अश्रूणि परिमृद्गन्ता रक्ताक्षो दीनभाषिणों।
अमात्यास्त्वरयन्ति सम तनयौ चापरा: क्रिया:॥ २६॥

The ministers now urged the two
princes—who were wiping their tears and
speaking in a piteous tone, their eyes
reddened through excessive grief—to
expedite the other rites pertaining to the
thirteenth day, viz., lifting the ashes and
immersing them into the river and so on.(26)
