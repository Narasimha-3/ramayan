नैतच्चित्र नरव्याप्रे शीलवृत्तविदां at
यदार्य त्वयि तिष्ठेत्तु निम्नोत्सष्टमिवोदकम्‌॥ १६॥
“It is no wonder that, even as water
discharged from above collects into a
depression, noble conduct for its part should
find its place in you, a tiger among men and
the foremost of those who know how to
behave well and conduct themselves with
propriety. (16)
