
TIT भूय अयोध्यां deat ह।
अयोध्यां तु तदा दृष्ट्वा पित्रा भ्रात्रा विवर्जितामू॥ २३॥
भरतो दुःखसंतप्त: सारथिं चेदमब्रवीत्‌।
सारथे पश्य विध्वस्ता अयोध्या न प्रकाशते॥ २४॥
निराकारा निरानन्दा दीना प्रतिहतस्वना॥ २५॥

Proceeding from Srngaverapura he
vividly saw Ayodhya once more: so the
tradition goes. Seeing Ayodhya deserted by
his father and elder brother, Sri Rama,
Bharata for his part felt sore stricken with
agony at that moment and spoke as follows
to the charioteer Sumantra: “Look, O
charioteer ! Desolate and shorn of its grace,
bereft of joy, wretched and lifeless, Ayodhya
does not look bright as before.”

(23—25)
