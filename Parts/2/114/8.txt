त्यक्तां यज्ञायुथे: सर्वेरभिरूपैश्व याजकै:।
सुत्याकाले सुनिर्वत्ते वेदिं गतरवामिव॥ ८॥

It resembled an altar which, when the
period of offering oblations into the sacred
fire has completely expired, has been cleared
of all sacrificial implements and deserted by
the learned priests, and where all sound
has consequently ceased. (8)
