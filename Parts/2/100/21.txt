
कच्चिन्न तर्कैर्युक्त्या वा ये चाप्यपरिकीर्तिता: ।
त्वया वा तब वामात्यर्बुध्यते तात मन्त्रितम्‌॥ २१॥

“| hope your deliberations, which have
not been proclaimed are not known to
others through surmises or reasoning and
the deliberations of others are known (before
they materialize) by yourself or by your
ministers. (21)
