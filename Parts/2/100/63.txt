
कच्चिदर्थ च art च धर्म a जयतां वर।
विभज्य काले कालज्ञ सर्वान्‌ ate सेवसे॥ ६३॥

“Do you pursue all the three objects of
human pursuit, viz., wealth and the delights
of sense and religion only during the hours
allotted to each,* O bestower of boons,
dividing them according to time, O jewel
among the victorious, knowing as you do
the time appropriate for each? (63)
