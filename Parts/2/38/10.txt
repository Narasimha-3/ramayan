
ननु पर्याप्मेव॑ ते पापे रामविवासनम्‌।
किमेभिः कृपणैर्भूयः पातकैरपि ते कृतैः॥ १०॥

“Indeed, sending Rama into exile in the
robes of a hermit, as you are doing, O sinful
woman, is enough for you. What more do
you seek to gain through these further sins
in the shape of exiling Sita and that too in
the robes of a hermitess which are going
to be perpetrated by you and which are
calculated to land you in untold suffering?

(10)
