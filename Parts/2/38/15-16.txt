
मया विहीनां वरद प्रपन्नां शोकसागरम्‌।
अदृष्टपूर्वव्यसनां ya: . सम्मन्तुमहसि॥ १५॥
पुत्रशोक॑ यथा नर्च्छेत्‌ त्वया पूज्येन पूजिता।
मां हि संचिन्तयन्ती सा त्वयि जीवेतू तपस्विनी ॥ १६॥

“When she is bereft of me and
consequently drowned in a sea of grief,
although she has known no suffering before,
O bestower of boons, you ought to show
greater regard to her, so that the poor lady
may not fall a prey to grief caused by
separation from me (her son) and treated
with respect by you, who are worthy of
adoration to her, and contemplating on me,
may survive under your care. (15-16)
