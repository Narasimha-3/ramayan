
कुरु नो याचनां पुत्र सीता तिष्ठतु भामिनी।
धर्मनित्य: स्वयं tard न हीदानीं त्वमिच्छसि॥ १९॥

“Grant our solicitation, 0 darling! Let
Sita, the pretty young lady, continue to stay
in Ayodhya though surely you are not
personally inclined to stay any longer,
righteousness being your eternal companion.”
