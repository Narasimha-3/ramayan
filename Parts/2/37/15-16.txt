राम॑ er तु सीताया aaat चीरमुत्तमम्‌।
अन्तःपुरचरा नार्यो मुमुचुर्वारि नेत्रजम्‌॥ १५॥
Bag wae राम॑ ज्वलिततेजसम्‌।
ae नैवं raed बनवासे मनस्विनी॥१६॥

Perceiving Sri Rama fastening the
excellent bark about Sita, the female inmates
of the gynaeceum began to shed tears and,

sore distressed as they were, spoke as
follows to Sri Rama of dazzling glory : “This
high-minded princess has not been so
commanded to reside in the forest (as you),
dear child ! (15-16)
