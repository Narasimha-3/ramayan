Reaching that lovely mountain,
abounding in flocks of birds of many kinds
and having a rich stock of roots and fruits
and having sweet water in abundance, Sri
Rama for his part said to Laksmana, “Soul-
captivating is this delightful mountain adorned
with many trees and creepers and abounds
in roots and fruits. To me it appears to be a
place where sustenance could be found
with ease, O gentle brother ! (13-14)
