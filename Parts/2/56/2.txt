
सौमित्रे श्रुणु वन्यानां वल्गु व्याहरतां स्वनम्‌।
सम्प्रतिष्ठामहे काल: प्रस्थानस्थ परंतप॥ २॥
“Hear, 0 Laksmana (son of Sumitra),

the notes of sweetly warbling wild birds, the
parrot, the cuckoo, myna and so on. Let us
duly resume our journey as the hour for
departure has commenced, O tormentor of
enemies ! (2)
