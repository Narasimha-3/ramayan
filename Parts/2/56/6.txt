आदीप्तानिव वैदेहि ada: पुष्पितान्‌ नगान्‌।
we: पुष्पे: किंशुकान्‌ पश्य मालिन: शिशिरात्यये॥ ६॥

“See, 0 Sita, a princess of the Videha
clan, the Kirnsuka (Butea frondosa) trees
laden with blossoms on all sides and
appearing as though illumined and adorned
with garlands, as it were, due to the rows of
their flowers in this vernal season
synchronizing with the close of winter. (6)
