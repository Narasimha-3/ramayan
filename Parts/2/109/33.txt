निन्दाम्यहं कर्म कृतं पितुस्तद्‌

यस्त्वामगृह्माद्‌ विषमस्थबुद्धिम्‌।
बुद्धयानयैवंविधया चरन्तं
सुनास्तिकं धर्मपथादपेतम्‌॥ ३३॥

“| denounce the action mentioned below,
of my father, who picked up as his counsellor-
priest you, a staunch unbeliever, who has

not only strayed away from the path of
righteousness but whose mind is set on a
wrong path opposed to the Vedic path, nay,
who is moving about in the world with such
an ideology conforming to the doctrine of the
atheistic Carvaka, who believes only in the
world of senses, as has been set forth in
your foregoing speech. (33)
