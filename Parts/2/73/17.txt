
अथवा मे भवेच्छक्तियोंगिर्बुद्धिबलेन वा।
सकामां न करिष्यामि त्वामहं पुत्रगर्द्धिनीम्‌॥ १७॥

“Or, even if such capacity could be
brought out in me through contrivances or
through intellectual calibre, | shall never
allow you to attain your desired end—you,
who covet sovereignty for your son, i.e.,
myself. (17)
