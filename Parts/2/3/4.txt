चैत्र: श्रीमानयं मास: पुण्यः पुष्पितकानन:।
यौवराज्याय. रामस्थ सर्वमेवोपकल्प्यताम्‌॥ ४॥
“The present is the splendid and sacred
month of Caitra (roughly corresponding to
March of the Gregorian calendar), when the
woodlands are adorned with blossoms (as
a mark of rejoicing). Let everything indeed
be got ready for the installation of Sri Rama
in the office of Prince Regent.” (4)
