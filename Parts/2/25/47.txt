निपीड् मातुश्चरणौ पुनः पुनः |

जगाम सीतानिलयं महायशाः
स राघव: प्रज्वलितस्तया fsa i ४७॥

Circumambulated clockwise by the said
pious lady and pressing as a token of
reverence his mother’s feet again and again,
that highly illustrious scion of Raghu,
irradiated as he was by the splendour
occasioned by the mother’s benedictory rites,
proceeded to Sita’s apartments. (47)
