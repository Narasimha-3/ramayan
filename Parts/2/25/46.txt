ata चाश्र॒ुप्रतिपूर्णलोचना

समाप्य च स्वस्त्ययनं यथाविधि।
प्रदक्षिणं चापि चकार राघवं
पुनः पुनश्चापि निरीक्ष्य सस्वजे॥ ४६॥
Nay, having finished the sacred rite of
invoking blessings on 51 Rama with due
ceremony, Kausalya whose eyes were
overflowing with tears, further went clockwise
round Sri Rama (a scion of Raghu) and,
gazing on him again and again, embraced
him. (46)
