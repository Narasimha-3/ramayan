
प्रजासुखत्वे चन्द्रस्य वसुधाया: क्षमागुणै:।
Teen बृहस्पतेस्तुल्यो al साक्षाच्छचीपते: ॥ ३०॥

“He is a compeer of the moon in
affording delight to all created beings, vies
with the earth in his forbearance and other
allied virtues, is a replica of the sage
Brhaspati, the preceptor of gods, in wisdom
and is a direct image of Indra (the spouse of
580) in valour. (30)
