ज्वरातुरों नाग FA AMAT: tl २७॥

While Laksmana, son of DaSaratha, a
friend of the people, was speaking the truth
as above out of affection for his elder brother,
Sri Rama, Guha, who was sore pinched
with adversity and oppressed with agony,
shed tears like an elephant tormented with
fever. (27)
