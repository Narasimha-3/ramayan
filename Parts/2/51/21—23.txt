“lf, on the other hand, Dasaratha
survives, people will move about happily in
the capital of my father, provided with cross
roads, allocated at lovely sites, and well-
aligned roads, nay, rich in mansions of well-
to-do men, temples of gods and royal palaces,
adorned with the foremost of courtesans,
crowded with chariots, horses and elephants,
made resonant with the sound of musical
instruments, full of all blessings and crowded

with merry and well-fed men, well-provided
with gardens and royal parks and bright
with festivities carried on under the auspices
of associations. (21—23)
