मया विसृष्टां भरतो महीमिमां
सशैलखण्डां सपुरोपकाननाम्‌।
शिवासु सीमास्वनुशास्तु केवलं
त्वया यदुक्त नृपते तथास्तु ATU ५६॥
“Let Bharata rule over this globe,

including the mountain ranges and cities
with their gardens, relinquished by me. He
should neither enjoy the sovereignty nor
feel proud of it but keep within the bounds
of righteousness salutary for a king. Let the
pledge which has been given by you to
Kaikeyl come to be true, O protector of
men ! (56)
