अनुजानीहि सर्वान्‌ नः शोकमुत्सूज्य मानद।
लक्ष्मणं मां च सीतां च प्रजापतिरिवात्मजानू॥ २४॥

“Giving up grief, 0 bestower of honour
on all, grant leave to us all, viz., Laksmana,
Sita and myself, even as Brahma, the lord
of created beings, did to his sons, Sanaka
and his three brothers, who were intent on
practising austerities in the forest.” (24)
