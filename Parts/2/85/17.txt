अन्तदहिन दहनः संतापयति राघवम्‌।

वनदाहाग्रिसंतप्तं गूढो5ग्रिरिव पादपम्‌॥ १७॥

Even as a fire hidden in the hollow of
a tree burns a tree already scorched by a
forest-consuming fire, so did the fire of anxiety
for Sri Rama inwardly begin to consume
Bharata (a scion of Raghu), who was already
scorched by the fire of grief caused by his
father’s death. (17)
