स्ती्त्वा महात्मा वरदो महानदीम्‌।
ततः समृद्धान्‌ शुभसस्यमालिनः
क्रमेण वत्सान्‌ मुदितानुपागमत्‌॥ १०१॥
Having crossed the great river Ganga,

the high-souled Sri Rama, who vied in glory
with the guardians of the spheres and was
capable of bestowing boons, thence reached
step by step the prosperous and happy
land of Vatsa, the land comprised between
the Ganga and the Yamuna rivers, which
contained rows of beautiful crops. (101)
