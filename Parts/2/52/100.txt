मुमोच बाष्पं व्यथितस्तपस्वी ॥ १००॥

Constantly gazing on Sri Rama, who
had soon reached the other bank of the
Ganga, and his vision having failed due to
the long distance covered by the exiles in
the meantime, Sumantra, who was full of
remorse and afflicted too, shed tears of
grief over his separation from Sri Rama.
