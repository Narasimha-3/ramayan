
राघवो5पि महातेजा नावमारुहा at ततः।
ब्रह्मवत्क्षत्रवच्चैच. जजाप हितमात्मनः ॥ ७८॥

Having got into the aforesaid boat,
Sri Rama, a scion of Raghu, for his part,
who was possessed of unique splendour,
then recited a sacred text (edt नावम्‌ etc.,)
fit for the Brahmanas and Ksatriyas
alike and conducive to his own good.
