
निवेश्य गड्ढामनु at महानदीं

ay विधाने: परिबरहशोभिनीम्‌।
sara WA तदा महात्मनो

विचिन्तमानो भरतो निवर्तनम्‌॥ २६॥

Having caused that army, which looked
charming with its equipage in the shape of
tents etc., to encamp methodically along the
bank of the great river Ganga, Bharata too
halted then, reflecting all the time how to
bring the high-souled Sri Rama back to
Ayodhya. (26)
