
Wart तु महारण्यं चिररात्राय राघवे।

बभूव नगरे मूर्च्छा बलमूर्च्छा जनस्य च॥ १८॥
Sri Rama, a scion of Raghu, having

departed for the great forest of Dandaka for

a long term, unconsciousness, however,
prevailed among the people in the city; there
was unconsciousness in the army including
even horses and elephants as well as among
the people visiting Ayodhya from the districts.
(18)
