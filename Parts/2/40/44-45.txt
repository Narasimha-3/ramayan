
तथा रुदन्तीं कौसल्यां रथं तमनुधावतीम्‌।
क्रोशन्तीं राम रामेति हा सीते लक्ष्मणेति च॥ ४४॥
रामलक्ष्मणसीतार्थ स्त्रवन्तीं वारि नेत्रजम्‌।
असकृतू tert स तां नृत्यन्तीमिव मातरम्‌॥ ४५॥

Sri Rama repeatedly gazed on his
aforesaid mother, Kausalya, who was
weeping as above and following that chariot
as though dancing, crying “Rama, O Rama,
O Sita, 0 Laksmana!” and shedding tears
for the sake of Sri Rama, Laksmana and
Sita. (44-45)
