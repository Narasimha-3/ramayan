स पादुके ते भरतः स्वलंकृते
महोज्ज्वले सम्परिगृह्य धर्मवित्‌।
प्रदक्षिणं da wan wad
चकार  चैवोत्तमनागमूर्थनि॥ २९॥
Receiving with reverence the aforesaid

pair of exceedingly bright-ornate wooden
sandals, the celebrated Bharata, who knew
what is right, went clockwise round Sti
Rama, a scion of Raghu, and further placed
the pair of sandals on the head of an excellent
elephant. (29)
