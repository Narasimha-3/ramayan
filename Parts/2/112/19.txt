कामाद्‌ वा तात लोभाद्‌ वा मात्रा तुभ्यमिदं Haq
aware कर्तव्यं वर्तितव्यं च मातृवत्‌॥ १९॥

“No matter whether all this was wrought
by your mother in your interest from affection
for you or from greed of sovereignty through
your installation as Prince-Regent, my
darling, it should not be taken to heart by
you and you should behave towards her as
towards a mother.” (19)
