नाश्रेयसि रतो ag न विरुद्धकथारुचि:।
उत्तरोत्तरयुक्तीनां वक्ता वाच्स्पतिर्यथा॥ १७॥
He never took delight in an act which

was not conducive to blessedness, had no
relish for profane talks and, like the sage
Brhaspati (lit, a master of eloquence),
advanced arguments and counter-arguments
in support of his contention. (17)
