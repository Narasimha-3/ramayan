उपशान्तवणिक्पण्या नष्टहर्षा निराश्रया।
अयोध्या नगरी चासीननष्टतारमिवाम्बरम्‌॥ ३५॥

The city of Ayodhya—in which the
business of the trading class had been
brought to a standstill and whose joy had
gone, nay, which had now become
supportless, looked dark as the sky in which
the stars had disappeared. (35)
