
न चाहष्यन्‌ न चामोदन्‌ वणिजो न प्रसारयन्‌।
न चाशोभन्त पण्यानि नापचन्‌ गृहमेधिनः॥ ४॥
People neither made merry nor did
they rejoice. Merchants did not spread their
merchandise, nor did the saleable goods
(spread by them) look charming. Nor, again,
did the householders cook their food at their
home. (4)
