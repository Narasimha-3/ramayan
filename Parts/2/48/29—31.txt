That lotus-eyed great chariot-warrior,
Sri Rama, elder brother of Laksmana—a
tiger among men and a subduer of foes—
who is dark-brown of complexion and has a
countenance resembling the full moon, nay
whose collar-bone is invisible (because of
its being covered with flesh), whose arms
extend up to the knees, who takes the
initiative in speaking, is sweet of expression,
truthful of speech and possessed of
extraordinary might, is easy of address to
all people and is delightful of aspect as the
moon, and whose gait resembles that of an
elephant in rut, will surely adorn the woods
while roaming through them.” (29—31)
