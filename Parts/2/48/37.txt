
प्रशान्तगीतोत्सवनृत्यवादना
विश्रष्टहर्षा पिहितापणोदया।
तदा ह्ायोध्या नगरी बभूव सा
महार्णव: संक्षपितोदको यथा॥ ३७॥

The celebrated city of Ayodhya—in
which all singing, rejoicing, dancing and
instrumental music had been completely set
at rest, whose delight had fled forever and
the steady rise of whose trade had been
hampered—looked at that time like an ocean
whose waters had dried up. (37)
