नष्टज्वलनसंतापा प्रशान्ताध्यायसत्कथा।
तिमिरेणानुलिप्तेव॒ तदा सा नगरी बभौ॥ ३४॥

The city of Ayodhya in which the
kindling of fires even for the purposes of
Agnihotra had ceased and the chanting of
the Vedas and the narration of sacred stories
relating to the Puranas died out, looked as
though coated with darkness at that time.
