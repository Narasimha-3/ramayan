
तदा ferat रामनिमित्तमातुरा

यथा सुते भ्रातरि वा विवासिते।
fara दीना रुरुदुर्विचेतसः

सुतर्हि तासामधिको5पि सो5भवत्‌॥ ३६॥

Giving expression to their grief in
various ways on that occasion, the women
of Ayodhya, who were sick of mind on
account of Sri Rama, as one would feel on
one’s own son or brother having been
sent into exile, cried miserably and fell
unconscious; for Sri Rama was dearer to
them than their very sons. (36)
