
अभिषिक्ते तु काकुस्थे प्रहष्टमुदिति जने।
प्रीतिमम arta भवेद्‌ राज्याच्चतुर्गुणम्‌॥ २१॥
“After Sri Rama, descendant of the

Kakutstha lineage, is coronated on the throne
and the subjects are blissfully happy, it will
give me fourfold more fame and happiness
than that from having obtained the kingdom.”
(21)
