
शत्रुघ्नोत्तिष्ठ कि शेषे निषादाधिपतिं गुहम्‌।

शीघ्रमानय ows ते तारयिष्यति वाहिनीम्‌॥२॥
“How do you continue to sleep till

now? Get up, O Satrughna, and fetch Guha,

the ruler of the Nisadas, quickly; so that
he will convey the army to the other bank.

intent to see the latter

May all be well with you.”

जागर्मि नाहं स्वपिमि तथेवार्य विचिन्तयन्‌।
इत्येवमब्रवीद्‌ भ्राता weet विप्रचोदितः॥३॥

“Contemplating on Sri Rama, my eldest
brother, even as you are, | am keeping
awake, not sleeping.” In these very words
did Satrughna, Bharata’s younger brother,
reply when admonished as above by Bharata.
