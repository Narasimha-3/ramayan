सा farpe भरतेन सेना
ad पुरस्कृत्य विधूय दर्पम्‌।
रघुनन्दनस्थ
विरोचते नीतिमता प्रणीता॥ ३१॥

Brought with the purpose of propitiating
Sri Rama, the delight of the Raghus, by
Bharata, who was rich in the sense of
propriety, placing righteousness in the
forefront and shaking off vanity, the aforesaid
army shone brightly in the vicinity of
Citrakuta. (31)
