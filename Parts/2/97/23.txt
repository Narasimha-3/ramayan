इमां चाप्येष वैदेहीमत्यन्तसुखसेविनीम्‌।
पिता मे राघवः श्रीमान्‌ वनादादाय यास्यति॥ २३॥

“Again, my aforesaid father, the glorious
DaSaratha, a scion of Raghu, will return to
the capital taking with him from the forest
this Sita (a princess of the Videha kingdom)
too, who has always enjoyed the utmost
amenities of life. (23)
