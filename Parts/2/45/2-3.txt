aya गुणसम्पन्न: पूर्णचन्द्र इब प्रिय: ॥ ३॥
Even when the king was made to return
much against his will, governed as he was
by the code of conduct prescribed for friends

and relations (accompanying a departing
friend to some distance only), they would

not return on any account and continued to
follow the chariot; for Sri Rama, who enjoyed
great celebrity and was richly endowed with
excellences, had become the favourite like
the full moon of the people residing in
Ayodhya. (2-3)
