On that occasion the city of Ayodhya—
which was full of noise and vied in its
splendour with Amaravati (the capital of
Indra) and which was crowded on all sides
with the people of the countryside, that had
collected there in their eagerness to behold
the capital—ooked like the sea-water infested
with aquatic creatures peculiar to the ocean.
