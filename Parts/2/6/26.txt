ते तु दिग्भ्यः पुरीं प्राप्ता द्रष्टु रामाभिषेचनम्‌।
रामस्थ पूरयामासु: पुरी जानपदा GAT: 1 २६॥

Having poured into the city of Ayodhya
from all directions in order to witness the
installation of Sri Rama as Prince Regent,
the people from the countryside had flooded
the prospective capital of Sri Rama. (26)
