
तान्‌ पार्थिवान्‌ वारणयूथपार्हान्‌

समागतांस्तत्र. महत्यरण्ये।
वनौकसस्तेडभिसमीक्ष्य. सर्वे

waa प्रविहाय हर्षम्‌॥ ४२॥

Perceiving the aforesaid princes, who
deserved to ride on lordly elephants (lit., the
leaders of herds of elephants), come together
in that forest, all those dwellers in forests
for their part began to shed tears totally
giving up joy born of his blessed sight.(42)
