Sita, a princess of the Videha territory,
in her turn, who had been observing austere
vows, had not so far heard all that had
happened in the meantime, causing
interruption in the installation of 51 Rama
and making for his exile into the forest, so
that the aforesaid installation alone of Sri
Rama in the office of Prince Regent stood
foremost in her heart. (3)
