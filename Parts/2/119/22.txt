इतीरितः प्राज्ललिभिस्तपस्विभि-
fea: कृतस्वस्त्ययनः परंतपः।
at सभार्य: प्रविवेश wea:
सलक्ष्मण: सूर्य इवाभ्रमण्डलम्‌॥ २२॥

Spoken to as above by the ascetics
with folded hands as a_ gesture of
supplication, Sri Rama, a scion of Raghu,
the scorcher of his foes, when he had been
presented by the Brahmanas with flowers
etc., accompanied by good wishes and
benedictions, made his way into the heart of
the forest alongwith his consort and
Laksmana, as the sun would penetrate a
mass of clouds. (22)
