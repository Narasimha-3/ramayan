जाने चैतन्मनःस्थं ते दृढीकरणमस्त्विति।
sas त्वां तवात्यर्थ कीर्ति समभिवर्धयन्‌॥ २१॥
“| already knew (by my yogic power)
what existed in your mind. All the same |
questioned you as though | knew nothing

in order that your resolution may

be strengthened and in order further to

enchance your renown beyond measure.
