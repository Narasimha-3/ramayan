
Me Tafa तं देशं बसाद्य सह मन्त्रिभि:।
एतं मे कुरु सुप्राज्ञ कामं कामार्थकोविद॥ २३॥

“You will certainly proceed to that area
tomorrow. Halt here with your counsellors
today. Grant this desire of mine, O highly
sagacious prince, knowing how to fulfil the
desired object of your friends and well-
wishers ! (23)
