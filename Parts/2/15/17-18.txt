पूज्या राज्ञो भवन्तश्न WaT तु विशेषतः।
aa पृच्छामि वचनात्‌ सुखमायुष्मतामहम्‌॥ १७॥
Wa: सम्प्रतिबुद्धस्य चानागमनकारणम्‌।
इत्युक्त्वान्त:पुरद्वारमाजगाम पुराणवित्‌॥ १८॥

“You are, however, worthy of adoration
even to the king, much more so to Sri
Rama. | shall, therefore, presently inquire
after His Majesty’s welfare on behalf of your
blessed selves as also the reason of his
failure to appear before you even though he
is fully awake.” Saying so, Sumantra, who
knew many ancient anecdotes, being many
winters old and belonging as he did to the
bard class, returned to the entrance of the
gynaeceum. (17-18)
