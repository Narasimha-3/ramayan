
अनसूयेति या लोके कर्मभिः ख्यातिमागता।
तां शीघ्रमभिगच्छ त्वमभिगम्यां तपस्विनीम्‌॥ १६॥

“Speedily seek you respectfully the
ascetic lady, who is worth approaching with
reverence and who has attained celebrity in
the world through her actions under the
name of Anasuya (lit., free from jealousy).”

(16)
