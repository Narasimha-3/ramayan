नातो विशिष्ट पश्यामि बान्धवं विमृशन्त्यहम्‌।
सर्वत्र ard वैदेहि तपःकृतमिवाव्ययम्‌॥ २५॥

“Though deeply pondering, | do not
see for a woman a friend greater than the
husband and more capable of yielding one’s
desired object at all places like the
imperishable fruit of one’s austerities, O
princess of the Videha kingdom ! (25)
