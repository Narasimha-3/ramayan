सीता त्वेतद्‌ बच: श्रुत्वा राघवस्य यशस्विनी।
तामत्रिपत्नीं धर्मज्ञामभिचक्राम मैथिली॥ १७॥
शिथिलां afeat वृद्धां जरापाण्ड्रमूर्थजाम्‌।
सततं वेपमानाड़ीं प्रवाते कदलीमिव॥ १८॥

Hearing this exhortation of Sri Rama
(a scion of Raghu), the illustrious Sita, a
princess of Mithila, for her part proceeded
to meet the celebrated and aged consort of
Atri, who knew what is right, nay, who was
feeble of body and wrinkled, with her hair
turned grey due to old age, and whose
frame constantly shook like a banana tree
in a storm. (17-18)
