सकतलत्रस्य संदेहो नित्यं युक्तस्थ राघव।
समर्थस्यापि हि सतो वासो दुःखमिहाद्य ते॥ २२॥
“Your continuance too at this place, 0
scion of Raghu, with your consort, although
you are ever alert and even though you are
powerful enough to put him down, is attended
with peril and conducive to misery now.”(22)
