
ततस्तत्र समासीनौ नातिदूरे निरीक्ष्य ताम्‌।
न्यग्रोधे सुकृतां शब्यां भेजाते धर्मवत्सलौ॥ ३३॥

Perceiving a bed decently prepared by
Laksmana at the foot of the banyan tree not
very far from the place occupied by them,
while they were comfortably seated there,
Sri Rama and Sita, who were tenderly alive
to their duty, then sought the bed. (33)
