मुहूर्तमपि जीवाबवो जलान्मत्स्याविवोद्धृतौ॥ ३१॥
“Bereft of you, 0 scion of Raghu,

neither Sita nor | myself can survive
even for a shortwhile any more than

fish taken out of water.

“Today | wish to see neither father nor
Satrughna (my younger brother) nor Sumitra
(my own mother) nor even heaven without
you, O tormentor of foes !” (32)
