
ततस्तु तस्मिन्‌ विजने महाबलौ
महावने राघववंशवर्धनौ।
न तौ भयं सम्भ्रममभ्युपेयतु-
dia fae गिरिसानुगोचरौ॥ ३५॥

Thenceforward the two exceptionally
mighty princes, Sri Rama and Laksmana, the
promoters of the race of Raghu, never
experienced fear or agitation while dwelling
in that great lonely forest any more than a
couple of lions living on a mountain-peak. (35)
