अधर्मभयभीतश्च॒ परलोकस्थय चानघ।
तेन लक्ष्मण नाद्याहमात्मानमभिषेचये॥ २६॥
“| am afraid of sin that will be incurred
by me if | fail to help my father in implementing
the boons conferred by him on Kaikeyi as
well as of ruining my prospects in the other
world, O sinless Laksmana ! Hence | do not
install myself in the office of Prince Regent
(by force) today.” (26)
