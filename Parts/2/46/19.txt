अस्मदव्यपेक्षान्‌ सौमित्रे निर्व्यपेक्षान्‌ गृहेष्वपि।
वृक्षमूलेषु संसक्तान्‌ पश्य लक्ष्मण साम्प्रतम्‌॥ १९॥
“Behold, 0 Laksmana, the citizens, full
of great longing for us and absolutely
unmindful of their homes as also of their

near and dear ones, lying close to the roots

of trees till this late hour, O son of Sumitra !
(19)
