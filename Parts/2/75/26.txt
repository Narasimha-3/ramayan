
संश्रुत्य a तपस्विभ्य: सत्रे वे यज्ञदक्षिणाम्‌।
at चापलपतां ud यस्यार्योउनुमते गतः॥ २६॥

“Let the sin attaching to those
withholding payment of the sacrificial fee
even after promising it to the priests
practising austerities and officiating at a
sacrifice be incurred by him with whose
concurrence my elder brother has gone into
exile. (26)
