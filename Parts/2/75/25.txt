बलिषड्भागमुद्धृत्य Tema: प्रजा:।
अधर्मो ASST SSCA यस्यार्यो STATA गत: ॥ २५॥

“Let the sin that attaches to a king who

fails to protect the people even after collecting
the land revenue in the form of one-sixth of

the produce be incurred by him with whose
concurrence my elder brother has gone into
exile. (25)
