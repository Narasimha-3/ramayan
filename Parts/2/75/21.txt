
कृतशास्त्रानुगा बुद्धिर्मा भूत्‌ तस्य कदाचन।
सत्यसंध: सतां श्रेष्ठो यस्यायोडनुमते Wa: २१॥

“Let his judgment never follow (the
teachings of) the scriptures as taught by his
preceptor, with whose concurrence my elder
brother, who is true to his promise and is
the foremost of the virtuous, has gone into
exile. (21)
