
प्रैष्य॑ पापीयसां यातु सूर्य a प्रति मेहतु।
Bq पादेन गाः सुप्ता यस्यार्योडनुमते Ta: २२॥

“Let him with whose concurrence my
elder brother has gone into exile suffer the
lot of a servant of men belonging to the
lowest stratum of society, incur the sin of
answering the calls of nature facing the sun
and reap the consequences of kicking cows
lying asleep. (22)
