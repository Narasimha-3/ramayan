
निशम्य तल्‍्लक्ष्मणमातृवाक्यं

रामस्थ मातुर्नरदेवपल्या:।
we: शरीरे विननाश शोकः

शरदगतो AT इवाल्पतोय:॥ ३१॥

On hearing that speech of Sumitra,
Laksmana’s mother, the grief that had
emaciated the body of Queen Kausalya, the
mother of Sri Rama, quickly disappeared
like an autumnal cloud containing meagre
water. (31)
