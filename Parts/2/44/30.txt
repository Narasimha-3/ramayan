आश्वासयन्ती विविधैश्व वाक्यै-

बॉक्योपचारे कुशलानवद्या।
रामस्थ ता मातरमेवमुक्त्वा

देवी सुमित्रा विरराम रामा॥३०॥

Having spoken to Sri Rama’s celebrated
mother as aforesaid, the charming and
faultless Queen Sumitra, who was a
pastmaster in eloquence and was busy
consoling her through various modes of
expression, became silent. (30)
