इदं तु SU यदनर्थकानि मे
ब्रतानि दानानि च संयमाश्च हि।
तपश्च de यदपत्यकाम्यया
सुनिष्फल॑ बीजमिवोप्तमूषरे॥ ५२॥
“This is all the more painful that my
sacred observances and gifts as well as
courses of self-discipline are of no use and
the asceticism practised with intent to do
good to my child has proved utterly
unfructuous like a seed sown in barren soil.
(52)
