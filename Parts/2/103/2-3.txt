Hearing the aforesaid unpleasant and
heart-rending news broken by Bharata, which
was piercing like a thunderbolt discharged
by Indra, the enemy of demons, on a field of
battle, and raising his arms, Sri Rama, the
scorcher of his enemies, for his part actually
sank to the ground precisely like a tree with
boughs full of blossom felled down with an
axe in a forest: so it is said. (2-3)
