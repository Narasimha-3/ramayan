सर्वेषु स हि धर्मात्मा वर्णानां कुरुते दयाम्‌।
चतुर्णा हि वयःस्थानां तेन ते तमनुव्रता:॥ १५॥
Since that pious-minded prince would
show compassion, commensurate, of course,
with their age, to all people belonging to the
four grades of society and even outside
their pale, they were accordingly devoted to
him in thought, word and deed. (15)
