Repeatedly addressing the aforesaid
charioteer in the words “O charioteer,” the

glorious Rama, the foremost of men, whose
voice resembled the cackling of a swan in
rut, said, “When, returning back to Ayodhya
and united once more with my parents,
shall | roam hunting in the woodland bordering
on the Sarayu and laden with blossoms?
