
राजर्षणां हि लोकेउस्मिन्‌ रत्यर्थ मृगया वने।
काले Sat at मनुजैर्धन्विनामभिकांक्षितामू्‌॥ १७॥

“Truly speaking, hunting in a forest
was resorted to in this world for the
gratification of royal sages. Even though it
was adopted at times by the sons of Manu
and was sought after by other bowmen, | do
not long for it excessively.” (17)
