ब्राह्यणो ह्ोकपाश्वेन नरान्‌ रोद्धुमिहाहति।

न तु मूर्धाभिषिक्तानां विधिः प्रत्युपवेशने॥ १७॥

“A Brahmana alone ought to restrain
people oppressing him by lying on one side
at their door. Ksatriyas, however, who are
or deserve to be sprinkled with holy water
on the head (when being installed as the
head of a state) are not enjoined to sit at the
door of anyone in this way. (17)
