
वृतो राजा हि कैकेय्या मया तद्बचचनं कृतम्‌।
अनृतान्मोचयानेन पितरं त॑ महीपतिम्‌॥ ३२॥

“As the king was solicited for a boon
by Kaikeyi, his pledge given to her has
been implemented by me by being in exile
in the forest. Now, acquit the said king, our
father, of the charge of falsehood by ruling
over Ayodhya and thereby discharging your
part of the obligation.” (32)
