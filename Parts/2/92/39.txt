सा प्रयाता महासेना गजवाजिसमाकुला।
दक्षिणां दिशमावृत्य महामेघ इवोत्थितः ॥ ३८ ॥
Full of elephants and horses, that

huge army on its march looked like a
vast cloud burst upon the view, enveloping

the southern quarter.

वनानि च व्यतिक्रम्य जुष्टानि मृगपश्षिभिः।
Term: परवेलायां गिरिष्वथ नदीष्वपि॥ ३९॥

Traversing woodlands inhabited by deer
and birds and hemming mountains as well
as rivers beyond the western bank of the
Ganga flowing in a southerly direction, the
army moved along. (39)
