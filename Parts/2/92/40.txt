रराज सेना wae तत्र॥ ४०॥

Striking the hosts of deer and birds
inhabiting that region with terror, while
penetrating through that extensive forest in
the precincts of Citrakuta, the said army of
Bharata, consisting as it did of overjoyed
elephants and horses,looked charming on
that spot. (40)
