ततस्तां रजनीं व्युष्य भरतः सपरिच्छद:।

Having spent the previous night with
his family at the hermitage of Bharadwaja,

कृतातिथ्यो भरद्वाजं कामादभिजगाम ह॥ II Bharata, to whom hospitality had been
extended by the sage, approached
Bharadwaja to take leave of him to proceed
to Citrakita: so the tradition goes. (1)
