शत्रुघ्नविक्षेपविमूढसंज्ञां

समीक्ष्य कुब्जां भरतस्य माता।
समाश्चासयदार्तरूपां
क्रौद्धीं विलग्रामिव वीक्षमाणाम्‌॥ २६॥
Perceiving the humpback stunned
because of her having been dragged to and
fro by Satrughna, and wearing a wretched
look and looking on her boss like a female

crane released from captivity, Bharata’s
mother slowly calmed her. (26)
