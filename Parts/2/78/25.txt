
सा पादमूले कैकेय्या wert निपपात ह।
निःश्वसन्ती सुदुःखार्ता कृपणं विललाप ह॥ २५॥

The notorious Manthara, it is said, fell
at the soles of Kaikeyl’s feet. (Nay) drawing
a deep audible breath expressive of
sadness, sore stricken as she was with
sorrow, she piteously wailed: so the tradition
goes. (25)
