
अनृतं aa लोको5यमज्ञानाद्‌ यदि वक्ष्यति।
तेजो नास्ति ot wa तपतीव दिवाकरे॥ ४॥

“It would be a matter for pity to me if in
the event of your not taking me with you the
people of Ayodhya utter through ignorance
the falsehood that ‘Supreme valour is lacking
in Sri Rama, even though he blazes like the
sun.’ (4)
