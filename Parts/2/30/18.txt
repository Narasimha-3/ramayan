यस्त्वया सह स स्वर्गो निरयो यस्त्वया विना।
इति जाननू परां प्रीतिं गच्छ राम मया सह॥ १८॥
“An abode which will be shared by me
with you will be pleasing as heaven to me;
while that which will be shorn of you will be
repellent as hell. Knowing thus my supreme
love for you, proceed to the forest with me,
O Rama! (18)
