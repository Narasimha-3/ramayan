मन्युर्न च॒ त्वया कार्यो देवि ब्रूमि तवाग्रतः।
यास्यामि भव सुप्रीता ad चीरजटाधर:॥ ४॥

“Nor should displeasure be shown by

you to me, O queen, because | am putting
this question to you. Wearing the bark of
trees and matted locks, | will certainly
proceed to the forest. Be fully pleased with
me. (4)
