Perceiving such wonderful firmness in
Sri Rama, a scion of Raghu, the people
of Ayodhya experienced joy and felt
disconsolate at the same time. They were
distressed to find that he was not going to
Ayodhya and felt rejoiced to note his firmness
of resolve. (34)
