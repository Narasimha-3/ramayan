The priests, the citizens and the leaders
of different bodies of men as well as the
mothers of Sri Rama and others, who had all
been rendered senseless, as it were, and
had tear-drops in their eyes, complimented
Bharata, who was speaking in that strain,
and bowing down low to Sri Rama, joined
Bharata in his supplication to Sri Rama. (35)
