
सुप्तायास्‍्ते गमिष्यन्ति नव वर्षाणि पञ्ञ च।
समग्रमिह ward मां द्रक्ष्यस सुहृदवृतम्‌॥ ३५॥

“Nine years and five will slip past you
even while you are asleep. One fine morning
you will find me duly arrived back here in
Ayodhya in my entire being alongwith Sita
and Laksmana, surrounded by my friends
and relations.” (35)
