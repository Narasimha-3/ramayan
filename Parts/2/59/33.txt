“It is most unfortunate that | am unable
to clasp on Rama, a scion of Raghu,
alongwith Laksmana on this spot today,

even though | long to see them.” Lamenting
thus, that highly — illustrious monarch
immediately dropped senseless, as it were,
on the couch. (33)
