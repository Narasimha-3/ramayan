संहष्टस्त्वहमपि दण्डकानु प्रवेक्ष्ये॥ १७॥

“Be you, 0 Bharata, the ruler of the
people themselves. | too shall be the emperor
of wild beasts. Return you, extremely
delighted, to Ayodhya, the foremost of cities,
this very day and | too shall enter the
Dandaka forest highly rejoiced. (17)
