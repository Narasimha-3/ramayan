शत्रुघ्नस्त्वतुलमतिस्तु ते wera:
सौमित्रिर्मम fated: प्रधानमित्रम्‌।
चत्वारस्तनयवरा ad नरेन्द्र
सत्यस्थं भरत चराम मा विषीद॥ १९॥

“Let Satrughna of peerless wisdom for
his part be your assistant and the well-
known Laksmana, son of Sumitra, be my
chief friend. Let us, his four worthy sons,
enable the king to adhere to truth. Do not
feel dejected.” (19)
