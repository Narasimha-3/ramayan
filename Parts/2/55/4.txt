Tigra:  संधिमासाद्य.. मनुजर्षभौ।
कालिन्दीमनुगच्छेतां नदीं पश्चान्मुखाश्रिताम्‌॥ ४॥

“Reaching the confluence of the Ganga
and the Yamuna, the two jewels among
men, should follow the river Yamuna, which
has taken a westerly course (for some
distance due to the great rush of the waters
of the Ganga). (4)
