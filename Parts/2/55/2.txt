तेषां स्वस्त्ययनं da महर्षि: स चकार ह।
प्रस्थितान्‌ ter aise पिता पुत्रानिवौरसानू॥ २॥

Nay, perceiving the three travellers
about to start on their journey, that great
seer too, so the tradition goes, performed a
religious rite ensuring their safe journey
(scattering boiled rice on the ground and
invoking blessings through the repetition of
certain sacred texts) in the same way as a
father would bless his own sons (and
daughter-in-law). (2)
