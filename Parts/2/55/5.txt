अथासाद्य तु कालिन्दीं प्रतिस्त्रोत:समागताम्‌।
तस्यास्तीर्थ प्रचरितं wart wea राघव।
wa यूयं wid कृत्वा तरतांशुमतीं नदीम्‌॥५॥

“Then, approaching the Yamuna turned
in an opposite direction, and carefully
perceiving in it a passage (across the river)

too much frequented, O scion of Raghu,
cross you the river Yamuna (the deity
presiding over which is the daughter of the
sun-god) after preparing a raft. (5)
