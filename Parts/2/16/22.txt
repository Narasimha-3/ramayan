“(Having once installed you in the office
of Prince Regent), the king ought to
consecrate you in course of time, as Brahma,
the maker of the universe, consecrated Indra,
for the Rajasuya sacrifice (as a preliminary
to which all the kings on the globe shall
have to be conquered and reduced to
submission), as his kingdom is inhabited by
learned Brahmanas well-versed in_ ritual
acts. (22)
