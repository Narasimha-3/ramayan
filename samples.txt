Query: 
Who proposed the marriage of Sita and Rama?
Answer: 
King Janaka offered Sita's hand in marriage to Rama.  While the context mentions the sages Viswamitra and Vasistha proposing marriages for Bharata and Shatrughna,  it clearly states that Janaka himself presented Sita to Rama.


Query: 
Why was ahalya cursed and how did she break free of it?
Answer: 
Ahalya was cursed because Indra, disguised as her husband Gautama, tricked her.  
Although Ahalya recognized Indra, she was deceived.  
When Gautama returned and discovered the deception, he cursed both Indra and Ahalya.  
Ahalya's curse was to remain unseen, subsisting only on air, covered in ashes, and repenting for many years.  
The curse was broken when Sri Rama visited the hermitage, restoring Ahalya to her original form and ending her penance.


Query: 
Why was ahalya cursed and how did she break free of it?
Answer: 
The previous answer correctly states the general story of Ahalya's curse and its lifting by Rama.  
However, it lacks some nuance.  While Ahalya did recognize Indra disguised as Gautama, texts differ on the degree of her culpability, 
ranging from willing participation to reluctant submission.  The curse itself involved Ahalya becoming invisible to most, subsisting on air, 
covered in ashes, and enduring intense repentance.  Her release came when Rama, on his way to Mithila with Viswamitra, stepped on the stone she had become.  This act, not simply a visit to the hermitage, restored Ahalya to her original form.  It's important to note that the additional context provided, regarding Viswamitra, Indra, and Rambha, doesn't offer any further details about Ahalya's story.


Query: 
What curse was placed on Indra?
Answer: 
Indra was cursed with the loss of his virility (manhood) by Sage Gautama.


Query: 
How did Indra handle his curse?
Answer: 
Indra, cursed with the loss of his virility, sought the help of other gods.  They appealed to the celestial manes, offering a ram as a substitute. The manes agreed, transferring the ram's attributes to Indra, restoring him.

Query: 
Explain Trisanku?
Answer: 
Trisanku was a king of the Ikshvaku dynasty who desired to ascend to heaven in his physical body.  
He initially sought the help of his family priest, Sage Vasistha, for a special sacrifice to achieve this, but was refused.  
Undeterred, he then approached Vasistha's sons, but they too rejected his request and, angered by his persistence,
 cursed him to become a Candala (an outcast). This curse dramatically altered Trisanku's appearance, causing his skin to darken, 
 his clothing to change, and his followers to abandon him. In his despair, Trisanku sought the help of another sage, Viswamitra, 
 who was moved by his plight and agreed to help him.
